# Updated Job Status Reporting for Delayed Jobs

## Updated Job Status Reporting for Delayed Jobs
## What Is New?
 The Medidata system's import job processor terminates jobs that take longer than six hours to process a job's first record. Prior to this update, Ingestor's job status screen labelled jobs terminated in this way as "Data Processing Failed".
 However with this update, Ingestor now presents jobs terminated in this way with the status "Processing completed, but there is a data delay".
 Feature/enhancement stories referenced in this article:

## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 All.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 This change brings more clarity to the status of your import jobs, especially edge cases.
## Where Do I See the Change?
 You can see the update on a job's Job Files tab. The new status appears under that screen's Status column.
