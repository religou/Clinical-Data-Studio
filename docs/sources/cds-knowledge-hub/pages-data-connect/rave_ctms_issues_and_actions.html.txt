# CTMS Issues and Actions

## CTMS Issues and Actions
 You can export CTMS Issues and Actions data objects detailing issues , actions, and comments data. The data include protocol deviation s and their associated action items for subject s, sites , and studies as reported in Medidata CTMS . 
 Note : All data are for cumulative and incremental extracts.
 Important : Your Rave URL configuration can affect your ability to access exported data. For details, please contact your Medidata representative or Medidata Support.
