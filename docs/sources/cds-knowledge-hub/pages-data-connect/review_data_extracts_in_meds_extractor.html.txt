# Review Data Extracts in MEDS Extractor

## Review Data Extracts in MEDS Extractor
 Note : This feature appeared as part of MEDS Extractor 2019.1.0.
## Description
 MEDS Extractor allows you to review the progress of extracts, and to download completed extracts to a local device.  You can review the current status of all data extracts  for all studies across environments.
## Affected Roles
 All.
## Advantages
 Reviewing data extracts allows you to:
- Review the success of exported data so you can take further action. 
- Download the exported data to your local drives for further processing. 
## User Impact
 In MEDS Extractor, you can select an existing extract to review its progress. While reviewing the progress of an existing extract, you can:
- Review extracts across other studies and environments. 
- Download exported data to your local drives whilst it is available for download. 
## Special Considerations
- Extracts are available for download to your local device up to 24 hours after completion.
