# Search Studies

## Search Studies
## Description
 MEDS Extractor can now search the study list for matching study names.
## Affected Roles
 All roles with access to MEDS Extractor. 
## Advantages
 You can now quickly search for a required study without navigating the whole study list.
## User Impact
 You can reduce the study list to studies of interest  by entering text  into the study search box, and clicking the search icon. 
## Special Considerations
 None.
