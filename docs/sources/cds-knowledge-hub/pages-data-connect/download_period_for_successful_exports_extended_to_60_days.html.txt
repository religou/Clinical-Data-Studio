# Download Period for Successful Exports Extended to 60 Days

## Download Period for Successful Exports Extended to 60 Days
## Description
 You can download extracted files from an export job up to 60 days after the most recent successful export.
## Affected Roles
 All.
## Advantages
 Previously, you had up to 24 hours after a successful export job to download the extracted files. While SFTP export has no limitation, the additional time gives you flexibility for when you can download your files, especially when an export job completes over a weekend or holiday.
## User Impact
 You can download extracted files up to 60 days after an export job successfully completes.
