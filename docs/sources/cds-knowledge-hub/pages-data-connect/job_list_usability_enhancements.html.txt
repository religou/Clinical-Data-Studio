# Job List Usability Enhancements

## Job List Usability Enhancements
## Description
 Updates to the job list page include  various usability enhancements. You can :
- Refresh the status display of export jobs without having to navigate through the hierarchy of studies,  
- Drill down on the export job name to access the Job Configuration Details and Job History pages. 
- Column headers, including any open filters, remain, frozen, on screen. 
- You can scroll the job list and the column headers, including any open filters, remain on screen.  
## Affected Roles
 All roles with the Manage Extracts permission. 
## Advantages
 The job list enhancements provide the following benefits:
- You can refresh the export job status without needing to navigate through the hierarchy of studies, saving navigation time and clicks. 
- The drill-down functionality allows you to review your export job configurations and history with more detail to review failed jobs.  
- The improved sorting and freezing for column headers provides improved readability, especially when there are multiple jobs displayed for a study environment. 
## User Impact
 On MEDS Extractor's job list page you can:
- Click a job name to access the job configuration details and job history pages. 
- Click   Refresh Job   List   to  track the progress of your current job. 
 Additionally, when you scroll through the job list, column headers and open filters remain on screen. 
## Special Considerations
 None.
