# System Error for Active Studies with Name Edits

## System Error for Active Studies with Name Edits
 Editing a study name in Cloud Administration after the study becomes active causes system errors in Data Connect . 
## Symptoms
 Downstream services in Data Surveillance and other Clinical Data Studio capabilities experience errors or fail to process data . 
## Causes
 You edited the study name after the study reached an active state . 
 Important : Medidata does not support editing study names after you create study artifacts in Data Connect, such as projects, templates, imports, exports, custom code, or datasets. 
## Diagnostic
 Review the study status to verify if it is active. Review the study history to verify if you edited the study name after creating study artifacts. 
## Resolution
- Please contact Medidata Support.
 Important : Do not edit study names after a study becomes active.
