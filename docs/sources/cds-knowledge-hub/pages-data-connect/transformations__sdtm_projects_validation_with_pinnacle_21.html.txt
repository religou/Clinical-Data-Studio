# Transformations: SDTM Projects Validation with Pinnacle 21

## Transformations: SDTM Projects Validation with Pinnacle 21
## What Is New?
 Users can now validate SDTM projects with Pinnacle 21 Enterprise. Users can make live edits to the SDTM dataset based on Pinnacle21 validation without losing context. 
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 All users with Pinnacle 21 enterprise license.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 Most of the industry has Pinnacle 21 Enterprise to validate their SDTM dataset for agency submission. Users can now identify the discrepancies between SDTM datasets created in Clinical Data Studio and agency submission requirements. 
## Where Do I See the Change?
 Users can access these functionalities from Data Connect's Transformation tab for SDTM Projects.
