# CTMS Documents, Submissions, Enrollment, Milestones, and Service Providers

## CTMS Documents, Submissions, Enrollment, Milestones, and Service Providers
 You can export study management data objects detailing study enrollment metrics and study milestone  dates.  
 Note : Milestones and Study Enrollment Status data are for cumulative and incremental extracts. Study Enrollment Metrics data are only available for cumulative extracts.
 Important : Your Rave URL configuration can affect your ability to access exported data. For details, please contact your Medidata representative or Medidata Support.
