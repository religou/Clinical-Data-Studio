# Manage Dataset Collections

## Manage Dataset Collections
 The Dataset Collections provide a way to organize and group datasets.
## Add Collection
- Access Data Connect , and  click   Datasets   in the left-hand menu. 
- On the Datasets page, click  Manage Collections  on the right-hand side. 
- Click + Collection. 
- Enter the Collection Name.  
- Click Save . 
 Note : You cannot delete the collections that have assigned datasets. Remove the datasets from the collection and then click the Trash icon to delete a collection.  
##  Add Dataset to Collections
- Access Data Connect , and  click   Datasets   in the left-hand menu. 
- Click the More icon for a dataset > Add to Collections . 
- Select one or more collections from the list. 
- Click Save . 
 Note : To modify the assigned collection for a dataset:
- Click the More icon for a dataset > Modify Collection .
