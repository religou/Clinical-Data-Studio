# Datasets: Data Profiling and Comparision

## Datasets: Data Profiling and Comparision
## What Is New?
 Users can:
- Visually verify the respective data source has everything they need before they create a custom data product, and then verify the custom data product that they created. 
- Compare both data profiles on single dataset with different filters. 
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 All.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the Workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 Users can confirm the datasets and verify the data they need to apply complex transformations amidst vast data.
## Where Do I See the Change?
 To access the data profiling feature, go to the Profiling tab on the Datasets screen.
