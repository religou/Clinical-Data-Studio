# Manage Datasets

## Manage Datasets
 While in the Datasets capability, you can see a list of accessible datasets within your study and their details. 
## Supported Dataset Types
- Raw Rave datasets 
- Raw external datasets 
- Derived datasets 
- Unit converted datasets 
- SDTM-like datasets* 
- Custom data models* 
 Important : Projects denoted with an asterisk are in limited preview for select customers. 
## Access Datasets
 The datasets list is accessible from the landing screen: 
- Access Data Connect , and click  Datasets in the left-hand menu .
