# Improved Job List

## Improved Job List
## Description
 MEDS Extractor enhances the job list with detailed information on extraction job progress, such as displaying the next job run date and time in your local timezone. MEDS Extractor also removes duplicate job information. 
## Affected Roles
 All.
## Advantages
 The job list page displays more detail for scheduled   jobs, providing a streamlined presentation.  
## User Impact
 When reviewing scheduled jobs, t he job list page displays: 
- Current job statuses as icons. 
- Future job run details with dates displayed in your local timezone. 
## Special Considerations
 None.
