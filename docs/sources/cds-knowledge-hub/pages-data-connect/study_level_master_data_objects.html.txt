# Study Level Master Data Objects

## Study Level Master Data Objects
 You can export Study Level Master Data data objects detailing team membership metrics, role assignment data, and more. These data objects differ from Master Data objects extracted at the Client Division level. For a list of client division level Master Data objects, see  Client Division Level Master Data Objects . 
 Important : Your Rave URL configuration can affect your ability to access exported data. For details, please contact your Medidata representative or Medidata Support.
