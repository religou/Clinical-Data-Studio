# RBQM: Ensure Study Data Accuracy Using Data Quality Plans

## RBQM: Ensure Study Data Accuracy Using Data Quality Plans
## What Is New?
 A Data Quality Plan (DQP) in a study is a comprehensive document that outlines the strategies, procedures, and measures to ensure the accuracy, completeness, reliability, and integrity of the data collected during the trial. The plan is essential for verifying that the data supports robust conclusions and complies with regulatory standards. Clinical Data Studio now includes a capability where you can build integrated data quality plans for your study. The RBQM tab in Clinical Data Studio includes a Plan Document capability where you can build data quality plans that include risk mitigations for your study.
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Data managers with the Manage Data Quality Plans building block can publish, whereas data managers with View Data Quality Plans permissions can only view existing plans.
## Workflow Process
 Data managers add mitigations and associate functional plans in the Risk Review section in Risk Management to track risks for better study data accuracy. Data managers then use various components available under the Plan Documents section to build integrated data quality plans which act as a guide for risk mitigation in the study. 
## Other Applications
 When adding and managing mitigations, you can associate mitigations with functional plans. When writing integrated data quality plans in the RBQM > Plan Documents capability, if you add a risk assessment table to a plan document, the risks table includes the Functional Plans Affected column, which auto-populates based on the entries made in the Mitigations and Plans > Functional Plans Affected field for each mitigation added to risks in the Add Risk and Risk Review subpages.
## How Do I Benefit?
 Data Quality Plans:
- Streamlines operational planning by enabling the quick creation and management of comprehensive plans, allowing for faster execution. 
- Transforms risk data into actionable, dynamic plans that actively guide team member actions. 
- Provides enhanced transparency into actions and mitigations linked to each plan, ensuring alignment, accountability, and improved compliance across teams. This turns data quality management into a proactive, integrated process.

## What Else Should I Know?
- You need the View Data Quality Plans and Manage Data Quality Plans building block added to your Cloud Administration role to view and publish plan documents, respectively. 
- Non latin characters display in the editor and PDF. 
- The downloaded PDF includes no headers or footers, or images. The PDF includes some data duplication around page breaks.
