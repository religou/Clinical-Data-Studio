# PDS: Patient Profiles Quality of Life Improvements for April 2024

## PDS: Patient Profiles Quality of Life Improvements for April 2024
## What Is New?
 On the Patient Data Surveillance (PDS) > Patient Profiles > Design page > Profiles tab > Patient Selector, you can now:
- Select the number of rows you want the Patient Selector to show on the page. You can show 25, 50, or 100 rows at a time.
- The default rows per page are 25 rows. 
- Paginate through the Patient Selector table. 
- Click Patient Selector column headers to sort the Patient Selector table by column vertically in ascending or descending alphanumeric order. 
 In addition, performance when filtering the Patient Selector by table column is improved.
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 All Patient Profiles users. 
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process. 
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications. 
## How Do I Benefit?
 In previous Patient Profiles releases, the Patient Selector listed all patient data for all patient profiles on a single page. Paginating the Patient Selector reduces the amount of data that the Design page consumes, which significantly improves page performance. 
 Filtering and sorting the Patient Selector table helps you scan the table to find specific patient profiles based on specific data.

## What Else Should I Know?
- If you access a patient profile directly from an AI Data Reconciliation report, the Rounding & Propagation > Patients with Recorded Events window, the Event Incidence plot, the Data Anomaly Detect plot > Summary table > Patient column (when viewing the Summary table by Patient), then  the paginated data shows when you clicks the Patient Selector upon landing on the patient details page. The pagination shows the total number of pages, current page number, and the default rows per page is set to 25. 
- You must enter a minimum of three characters to initiate the Patient Selector column filters. 
- You can only filter up to 10 Patient Selector columns at a time. 
- You cannot sort the Patient Selector table by the Unreviewed Patient Status column. 
- If you want to navigate to page 10 or higher, we recommend using the pagination's next arrow, > , instead of entering a specific page number
