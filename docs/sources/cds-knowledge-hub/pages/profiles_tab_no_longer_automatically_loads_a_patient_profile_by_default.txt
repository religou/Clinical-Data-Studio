# Profiles Tab No Longer Automatically Loads a Patient Profile By Default

## Profiles Tab No Longer Automatically Loads a Patient Profile By Default
## What Is New?
 In previous Patient Profiles releases, when you initially accessed the Patient Profiles Design page > Profiles tab, then the patient profile of the Patient Selector's top-listed patient ID would show on the page by default. 
 Now the Profiles tab no longer shows a patient profile on the page by default. 
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 All Patient Profiles users.
## Workflow Process
 When you access the Patient Profiles Design page > Profiles tab, you must select a patient ID from the Patient Selector for a patient profile to appear on the page. 
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 Not having a patient profile automatically selected on the Profiles tab by default ensures that subsequently viewed or exported profiles show the correct information.
## Where Do I See the Change?
 On the Patient Profiles Design page > Profiles tab.
