# Data Surveillance: Clean Patient Tracker Metrics and Table Improvements

## Data Surveillance: Clean Patient Tracker Metrics and Table Improvements
## What Is New?
 Clean Patient Tracker includes the following improvements:
- The high-level outstanding data cleaning metrics at the top of the Clean Patient Tracker page show in a larger format. 
- The Patient in Progress card shows the percentage of enrolled patients that are reviewed and currently have no outstanding data clean tasks. 
- The Sites to Review card shows the percentage of sites in the study that are reviewed and currently have no outstanding data clean tasks. 
- The Outstanding Tasks cards shows the percentage of all data cleaning tasks across the entire study that are  in progress or unreviewed . 
- You can sort the Patients table by:
- Patient ID 
- Site 
- Country 
- In Progress Patients 
- Enrolled Data 
- Missing Pages 
- You can sort the In Progress Patients By Site - Detailed View > Sites table by:
- Site 
- Country 
- In Progress Patients 
- Missing Pages 
- Open Queries 
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Data managers.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
- The high-level outstanding data cleaning metrics at the top of the Clean Patient Tracker page are easier to read. 
- Showing the percentage of patients in progress, percentage of sites to review, and percentage of outstanding tasks:
- Informs you of the volume of data cleaning work across the study that is complete and still needs to be completed. 
- Help you monitor your current data cleaning progress across the study to ensure that tasks are being completed an a healthy, expected pace. 
- Sorting the Patients table and In Progress Patients By Site - Detailed View > Sites table helps you better find, manage, and analyze the data cleaning metrics you need. 
## Where Do I See the Change?
 In RBQM > Clean Patient Tracker:
- The metrics cards at the top of the Clean Patient Tracker page show in larger text. 
- The Patients in Progress card now shows % Reviewed . 
- The Sites to Review card now shows % Reviewed . 
- The Outstanding Tasks card now shows % Outstanding . 
- In the Patients table, you can click the Patient ID , Site , Country , In Progress Patients , Enrolled Data , Missing Pages column headers to sort the table by header. 
- In the In Progress Patients By Site - Detailed View > Sites table, you can click the  Site , Country , In Progress Patients ,  Missing Pages, Open Queries column headers to sort the table by header.
