## Change Details

 Change Type

 Summary of Changes 
 | 
 As highlighted in the Release Newsletter :
 Critical Data, Critical Processes, and Categorization now integrate with Audit Viewer . With this integration, you have direct access to view audit reports for these functionalities. 
 Benefit to Users 
 | 
 Audit Reports allow you to maintain a consistent audit trail to track actions and updates made on the  Critical Data, Critical Processes, and Categorization pages. 
 Summary of User Impact 
 | 
 Impact to Users and Roles 
- Risk assessment administrators and risk managers. 
- Users with permissions to view and manage Critical Data, Critical Processes, and Categorization. 
 Impact to Workflow Processes 
 You can access audit reports for Critical Data, Critical Processes, and Categorization directly from their individual pages to view all changes made to date.   
 Impact to Applications 
 No impact to other applications.
 Impact on Interface 
 Audit Reports are available for Critical Data, Critical Processes, and Categorization within Risk Assessment.

 User Impact Assessment 
 The impact of the change on system behavior and on the user's interaction with it.
- Low – There is no change to current software behavior.
- Moderate – Change includes corrections or improvements that may change user interaction with the software, such as minor UI or minor functionality changes.
- High – Change includes software changes which require review of existing settings, workflow and/or operational procedures to ensure correct user interaction with the software.
