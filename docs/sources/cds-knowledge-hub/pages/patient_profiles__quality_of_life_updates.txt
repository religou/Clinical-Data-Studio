# Patient Profiles: Quality of Life Updates

## Patient Profiles: Quality of Life Updates
## What Is New?
 Patient Profiles includes the following quality of life enhancements:
- Designs now include the name of the last user who refreshed the profile. 
- The color palette of the colors used for your highlighting and your section coloring has been updated. 
- The Download Profile button in the Patient Selector is bundled in the More menu. 
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 All.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
- Displaying the name of the last users who refreshed the profile enables you attribute who produced the current profile view that you are looking at. 
- Updating the highlighting and section color palette better matches the Web accessibility guidelines for color distances and for readability. 
- Moving the Download Profile button to the More menu gives the Patient Selector a cleaner look and feel. 
## Where Do I See the Change?
- The name of the last user who refreshed the profile shows at the top of the page next to the snapshot date. 
- The updates color palette shows when adding element highlight conditions, applying section colors when adding elements to the Design Layout, and when view a patient profile report. 
- The Download Profile button is located in the More menu in the Patient Selector header.
