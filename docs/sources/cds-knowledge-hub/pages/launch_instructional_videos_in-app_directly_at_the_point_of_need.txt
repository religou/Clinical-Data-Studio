# Launch Instructional Videos in-App Directly at the Point of Need

## Launch Instructional Videos in-App Directly at the Point of Need
## What Is New?
 The Medidata Detect user interface currently provides progressive user assistance (PUA) in select locations indicated by a clickable icon. 
 Numerous Medidata Detect PUA access points are now enhanced by providing interactive instructional videos that you can watch and control directly within the in-app guide. A clickable icon indicates a PUA guide that also contains an instructional video.  Videos provide instruction on how to perform specific tasks related to the corresponding page. 
 You can:
- Play and pause the video 
- Skip to a direct point in the video 
- Adjust the volume 
- Expand the video to full screen 
- Adjust the playback speed 
- Pop the video out to view and reposition alongside the page you are working in
 Tip : Click the three-dotted Actions button in the lower right-hand corner of the video player to adjust the playback speed or pop the video out in a separate window.
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 All.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 Providing in-app instructional videos directly within the Detect user interface: 
- Ensures that you find the instructional videos you need faster and easier than searching through online help. 
- Provides the precise instructional videos you need at the exact location within the Detect user interface where you need assistance. 
- Empowers self-reliant learners. 
- Offers an alternative, more engaging learning mechanism for whose who prefer visual learning. 
- Complements text-based instructions for a more well-rounded learning experience. 
 In general, most learners prefer video-based instruction over text-based instruction.  
## Where Do I See the Change?
 As of the Medidata Detect November 2023, you can find PUA access points containing instructional videos in the following Detect locations:
- Patient Data Surveillance > Data Reviewer > Data Sets page header 
- Patient Data Surveillance > Data Reviewer > Data Listings page header 
- Patient Data Surveillance > Patient Profiles > Patient Profile Designs page header 
- Quality Tolerance Limits > QTL Dashboard > QTL Dashboard page header 
- Centralized Statistical Monitoring > Reporting Patterns > Event Incidence page header 
- Centralized Statistical Monitoring > Exploratory Analysis > Data Anomaly Detection page header 
 You can expect Medidata Detect to release new or updated PUA with instructional videos to coincide with or follow future product releases.
