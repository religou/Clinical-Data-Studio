# Risk Review Detailed Page Improvements

## Risk Review Detailed Page Improvements
## What Is New?
 The variable Visible at Site has been removed, and replaced with Include in Risk Plan Report?. When you select 'No' for Include in Risk Plan Report? that risk no longer shows in the Risk Plan Report. 
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Data manager.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 You now have the ability to filter out risks that you do not need to include in the Risk Plan Report. 
## Where Do I See the Change?
 Detect Landing Page > Risk Management > Risk Inventory  > click on a Risk ID
