# Data Surveillance and RBQM: KRI, QTL, And Derived Variables Configuration Updates Enhance Usability

## Data Surveillance and RBQM: KRI, QTL, And Derived Variables Configuration Updates Enhance Usability
## What Is New?
 On the Data Surveillance or RBQM > Configuration > Derived Variables page, if you click preview while creating a derived variable, then the Preview button appears disabled once you save. 
 On the RBQM > Configuration >  KRI or QTL pages, the Copy to Another Study button no longer appears on the respective pages.
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Data Managers.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 With a cleaner user interface, you can perform study data quality management activities with greater efficiency.
