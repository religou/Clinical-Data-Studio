# Risk Assessment: Updated Risk Management Provides A Better User Experience

## Risk Assessment: Updated Risk Management Provides A Better User Experience
## What Is New?
 Risk Management includes the following updates that ensure a clearer user interface and an enhanced user experience.
- Risk name appears as the first field when you add a risk or perform a risk review on any of the pages: RBQM > Risk Assessment  > Details or Critical Processes or Categorization. 
- You can select derived datasets and external data points when you add critical data to your study on the RBQM > Risk Assessment > Critical Data page. 
- You can download an audit report from the RBQM > Risk Inventory and Risk Review pages by clicking  next to the page title, or the top-right corner of the page, respectively. 
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Data managers.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 Improved navigation and labeling on the Risk Management pages provide a more consistent user experience with the capability. Data managers can better track all actions related to a particular risk so evidence of adherence can be provided for audits.
