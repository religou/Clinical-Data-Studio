# Data Surveillance: Improved Profile Designs Enables Identifying Reviewed Patients

## Data Surveillance: Improved Profile Designs Enables Identifying Reviewed Patients
## What Is New?
 The Profile tab for profile designs in Patient Profiles includes the following updates: 
- The Unreviewed Patient Status column renamed to Review Status. 
- Profile Updated renamed to Last Rave Entry. 
- Last Reviewed Date renamed to Reviewed On. 
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 All Patient Profile users.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process. 
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications. 
## How Do I Benefit?
 The updated profile designs enable you to differentiate easily between reviewed and unreviewed patients.
## Where Do I See the Change?
 In the Data Surveillance > Patient Profiles > once you select a published or draft design > Profiles tab, the following columns are updated:
- Unreviewed Patient Status renamed to Review Status. 
- Profile Updated renamed to Last Rave Entry. 
- Last Reviewed Date renamed to Reviewed On.
