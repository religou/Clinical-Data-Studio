## Change Details
 Product 
 | Clinical Data Studio - Signals
 Version 
 | 2026.1.0
 Change Type 
 | Enhancement (Updated Functionality)
 Functionality or Module 
 | Signals
 Summary of Changes 
 | 
 As highlighted in the Release Newsletter : 
 You can now download a single or multiple signals as PDF files. You can also select specific signals and choose to download only those selected. 
 Benefit to Users 
 | Central Monitors, Central Statistical Monitors, and Clinical Research Associates can now download and share signal data outwith Clinical Data Studio with study team members for action. 
 Summary of User Impact 
 | 
 Impact to Users and Roles 
- Central Monitors and Central Statistical Monitors, Clinical Research Associates 
-  Users with View Signals and Manage Signals building blocks. 
 Impact to Workflow Processes 
 No impact to workflow process. 
 Impact to Applications 
 No impact to other applications.  
 Impact on Interface 
 The Download Signals data window includes a .pdf option.You download one, all or specific signals. If you select the .pdf option, you can download signal observations in a pdf file. When a download is in progress, the Download button on the top right corner of the Signals page, remains disabled. For PDF and .xlsx downloads, once the downloaded files are ready, you receive an email confirmation as well as a new message in the Notification Centre.
 Impact to Existing Data or Settings 
 No existing data changes; no configuration or permission updates required.
 No impact to other data or settings.
 User Impact Assessment 
 | Medium

 Special Considerations 
 |  You can start downloading a new set of signals once a download that is in progress, completes. To view Medidata testing documentation for this release, refer to the Medidata Validation Portal.
