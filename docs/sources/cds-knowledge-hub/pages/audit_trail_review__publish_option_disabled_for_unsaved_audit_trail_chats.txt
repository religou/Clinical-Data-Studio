# Audit Trail Review: Publish Option Disabled For Unsaved Audit Trail Chats

## Audit Trail Review: Publish Option Disabled For Unsaved Audit Trail Chats
## What Is New?
 You cannot publish an audit dataset without saving the audit trail chat first . The Publish as Dataset button remains disabled: 
- For unsaved chats.  
- A message appears if you hover over the button indicating that the study already has 10 published datasets. 
 Similarly, you cannot delete chats associated with a published audit dataset. 
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Data managers.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 Data managers can improve audit practices by publishing only audit data of interest, and take appropriate action once the study has 10 published datasets.  
## Where Do I See the Change?
- On the RBQM tab > Audit Trail Review page, if you do not save a completed chat using the Save Prompt option, and then, if you click , the More icon in the top-right corner of the Data Preview section, the Publish as dataset button appears disabled. 
- On the RBQM tab > Audit Trail Review page, if you hover over , the More icon in the top-right corner of the Data Preview section, the Publish as dataset button remains disabled once the study has a total of 10 published audit datasets. A message "Maximum of 10 datasets already published." also appears.  
- On the RBQM tab > Audit Trail Review page, if you click the Saved Chats button, the Saved Chats slide-out panel lists all saved chats. If you click , the More icon for a saved chat linked to published audit dataset from the list, the Delete option appears disabled. In the example below,  Delete appears disabled for non-conformant data entries from Sites in Spain , as this chat is linked to an active, published audit dataset for the study.

## What Else Should I Know?
 You must have the Manage Audit Trail Review building block added to your Cloud Administration role to publish audit datasets.
 Once an active, published dataset is deleted from a study, you can delete the associated chat. Contact your Medidata representative to delete a published audit dataset for a study.
 To view Medidata testing documentation for this release, refer to the  Medidata Validation Portal .
