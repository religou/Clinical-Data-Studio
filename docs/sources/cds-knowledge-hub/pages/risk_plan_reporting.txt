# Risk Plan Reporting

## Risk Plan Reporting
## Description
 You can create a risk plan report that provides risk assessment details for your study. View the report online or download it as a PDF file. 
## Affected Roles
 Study User.
## Advantages
 View the critical data, critical processes, risk categories, and risk inventory in a single consolidated report or in a selection of reports.
## User Impact
 Use this feature to:
- Update risk plan report details and participants. 
- Indicate which participants are required to acknowledge the report. 
- Configure the report to print all or specific components such as critical data, critical processes, risk categories, or the risk inventory. 
- Generate the risk plan report for online review or to download in PDF format. 
## Special Considerations
 Ensure that your role includes the  Generate Risk Plan Report  building block permission. This allows a study user to generate the risk plan report.
 Note : The Study Approval / Acknowledge permission associated with an eSignature is not yet active for Risk Management. Instead, you may acknowledge the report manually by signing in the Signature box at the top of a printed copy or by using a signature feature if included in your PDF viewer software.
