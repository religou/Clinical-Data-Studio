# Risk Assessment: Reuse Templates Across Study Groups

## Risk Assessment: Reuse Templates Across Study Groups
## What Is New?
 You can now reuse the templates for critical data / processes / categorization across study groups for which you have access. 
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Risk Assessment users.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 Users do not need to create the same template across different study groups. Users can only import activated templates across study groups. Users do not any new permissions for templates. 
## Where Do I See the Change?
 Templates are now available directly at the study level in the Library in the left hand menu.
