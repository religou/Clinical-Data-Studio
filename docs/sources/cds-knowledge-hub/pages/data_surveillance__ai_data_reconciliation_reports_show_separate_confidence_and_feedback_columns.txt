## Data Surveillance: AI Data Reconciliation Reports Show Separate Confidence And Feedback Columns
## What Is New?
 AI Data Reconciliation reports now show Confidence and Feedback as two separate columns.
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Data managers.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 Data managers can evaluate the algorithm-assigned confidence level for a report in the Confidence column, and provide feedback by clicking the , thumbs up or thumbs down icon in the Feedback column.
## Where Do I See the Change?
 In the Data Surveillance > AI Data Reconciliation reports, the Confidence and Feedback columns appear separately in all reports.

## What Else Should I Know?
 Data sets must be mapped in Event Analysis, otherwise, the data sets do not appear in the reports or be processed through AI Data Reconciliation's algorithms.
