# RBQM: Technical Changes In Risk Assessment

## RBQM: Technical Changes In Risk Assessment
## What Is New?
RACT scoring no longer appears on the Risk Categorization page in Risk Assessment.
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Risk managers.
## Workflow Process
 No change in the workflow.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 Removing scoring for risk category questions enables you to focus on tasks more relevant to your study, for example, scoring risks affect your study the most. 
 Where Do I See the Change? 
 RACT Question Scoring no longer appears on the RBQM > Risk Assessment > Risk Categorization page.
