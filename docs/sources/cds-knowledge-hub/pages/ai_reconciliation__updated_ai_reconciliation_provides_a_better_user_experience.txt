# AI Reconciliation: Updated AI Reconciliation Provides A Better User Experience

## AI Reconciliation: Updated AI Reconciliation Provides A Better User Experience
## What Is New?
 AI Reconciliation includes updates to the page that enhance the user experience.
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Data managers.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 The updated AI Reconciliation page enhances the user experience and enables data managers to filter and find records of interest, thus enabling greater efficiency in data reconciliation.
## Where Do I See the Change?
 On the Data Surveillance tab, if you click AI Reconciliation on the left-hand menu, the AI Reconciliation page appears. The updated page has the following changes:
