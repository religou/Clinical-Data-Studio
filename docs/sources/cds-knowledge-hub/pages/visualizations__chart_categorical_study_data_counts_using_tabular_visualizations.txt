# Visualizations: Chart Categorical Study Data Counts Using Tabular Visualizations

## Visualizations: Chart Categorical Study Data Counts Using Tabular Visualizations
## What Is New?
 Data Surveillance > Visualizations includes a new chart type, tabular visualizations, that you can use to obtain counts or percentages of categorical study data. Tabular visualizations aggregate data by summing or averaging multiple dataset variables in a table format created using Data Connect Transformations . The chart shows data in rows and columns with information summarized (aggregated) at the intersection points. For example, the percentage of patients experiencing the AE at the preferred term level, and the associated seriousness.
 Note : You must have at least one Tabular Visual type collection  in Data Connect to create tabular visualizations in Data Surveillance.

## How Do I Benefit?
 With tabular visualizations, data managers can segment populations of interest and identify patient groups for further investigation. Investigating counts or percentages of required parameters in study data enables data managers to:
- Understand treatment efficacy by comparing outcomes and treatments. 
- Evaluate compliance looking at rates of completion and adherence to medication. 
- Explore differential subgroup responses based on demographic or baseline characteristics. 
- Examine how well groups meet primary and secondary endpoints. 
- Analyze AE trends relative to outcomes, treatments, or other effect states.
