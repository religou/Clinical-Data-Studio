# Design Reports Show Last Updated And Published Details

## Design Reports Show Last Updated And Published Details
## Description
 Patient Profile Design Reports now include last updated and published dates along with the name of the user who published or made the last update to the design.
## Affected Roles
 All.
## Advantages
 Profile reviewers can identify whether the profile design contains the most updated data based on the published or last updated date.
## User Impact
 The Medidata Detect > Patient Data Surveillance (PDS) > Patient Profiles > Reports > Patient Profiles Design Report displays the Published Date if a design was published along with the Published By and the Last Updated Date.
