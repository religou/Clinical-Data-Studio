# Identify Critical Data In Expression Builder, KRIs, QTLs and In Data Mapping

## Identify Critical Data In Expression Builder, KRIs, QTLs and In Data Mapping
## What Is New?
 While configuring KRIs, QTLs and Data Mapping, and in the Expression Builder in Data Sets:
 Critical data sets and variables appear with a blue flag indicating critical data. A tooltip also appears for data sets with critical data.
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Risk managers, study managers, data managers.
## Workflow Process
 No change to workflow process.
## Other Applications
 None.
## How Do I Benefit?
 Identifying critical data sets used in your study helps you focus on and monitor the most important data to ensure:
- Protocol compliance, patient safety, data quality, and data integrity. 
- Product or treatment efficacy. 
## Where Do I See the Change?
 For any of the following pages in RBQM > Configuration, or Data Surveillance > Configuration, if you create or edit any configuration, c ritical data sets and variables appear with a blue flag indicating critical data. If you hover over the critical data, a tooltip also appears. 
- KRIs 
- QTLs 
- Data Mapping  
 Similarly, in Data Surveillance > Listings, if you select data sets and variables in an expression builder to create a listing, data sets and variables with critical data points to the study appear with a blue flag .
