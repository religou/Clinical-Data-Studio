# New Risk Category Added for Diversity

## New Risk Category Added for Diversity
## Description
 A new category, Diversity, is available in the list of predefined categories to choose from when adding a new Risk Category at the study group or study level.
## Affected Roles
 All.
## Advantages
 The study manager can use the Diversity risk category to better understand the risks associated with different groups of participants. 
## User Impact
 The Diversity category is an addition to the existing set of categories you can select when creating or editing a risk. The risk objective for the Diversity category is: “Focus on diverse patient populations including race, ethnicity, and underrepresented populations such as sex, gender identity, age, socioeconomic status, disability, pregnancy status, lactation status, and co-morbidity.”
