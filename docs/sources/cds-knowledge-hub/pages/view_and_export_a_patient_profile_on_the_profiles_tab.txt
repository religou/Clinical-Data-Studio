# View and Export a Patient Profile on the Profiles Tab

## View and Export a Patient Profile on the Profiles Tab
## Description
 The Design page now includes the Profiles tab, which allows you to view and export a patient profile. The Profiles tab displays the:
- Patient Selector – Displays the full list of study patients. You can select any patient from the list to view that patient's patient profile. The Patient Selector section is expanded by default. 
- Patient Profile  – Displays the patient profile for the patient that you selected in the Patient Selector. The patient  profile displays with actual study data to help you evaluate how the profile design is taking shape in real time.  The patient profile for the top-listed patient in the Patient Selector displays by default. 
 You can also view and print patient profiles in PDF format by way of the Profiles tab.
## Affected Roles
 All.
## Advantages
 By viewing the patient profile during the profile design process, you can:
- view the patient profile with actual patient study data to validate if the design is correct and displaying the desired results. 
- edit the design and then view the profile again with the modified design against the subject data in iteration. 
 Tables in the patient profile display as expanded by default; however, you can collapse tables to enhance your ability to view and scan the data in the profile.  You can also expand and collapse the Patient Selector table. 
 Viewing and printing a patient profile in PDF format allows you to  send the patient profile to design reviewers who do not have access to Medidata Patient Profiles. Therefore, reviewers without Medidata Patient Profiles can still evaluate the patient profile design and provide feedback to the designer regarding design layout adjustments.
