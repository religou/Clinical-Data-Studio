# PDS: Minor AI Data Reconciliation User Interface Enhancements for April 2024

## PDS: Minor AI Data Reconciliation User Interface Enhancements for April 2024
## What Is New?
 Patient Data Surveillance (PDS) > AI Data Reconciliation includes the following minor enhancements:
- When viewing any AI Data Reconciliation report, when you hover your cursor on the Reviewed icon, , in the Status column, your cursor does not change to a pointer finger. 
- The Status column is now a fixed column positioned to the right of the Country column. 
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Data managers.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 In previous Data Reviewer releases, when you hovered your cursor on a Reviewed icon in the Status column, your cursor changed to a pointer finger indicating that the icon was clickable. However, you cannot click the Reviewed icon. Updating the cursor to not change to a pointer finger eliminates the potential of confusing you into thinking that you can click the icon to access more details or functionality.
 The fixed Status column enables you to always see a record's review and comment status as you horizontally scroll through reports.
