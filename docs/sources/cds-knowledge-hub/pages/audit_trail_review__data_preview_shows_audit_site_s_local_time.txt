 Summary of Changes 
 | 
 As highlighted in the Release Newsletter : 
 When you submit a prompt in the Audit Trail Review chat, the Data Preview section now includes an Audit Site's Local Time column.
 Benefit to Users 
 | Including the Audit Site's Local Time column in the Data Preview section:
- Allows you to track data modifications and filter dates/times based on the site’s local time zone, rather than being restricted to standard UTC.
- Enhances data monitoring and anomaly detection by enabling precise filtering for actions occurring outside standard operating hours.
- Supports highly specific localized use cases, such as identifying data modified or inactivated outside of business hours (for example, 8:00 PM to 7:00 AM, or 12:00 AM to 6:00 AM local time).
- Tracks data entered or queries opened over the weekend (for example, Friday 9:00 PM to Sunday 9:00 PM), automatically accommodating country-specific weekend definitions.
