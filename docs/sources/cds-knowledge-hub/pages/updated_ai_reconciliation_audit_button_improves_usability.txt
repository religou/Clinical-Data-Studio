# Updated AI Reconciliation Audit Button Improves Usability

## Updated AI Reconciliation Audit Button Improves Usability
## What Is New?
 The Audit button now appears in the top-right corner of the AI Reconciliation page.  You can view an audit report of historical actions and updates made on all AI Reconciliation reports in your study using the audit button. 
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Data Managers.
## Workflow Process
 No impact on the workflow.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 The updated position of the audit button allows you to easily initiate audit tracking for historical actions and updates made in AI Reconciliation reports.
