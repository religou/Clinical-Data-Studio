## Change Details
 Product 
 | Clinical Data Studio
 Version 
 | 2026.1.0
 Change Type 
 | Enhancement (Updated Functionality)
 Functionality or Module 
 | Data Surveillance Configuration
 Summary of Changes 
 | 
 You can now configure the AI Reconciliation reports you want in Clean Patient Tracker on the Data Surveillance Configuration page.  
 Benefit to Users 
 | Configuring Data Surveillance to auto-populate the essential AI Reconciliation reports in Clean Patient Tracker: 
- Ensures only AI Reconciliation reports that are necessary for your study appear. 
- Enables your organization to seamlessly incorporate AI Reconciliation reports data into Data Surveillance for tracking and analysis. 
- Ensures Clean Patient Tracker analyses the most recent available AI Reconciliation reports to produce the most accurate data analysis. 
- Reduces the time and effort to track AI Reconciliation reports. 
- Ensures data analysis quality by reducing data entry error. 
 Summary of User Impact 
 | 
 Impact to Users and Roles 
 Data managers. 
 Impact to Workflow Processes 
 No impact to workflow process.
 Impact to Applications 
 No impact on other applications.  
 Impact on Interface 
 The Data Surveillance > Configuration > Clean Patient Tracker page now includes a section to select AI Reconciliation reports.  
 If you select the AI Reconciliation reports you want Clean Patient Tracker to show and click Save , the configuration is saved. 
 Impact to Existing Data or Settings 
 No existing data changes; no configuration or permission updates required.
