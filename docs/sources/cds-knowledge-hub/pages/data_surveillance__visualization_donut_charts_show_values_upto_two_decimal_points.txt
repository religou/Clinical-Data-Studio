# Data Surveillance: Visualization Donut Charts Show Values Up to Two Decimal Points

## Data Surveillance: Visualization Donut Charts Show Values Up to Two Decimal Points
## What Is New?
 Visualization donut charts now show values up to two decimal points.
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 All study team members.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process. 
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications. 
## How Do I Benefit?
 Graphically visualize study data relationships in donut chart enables you to accurately: 
- Gain a clearer understanding or perspective of the study data. 
- Identify study trends and patterns. 
- Track study performance. 
- Build and share reports or present on study data metrics using clear, visually appealing graphical renderings.  
## Where Do I See the Change?
 In the Data Surveillance > Visualizations page, if you create a donut chart with percentage, the chart shows values up to two decimal points.
