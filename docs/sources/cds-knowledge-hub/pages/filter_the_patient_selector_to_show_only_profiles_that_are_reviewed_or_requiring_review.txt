# Data Surveillance: Filter the Patient Selector to Show Only Profiles That Are Reviewed or Requiring Review

## Data Surveillance: Filter the Patient Selector to Show Only Profiles That Are Reviewed or Requiring Review
## What Is New?
 In Data Surveillance > Patient Profiles: when working on published or draft designs, the Profiles tab > Patient Selector table > Review Status column now includes a Non Blanks filter option. Filtering the Review Status column by Non Blanks refreshes the Patient Selector to show only profiles that are in 'Reviewed' or 'Requiring Review' status. 
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 All.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 In previous Patient Profiles releases, you could filter the Patient Selector > Review Status column by:
- Reviewed   – Profiles that have been reviewed since the last profile update. 
- Requiring Review   – Profiles that have been reviewed at least once before, but not since the last profile update. 
- Blanks – Profiles that have never been reviewed. 
 Now you can further refine your Patient Selector view by filtering by: 
- Non Blanks – Profiles that are in 'Reviewed' or 'Requiring Review' status.
