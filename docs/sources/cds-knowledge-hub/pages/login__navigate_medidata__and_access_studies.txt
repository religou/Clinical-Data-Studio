# Log In, Navigate Medidata, and Access Studies

## Log In, Navigate Medidata, and Access Studies
 You log in to Medidata to access your studies. New users must first activate your Medidata account.
 &amp;lt;p&amp;gt;&amp;lt;br/&amp;gt;&amp;lt;/p&amp;gt;  
## Activate Your Medidata Account
 Access to iMedidata is by invitation only. As a new user, you cannot log in until you receive an email and click the link to accept the invitation to join iMedidata. You are then redirected to an activation web page where you create your username and password and register as an iMedidata user. 
 You can register, create a username and password, and accept the Terms of Use for up to 45 days after you receive an invitation.
 To learn more, see  How to Activate Your iMedidata Account .
## Log In to Medidata
 You can access iMedidata after you receive your email invitation and activate your iMedidata account .
- Enter  www.imedidata.com  in your browser address field.  
- Enter your username and password on the iMedidata login page.
Your Medidata homepage  appears. This  includes any assigned studies, study groups, app access, and eLearnings. 
 If you cannot log in to iMedidata, see Trouble Logging into iMedidata for help with resolving potential login issues.
## Navigate the Medidata Home Page
 The Medidata home page appears when you sign into Medidata. The home page provides a unified starting point for all your studies and Medidata capabilities.
 From the home page, you can access your recent activity, studies, and sites. You can also access the help center, and your profile, courses, and notifications.

## All Clients Page
 Click the Clients menu > All Clients to view your clients.
- Enter a study group name in the search field to find a study group. 
- Click a study group name to select a capability.  
## All Study Groups Page
 Click the Study Groups menu > All Study Groups to view your study groups.
- Enter a study group name in the search field to find a study group. 
- Click a study group name to select a capability.  
## All Studies Page
 Click the Studies menu > All Studies to view your studies.  
- Enter a study name in the search field to find a study. 
- Click a study to select a study 
## All Sites Page
 Click the Sites menu > All Sites  view all sites for your assigned studies.  
## Find and Access Studies
- Log in to iMedidata.com .
 The Medidata Home page shows. To learn more, see Navigate the Medidata Home Page . 
- Click the an app name  in the tab bar below the Medidata Home page welcome message. For example, Data Surveillance.

- In the Clients table, click the client name containing the study you want to access. 
- In the Study Groups table, click the study group name containing the study you want to access. 
- In the Studies table, click the study you want to access.
