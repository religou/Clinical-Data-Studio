# Generate Patient Profile PDFs in Chinese

## Generate Patient Profile PDFs in Chinese
## Description
 You can now export patient profiles to PDF format in Chinese. 
## Affected Roles
 All.
## Advantages
 If you use Chinese characters in your text editor, you can view profile exports in Chinese.
## User Impact
 On the Medidata Detect > Patient Data Surveillance (PDS) > Patient Profiles > Patient Profile Designs > Profiles tab, if you click PDF  and select Single Patient or Multiple Patients, you can download patient profile PDFs for individual or multiple patients. The PDF export includes Chinese characters or letters included in the patient profile.
