# Insights: Unclean Patients Relabeled to In Progress Patients

## Insights: Unclean Patients Relabeled to In Progress Patients
## What Is New?
 All instances of the Unclean Patients label are updated to In Progress Patients throughout Clean Patient Tracker.
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Data managers
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process. 
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications. 
## How Do I Benefit?
 The updated label is more appropriate for global audiences.
## Where Do I See the Change?
 In the Insights > Clean Patient Tracker, all occurrences of "Unclean Patients" are relabeled to "In Progress Patients" in the: 
- Unclean Patients By Site - Top 10 section 
- Unclean Patients By Site - Detailed View window 
- Patients section
