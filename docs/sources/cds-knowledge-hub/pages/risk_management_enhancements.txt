# Risk Management Enhancements

## Risk Management Enhancements
## What Is New?
 Easier access to Detect:
- You can now go to Detect from the Risk Management application with the aid of the Overview icon in the left navigation panel. 
 Risk Inventory capability for saving risk details and enhanced risk identification: 
- The Risk Inventory now includes an Export button for downloading risk data. 
- The naming convention of downloaded files now ensures a unique file name every time you export a file.
- In the Risk Inventory, you can now see the Risk Origin, that is, Critical Data, Critical Processes, and Risk Categorization. This allows you to distinguish the source to which risks belong.  
- The Risk Category details from the Risk Origin column of the Risk Inventory are removed to increase Risk Management performance. 
- The hover text on the Risk Origin now shows only the source of the risk, and does not open any popup for the source details. 
- For newly added risks, the risk review schedule is based on the set frequency and the target review date.
