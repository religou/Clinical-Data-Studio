# Patient Profiles: Quality of Life Improvements

## Patient Profiles: Quality of Life Improvements
## What Is New?
- If you do not have access to all sites and countries and you try to access a published design, you receive the following message: "Not all sites and countries are assigned to this use. Please contact your user administrator." 
- When viewing patient profiles in the Profile tab or PDF export, element titles are colored accordingly based on the section color configured in the design layout. 
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
- Users with limited site access receive the system message. 
- All Patient Profiles users see the updated patient profile element title colors. 
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process. 
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications. 
## How Do I Benefit?
- You receive a clear message explain why you cannot access published designs and direction for remediating actions. 
- When viewing a patient profile in the Profile tab or PDF export, element titles:
- Show in the color selected for the corresponding section in the Layout tab for the design. 
- Are clearly visible and consistent   across both the Profile tab and the PDF export. 
## Where Do I See the Change?
- On the Patient Profile Designs page > Published tab > Published Designs table, if you do not have access to any sites and countries, then you cannot click to open any published design. If you try to open a published design, then you receive the following message: "Not all sites and countries are assigned to this use. Please contact your user administrator." 
- When working in a design on the Profiles tab, if you click a patient ID from the Patient Selector to view a patient profile or if you click Download or Download Profile to export patient profiles to PDF, then the section colors configured in the design's Layout tab are reflected in the corresponding patient profile element titles. 
## Where Do I Learn More?
 There   are   no additional relevant resources. 
## What Else Should I Know?
 When viewing a patient profile in the Profile tab or PDF export, if a section color is black, then the text color shows in white.
 When viewing a patient profile PDF export, if the background color of an element as well as the section title is black, then the text color is white; otherwise, the text color is black.
 When a new section is to the design layout on the Layout tab, then when viewing a patient profile in the Profile tab or PDF export, by default the section header color is white and the tables show in the following respective colors:
- All graphs – Purple 
- Patient Information table – Purple 
- Transposed table – Green 
- Wide table -- Green 
- Timeline elements – Purple
