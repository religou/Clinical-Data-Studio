# Insights: Clean Patient Tracker Terminology Updates

## Insights: Clean Patient Tracker Terminology Updates
## What Is New?
 Clean Patient Tracker includes the new terminology updates:
- The previous label, Last Calculation, which shows the timestamp of the most recent recalculations is now labeled as Current Calculation. 
- The renamed AI Data Reconciliation report names are reflected in the following locations:
- Unclean Patients By Site - Detailed View window > Sites section 
- Outstanding Tasks section 
- Patients section 
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Data managers.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 Relabeling Last Calculation to Current Calculation more accurately communicates that Clean Patient Tracker currently reflects the most recent recalculation as of the stated timestamp.
 Updating the AI Data Reconciliation report names throughout Clean Patient Tracker ensures that the correct and most current names are consistently used throughout all Detect capabilities.
