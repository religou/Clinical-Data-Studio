# RBQM: Enhance Study Data Compliance And Accuracy With Audit Trail Review

## RBQM: Enhance Study Data Compliance And Accuracy With Audit Trail Review
## What Is New?
 Audit trails are computer-generated records that capture all the activity in the system, and are valuable tools to maintain a high-level of data integrity, and are extremely useful to study teams. An Audit Trail Review (ATR) involves examining the recorded history of transactions or activities within a system to ensure compliance with regulations, detect any discrepancies, and verify the integrity of data. This review tracks all changes made to records, who made them, and when, providing a transparent and accountable record of system usage. It is important in ensuring accuracy in financial reporting, and supporting forensic investigations in cases of fraud or unauthorized access. The Audit Trail Review feature in Clinical Data Studio applies Large Language Models (LLMs) and Generative AI to explore complex audit trail data sets.   
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 All study team members.
## Workflow Process
 Audit Trail Review (ATR) pulls in operational metadata from Rave EDC to show edits, updates, and deletions made to data endpoints. Clinical Data Studio uses Generative AI to automatically build customized queries, and executes them against the operational Rave data to generate meaningful audit data sets that facilitate data quality checks.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 Audit Trail Review: 
- Provides an intuitive, user-friendly interface that makes data exploration simple and accessible. 
- Enables you to effortlessly extract insights from vast and complex audit trail datasets using Large Language Models (LLMs) and Generative AI technology. 
- Seamlessly filters massive datasets, where you can type a question into the interactive field to generate a meaningful dataset from your audit trail data. 
- Delivers actionable insights that allow you to make smarter data decisions. 
- Provides an Intuitive and user-friendly interface where AI-driven capabilities make finding the insights you need quicker and easier. 
- Accelerates decision making by allowing you to create, explore, and analyze audit trail data in ways that were previously time-consuming or complex. 
- Enables you to configure published audit data sets in KRIs and listings for use in your study. 
 For ATR Manager users (with the Manage Audit Trail Review access), ATR offers additional flexibility to publish data sets directly within Clinical Data Studio capabilities. This integration allows audit trail insights to be used for analysis, collaboration, and reporting across projects and teams.

## What Else Should I Know?
 Permissions 
- You must have the  View Audit Trail Review t o ask questions and save prompts, whereas to publish audit data sets you need the Manage Audit Trail Review building block added respectively to your role in Cloud Administration.  
 ATR Behavior 
- No blinded data sets are shown as part of audit trail review data. 
- You can only publish a maximum of 10 audit data sets in any study.  
- You can publish only ten audit data sets per study environment. 
- The audit workflow runs weekly, every weekend. The published data set is available in the study only after the audit workflow runs. 
- You cannot publish and download a data set if the Data Preview shows no records. 
 ATR Limitations 
- Audit trail review does not support: 
- Any aggregations. For example, a question like Give audits for AE form where a data point was modified more than 10 times, may produce a data set that was modified just once. 
- Complex computations, or calculating differences in timestamps. For example, a question like Give audits where a datapoint was modified 10 days after it was entered. may not produce a result. 
- Results at any level other than data points. For example, a question like Give me all sites in the data, does not produce a result. 
- Filtering on data point values. 
- Question prompts asking for all audits in the study. 
- Filtering in the published audit data set is limited to the following fields:  
- audit user, audit date/date range, form oid, field oid, audit subcategories, patient ID, country, and site. 
- Published audit data listings do not support the following functionality: 
- Submit queries. 
- Submit vendor queries. 
- Cross-dataset listing creation 
- Missing records 
- The ATR functionality times out if the learning model takes longer than five minutes to process a request.
