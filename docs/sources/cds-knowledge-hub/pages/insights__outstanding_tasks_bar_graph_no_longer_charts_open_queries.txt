# Insights: Outstanding Tasks Bar Graph No Longer Charts Open Queries

## Insights: Outstanding Tasks Bar Graph No Longer Charts Open Queries
## What Is New?
 The Clean Patient Tracker > Outstanding Tasks section no longer charts open queries.
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Data managers. 
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process. 
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications. 
## How Do I Benefit?
 The Outstanding Tasks section previously charted at the study level all:
- Open queries. 
- AI Data Reconciliation reports containing unreviewed records. 
- Data listings containing unreviewed records. 
 However, some studies may have an exceedingly large number of open queries compared to the number of unreviewed records in a given AI Data Reconciliation report or data listing. Therefore, the Outstanding Tasks bar chart > Open Queries bar would skew the graph and make it less readable. Removing open queries from the Outstanding Task bar chart makes the graph more readable.
 Also, the number of open queries shows in the metrics at the top of the Clean Patient Tracker page. Therefore, including the open queries count in the Outstanding Tasks section offered no benefit.
## Where Do I See the Change?
 You can see this enhancement in the Insights > Clean Patient Tracker page > Outstanding Tasks section.
