# Patient Profiles Clouds Adds a Leading Zero to Single-Digit Dates

## Patient Profiles Clouds Adds a Leading Zero to Single-Digit Dates
## Description
 If someone enters the day or month portion of a date into Rave X with a single digit rather than two digits (for example, 2 May 2018 instead of 02 May 2018), then when Patient Profiles Cloud consumes that data, it automatically inserts a leading zero to begin the number. For example, Patient Profiles Cloud automatically turns 2 May 2018 into 02 May 2018.
## Affected Roles
 All.
## Advantages
 Before Patient Profiles 2018.3.0, if someone entered the day or month portion of a date into Rave X with a single digit rather than two digits (for example, 2 May 2018 instead of 02 May 2018), then Patient Profiles Cloud did not recognize the patient's Day 0 Date. Therefore, that patient's profile would display empty graphs and timelines even though data was present for that patient in Patient Profiles Cloud.
 Now if someone enters the day or month portion of a date into Rave X with a single digit rather than two digits, Patient Profiles Cloud automatically inserts a leading zero in the digit. As a result, Patient Profiles can better handle data from Rave X to render graphs and timelines in profiles.
 In addition, the Design Page > Profiles tab > Patient Selector table always displays the day and month portion of a date with two digits.
## User Impact
 None.
## Special Considerations
 None.
