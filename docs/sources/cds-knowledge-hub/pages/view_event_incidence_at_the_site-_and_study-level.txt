# View Event Incidence at the Site- and Study-level

## View Event Incidence at the Site- and Study-level
## Description
 The Event Incidence Plot is a scatter plot that displays your study's patient-reported events and the rate the events occurred at the site- and study-level. 
 You can use the Event Incidence Plot to analyze:
- Which specific event terms were reported as experienced by patients within a domain at the site level. 
- How many patients at a specific site reported an event term compared to all patients at the site. This is also known as the incidence rate. 
- The incidence rate for reported event terms at each individual site compared to the overall study event incidence rates. 
- Which sites are considered  statistically  normal, outliers, or extreme outliers in terms of probability of having the event occur. 
## Affected Roles
- Central monitors 
- Data managers 
- Medical monitors 
## Advantages
 By analyzing event terms in your study, you can:
- Identify whether sites are tracking events correctly. For example, whether a site is over or underreporting adverse events. 
- View event trending patterns. For example, if a large number of patients at a site experience headaches. 
- Detect correlation between events. For example, if a large number of patients at a site experience URTI and increased heart rate. 
- Identify sites that have significantly higher or lower incidence rates for individual events compared to overall study event incidence rates. 
 Based on your findings, you can determine appropriate corresponding actions such as to:
- Investigate sites to ensure proper protocols and procedures are being followed. 
- Discontinue the drug.
