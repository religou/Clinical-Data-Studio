# Patient Profiles: Audit Details Capture Reviews and Comments

## Patient Profiles: Audit Details Capture Reviews and Comments
## What Is New?
- The Patient Profiles   audit feature now captures  reviews and comments . 
- The Audit History page is renamed from Patient Profiles Comments to Patient Profiles. 
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Patient Profiles users with audit review permissions.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 The Patient Profiles audit history is expanded to include details about patient profile review status and action taken.
## Where Do I See the Change?
 The audit button now appears in the header section of a patient's profile instead of the comments section. The Patient Profiles Audit History page includes the Activity and Activity Details columns. Statuses for both columns include "Reviewed" or "Review Status Removed."
