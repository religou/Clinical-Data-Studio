# Data Surveillance: Add Patient-level Internal Review Comments Within Patient Profile Designs

## Data Surveillance: Add Patient-level Internal Review Comments Within Patient Profile Designs
## What Is New?
 When reviewing patient profiles, you can add patient-level review comments directly within published patient profile designs.
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Patient profile designers.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 Adding patient-level review comments directly within patient profile designs:
- Provides a convenient, centralized way to summarize or notate interesting review findings. 
- Facilitates a highly visible and continuous flow of notes and findings to reference across reviews cycle. 
## Where Do I See the Change?
 In Data Surveillance > Patient Profiles, when working with published designs, the Profiles tab > Patient Selector table now contains a  Comments column. The Comments column shows one of the following icons in each row:
- Plus Icon – Indicates that no comments have been made on the patient in the corresponding row. You can click the Plus icon to add a comment on the patient and start a comment thread. 
- Comment Bubble Icon – Indicates that there has been at least one comment made on the patient. You can click the Comment Bubble icon to view previous comments made on the patient or to add comments to the thread. 
 Tip : Click images to enlarge.

## What Else Should I Know?
- All team members in your study can view all patient profile comments. 
- You cannot add comments to a patient profile design while a data refresh for that design processes. 
- You cannot edit, delete, or download patient profile comments.
