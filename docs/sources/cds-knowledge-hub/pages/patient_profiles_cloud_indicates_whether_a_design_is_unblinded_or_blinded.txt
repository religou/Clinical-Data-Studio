# Patient Profiles Cloud Indicates Whether a Design is Unblinded or Blinded

## Patient Profiles Cloud Indicates Whether a Design is Unblinded or Blinded
## Description
 Patient Profiles Cloud provides visual cues to let you know whether a design is blinded or unblinded.
## Affected Roles
 All.
## Advantages
 Clearly indicating whether a design is blinded or not lets you know whether or not you are looking at sensitive data.
## User Impact
- The Patient Profile Designs page includes a new column, Privacy, which lists whether a design is unblinded or blinded.
 Tip : Click image to enlarge.
- When you access a blinded design, a   icon displays next to the study name on the Design page, Reports, page, and Settings page. However, no icon displays next to the study name for unblinded designs.
## Special Considerations
 None.
