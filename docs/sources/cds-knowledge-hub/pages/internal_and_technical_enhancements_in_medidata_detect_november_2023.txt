# Internal and Technical Enhancements in Medidata Detect November 2023

## Internal and Technical Enhancements in Medidata Detect November 2023
## What Is New?
 Medidata Detect November 2023 includes the following internal and technical enhancements:
- Improved Detect Landing Page and Data Reviewer page load time and efficiency. 
- Key Risk Indicators, Quality Tolerance Limits, and Detect Configuration Manager security upgrades. 
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Users with access to the Patient Data Surveillance (PDS) or Key Risk Indicators (KRIs) offerings.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 The Detect Landing Page and the data that shows in the Patient Data Surveillance (PDS) card and Key Risk Indicators (KRIs) card now loads faster.
 Key Risk Indicators, Quality Tolerance Limits, and Detect Configuration Manager security is modernize and upgraded.
## Where Do I See the Change?
 No changes to the user interface.

 There are no additional relevant resources.
