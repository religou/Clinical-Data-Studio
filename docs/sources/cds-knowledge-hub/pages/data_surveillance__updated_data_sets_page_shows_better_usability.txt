## Data Surveillance: Updated Data Sets Page Shows Better Usability
## What Is New?
 The Data Surveillance > Data Sets page includes a clearer look with enhanced usability. This includes updates to various screen components such as:
- Columns 
- Filtering options 
- Hyperlinks on Patient IDs. 
- Patient ID, Site Name, and Site Country columns remain fixed on scrolling. 
- Better pagination. 
- Better search option. 
- Faster data retrieval. 
- Better placed data grid. 
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Study managers, and data managers.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 With better usability, you can locate and access data easily in Data Sets and carry out study-related tasks with greater efficiency.
