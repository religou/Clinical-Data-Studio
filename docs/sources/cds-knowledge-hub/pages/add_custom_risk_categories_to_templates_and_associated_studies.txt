# Add Custom Risk Categories to Templates and Associated Studies

## Add Custom Risk Categories to Templates and Associated Studies
## What Is New?
 You can now create a custom category when you select Custom in the Select Category dropdown, while creating a Risk Categorization Template, Critical Data, or Critical Process at client division level. You can also add these templates to their study and the categories associated with them.
- You can now create multiple custom risk categories to a risk categorization template, and 
- Create custom categories to use the templates at study-level for Critical Data and Critical processes. 
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Data manager.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process. 
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications. 
## How Do I Benefit?
 You can now create and use custom risk categories for your studies.
## Where Do I See the Change?
 Detect Landing Page > Risk Management > Studies > All Studies > Risk Categorization Templates
