# Insights: Download the Clean Patient Tracker Patients List and Open Queries List

## Insights: Download the Clean Patient Tracker Patients List and Open Queries List
## What Is New?
 You can download the Clean Patient Tracker Patients list and the Open Queries list to a CSV file.
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Data managers.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 Downloading the Clean Patient Tracker Patients list and Open Queries list enables you to:
- Easily exchange the lists externally with stakeholders to facilitate communication. 
- Locally save and archive the lists. 
## Where Do I See the Change?
 On the:
- Insights > Clean Patient Tracker page > Patients section, and 
- Insights > Open Queries page > Open Query Records section 
 you can click the Download button. Insights shows a success message and the file opens locally on your machine as a CSV file.
