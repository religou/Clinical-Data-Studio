# PDS: Show or Hide Tables Columns for Approved Listings Results and AI Data Reconciliation Reports

## PDS: Show or Hide Tables Columns for Approved Listings Results and AI Data Reconciliation Reports
## What Is New?
 You can now filter an approved listing's results table and AI Data Reconciliation reports to show or hide specific table columns.
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 All.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 You can filter the tables to show only specific information to help find the specific data listing records or report information you need. The Show Columns menu functionality and look and feel now also consistently matches that of the functionality on the Data Sets page.
## Where Do I See the Change?
 On the Data Reviewer > Data Listings page > Approved Listings tab: When you select an approved listing, the listing's results table now includes a Show Columns menu. 
 Tip : Click images to enlarge.
 On the Data Reviewer > AI Data Reconciliation page, when you select to view any report, the report's table now includes a Show Columns menu.
