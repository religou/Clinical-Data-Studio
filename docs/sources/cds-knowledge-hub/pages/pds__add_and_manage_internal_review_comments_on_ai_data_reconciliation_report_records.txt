# PDS: Add and Manage Internal Review Comments on AI Data Reconciliation Report Records

## PDS: Add and Manage Internal Review Comments on AI Data Reconciliation Report Records
## What Is New?
 You can add comments directly on individual records that appear in any AI Data Reconciliation report. You can also: 
- View a record's comment history. 
- Edit and delete your own comments. 
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Data Managers. 
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process. 
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications. 
## How Do I Benefit?
 Comments enable you to share review notations with your study team. For example, you can comment on your rationale for marking a record as reviewed. Or you can communicate with others your intention to review a record once its query is closed.
## Where Do I See the Change?
 In Patient Data Surveillance (PDS) > AI Data Reconciliation: when viewing any report, you can c lick the More button, , in the row corresponding to a finding you want to comment on, and then click Comment . A Comments panel appears where you can enter and submit your comment. Afterwards, i n the row containing the finding, the Status column shows a Comment icon, . You can click a Comment icon to: 
- View the comment history and associated metadata. 
- Edit or delete your own comments.
