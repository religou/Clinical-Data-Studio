# CSM: Rounding and Propagation Heatmap and Summary Table Synchronously Highlight Same Site Selection

## CSM: Rounding and Propagation Heatmap and Summary Table Synchronously Highlight Same Site Selection
## What Is New?
 In the Sites with Rounding heatmap, when you click a site ID in the heatmap's x-axis; for example, Site 1078, then the Rounding Events Summary table synchronously filters and highlights to show the row containing the same site ID.
 Likewise, in the Rounding Events Summary table, when you click the row of a particular site ID, then the Sites with Rounding heatmap synchronously:
- Repositions to show the selected site as the first site listed on the left on the x-axis. 
- Highlights the site ID in the x-axis in blue font color. 
 This same functionality also occurs with the Sites with Propagation heatmap and Propagation Events Summary table.
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Central monitors
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 Synchronizing the site selection in the heatmaps and summary tables improves your ability to simultaneously analyze site data from the heatmap and tabular perspective.
## Where Do I See the Change?
 You can find the heatmap and summary table synchronicity on the:
- Centralized Statistical Monitoring (CSM) > Data Veracity > Rounding & Propagation page > Rounding Events tab 
- Centralized Statistical Monitoring (CSM) > Data Veracity > Rounding & Propagation page > Propagation Events tab
