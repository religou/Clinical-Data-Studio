# RBQM: Risk Inventory Filter Selections Persist When Changing Tabs

## RBQM: Risk Inventory Filter Selections Persist When Changing Tabs
## What Is New?
 On the RBQM > Risk Inventory page, if you Review Status, Risk Status, and Risk Owner filter selections persist when you switch between the Risks tab, QTLs tab, and KRIs tab.
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Risk managers.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 Retaining your Risk Inventory filter selections improves your data exploration experience since you no longer need to reselect your filters each time you switch between tabs.
## Where Do I See the Change?
 On the RBQM > Risk Inventory page.
## Where Do I Learn More?
 None.
