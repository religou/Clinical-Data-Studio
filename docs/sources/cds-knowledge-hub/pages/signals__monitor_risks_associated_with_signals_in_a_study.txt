## Change Details
 Product 
 | Clinical Data Studio 
 Version 
 | 2026.1.0
 Change Type 
 | Enhancement (Updated Functionality)
 Functionality or Module 
 | Signals 
 Summary of Changes 
 | 
 As highlighted in the Release Newsletter : 
 You can assess and associate potential risks configured in a study with signal observations you recieve on the Signals page. 
 Benefit to Users 
 | Data managers can assess risks associated with the study and the signal observations, prioritize efforts and ensure effective study oversight. 
 Summary of User Impact 
 | 
 Impact to Users and Roles 
- Central Monitors and Central Statistical Monitors, Clinical Research Associates 
-  Users with View Signals and Manage Signals building blocks. 
 Impact to Workflow Processes 
 No impact to workflow process. 
 Impact to Applications 
 No impact to other applications.  
 Impact on Interface 
 If you click the three dots (More icon), for any signal, and then click Open Signal Details , the Signal details slide out panel shows a new Associated risks field, where you can link study risks with the signal.
 Impact to Existing Data or Settings 
 No existing data changes; no configuration or permission updates required.
 User Impact Assessment 
 | High

 User Impact Assessment 
 The impact of the change on system behavior and on the user's interaction with it.
- Low – There is no change to current software behavior.
- Moderate – Change includes corrections or improvements that may change user interaction with the software, such as minor UI or minor functionality changes.
- High – Change includes software changes which require review of existing settings, workflow and/or operational procedures to ensure correct user interaction with the software.
