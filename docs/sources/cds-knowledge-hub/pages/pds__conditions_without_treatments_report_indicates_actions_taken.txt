# PDS: Conditions Without Treatments Report Indicates Actions Taken

## PDS: Conditions Without Treatments Report Indicates Actions Taken
## What Is New?
 In AI Data Reconciliation, the Conditions without Treatments report now includes the following columns:
- ACTION TAKEN CM – A check mark indicates that the patient took a concomitant medication.  
- ACTION TAKEN CP – A check mark indicates that the patient underwent a concomitant procedure. 
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Data managers.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 You must map a Treatment ConMed and Treatment ConProcedure in Detect Mapping's Event Analysis for the Action Taken CM and Action Taken CP columns to show in the Conditions without Treatments report.
## How Do I Benefit?
 You can quickly determine whether or not there have been concomitant medications taken or concomitant procedures performed on patients who experienced specific conditions.
## Where Do I See the Change?
 Patient Data Surveillance > AI Data Reconciliation > Conditions tab > Conditions without Treatments report
