# Determine Study Data Outliers, Risks, and Trends Using Error Plots

## Determine Study Data Outliers, Risks, and Trends Using Error Plots
## Description
 Error plots are graphical representations of Rave CSA's data analysis on a variable or combination of variables in your study data. The data analysis displays in the form of frequency distribution plots, box-and-whisker plots, or scatter plots.
## Affected Roles
- Data managers 
- Central monitors 
- Medical monitors 
## Advantages
 You can use error plots to determine outliers, anomalies, risks, and trends in your study data. 
 Determining the outliers, anomalies, risks, and trends in your study data puts you and your life sciences team in an optimal position to take actions on your study, such as:
- cleaning data, 
- opening issues, 
- opening queries, 
- issuing corrective actions, 
- coordinating additional training, 
- and much more. 
## User Impact
 You can access error plots on the Error Plots page. To learn more, see  Data Anomaly Detection Plots .
