# Risk Management: Associate Critical Data Elements with Specific Data Points

## Risk Management: Associate Critical Data Elements with Specific Data Points
## What Is New?
 When adding and managing a Risk Assessment's critical data elements, you can now flag and associate specific study data points to the critical data elements. 
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Risk Manager 
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process. 
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications. 
## How Do I Benefit?
 Flagging critical data points helps your study team to proactively identify and monitor threats to data quality and integrity during the data review process.  
## Where Do I See the Change?
 You can add and manage data points associated with a critical data element in the Risk Management > Risk Assessment page > Critical Data page > Add Critical Data window or Edit Critical Data window. The Add Critical Data window and Edit Critical Data window now includes a Data Points section. In Data Points, you can select:
- Data Set   – The study data set containing specific data points associated with the critical data that you want to flag as critical to the study and track for risk. 
- Variable   – The variables associated with a critical data that you want to flag as critical to the study and track for risk.
 You can also view the number of data points associated with a critical data element on Risk Management > Risk Assessment page > Critical Data page > Data Points column.
