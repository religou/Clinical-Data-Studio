# CSM: Potential Duplicate Patients Analysis Accounts for Sites Without Recognizable Addresses

## CSM: Potential Duplicate Patients Analysis Accounts for Sites Without Recognizable Addresses
## What Is New?
 In previous releases, Potential Duplicate Patients could not run its analysis on sites whose addresses were not recognized by the address search plugin that Medidata uses . Therefore, those sites and associated patients were not accounted for in the overall potential duplicate patient analysis. Now, Potential Duplicate Patients runs its analysis sites without recognizable site addresses at the country level. In addition, t he Sites card reports the number of sites with and without recognizable site addresses.

 On the Summary - Sites table, if you click a site without an address, the Potential Duplicate Patients heatmap remains on the Global view and does not zoom to the Site view.
