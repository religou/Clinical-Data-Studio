# Risk Inventory Includes Study Risk Score

## Risk Inventory Includes Study Risk Score
## What Is New?
 Data managers can now view a study risk score that combines all active risk scores for the study.
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Data manager.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 You can now see all the risks in a trial in one score.
## Where Do I See the Change?
 Detect Landing Page > Risk Management > Risk Inventory > Study Risk Score 
## Where Do I Learn More?
 The Detect Knowledge Space provides new and updated content to support this feature/enhancement:
- Risk Inventory   
## What Else Should I Know?
 Important: The total study risk score is a summation of all the risk scores, where the risk status is not 'Closed'.
