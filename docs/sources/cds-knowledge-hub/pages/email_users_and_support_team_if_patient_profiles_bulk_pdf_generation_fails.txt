# Email Users And Support Team If Patient Profiles Bulk PDF Generation Fails

## Email Users And Support Team If Patient Profiles Bulk PDF Generation Fails
## Description
 For failed bulk patient profile design PDF exports, you receive an email displaying: 
- The number of patients who failed bulk PDF generation. 
- An attachment listing the corresponding patient IDs. 
 The Medidata Support Team also receives  this email for further action. 
## Affected Roles
 All. 
## Advantages
 With clear indication about patient IDs that failed bulk PDF generation, Patient Profile design reviewers can reach out to the Medidata Patient Profiles representative for further action. 
## User Impact
 Bulk patient profile PDF generation email now include patient IDs that fail to export. The email is in the following format:  
 The Patient Profiles PDF you requested on <date/time> has encountered an error. A notification has been sent to the Medidata Patient Profiles support team.   For further information, contact your Medidata Patient Profiles representative. 
 The list of patient IDs that failed to export to PDF is in the format :  List of PDF Failures <date & time> . 
## Special Considerations
 Processing times may vary depending on the number of patient profiles in your study. The minimum time taken is one hour.
