# View Patient-level Data for QTLs

## View Patient-level Data for QTLs
## What Is New?
 You can now preview QTL data and the number of patients that meet the QTL criteria by viewing patient-level data for all the configured QTLs.
- The Quality Tolerance Limits listing page now includes a radio button next to each QTL and a new QTL Patient Matches button, which allows you to retrieve patient-level data that matches the QTL configuration. 
- When you click the   QTL Patient Matches button, the QTL Patient Matches page shows, and lists the following: 
- Cumulative data: 
- Total Patients 
- Patient Matching  Numerator 
- Patient Matching Denominator 
- Patient-level data: 
- Patient ID 
- Site 
- Country 
- Numerator query 
- Denominator query. 
- The   QTL Patient Matches page is paginated, and shows by default up to 25 patient details per page. You can select up to 100 records per page. 
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Users who can view or manage QTL Configurations.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
- You can now perform enhanced Quality Assurance for QTL patient data. 
- Execute QTL logic Quality Assurance after the QTL is activated. 
## Where Do I See the Change?
 Detect Landing Page > Configuration and Reporting > QTL Configuration >  Quality Tolerance Limits  page > click QTL Patient Matches > QTL Patient Matches page.
