# RBQM: Subjects Updated To Patients In KRIs and QTLs

## RBQM: Subjects Updated To Patients In KRIs and QTLs
## What Is New?
 All instances of the term Subjects have been replaced by Patients in Clinical Data Studio - KRIs and  Clinical Data Studio - KRIs - QTLs.
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 All Clinical Data Studio users.
## Workflow Process
 No impact on the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact on other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 You can see a uniform usage of patient-centric terminology across Clinical Data Studio.
## Where Do I See the Change?
 All instances of Subjects have been replaced by Patients in the following pages:
- In Data Surveillance > Configuration > Key Risk Indicators 
- In Data Surveillance > Configuration > Quality Tolerance Limits 
## Where Do I Learn More?
 Use the following links to find updated content in support of the enhancement:
- KRI Configuration 
- QTL Configuration
