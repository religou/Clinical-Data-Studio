# RBQM: Redesigned Risk Documentation Interface Mirrors and Streamlines Risk Examination Workflow

## RBQM: Redesigned Risk Documentation Interface Mirrors and Streamlines Risk Examination Workflow
## What Is New?
 When you: 
- Create a new risk in the Risk Assessment > Add Risk window,  
- View or edit a risk in the Risk Assessment > Risk Review window, or 
- View or edit a risk in the Risk Inventory > Risk Review window 
 the Add Risk window and Risk window now function as subpages. The subpages show:
- A restyled, modernized user interface. 
- A new header section that includes: 
- The Add Risk or Risk Review subpage name. 
- The risk documentation completion status; that is, whether all required risk documentation fields are properly addressed. 
- The Save button. 
- The risk review status.
 Note : The risk review status only shows in the Risk Review subpage header section, and not in the Add Risk subpage.
- The Mark Reviewed button
 Note : The Mark Reviewed button only shows in the Risk Review subpage header section, and not in the Add Risk subpage.
- The Back ( < ) button . 
- On the Add Risk subpage, depending on the page you arrived from, you can click the Back ( < ) button return to the: 
- Critical Data page, 
- Critical Processes page, or 
- Categorization page. 
- On the Risk Review subpage, depending on the page you arrived from, you can click the Back ( < ) button return to the: 
- Critical Data page, 
- Critical Processes page, 
- Categorization page, or 
- Risk Inventory page. 
- Three tabs: Details, QTLs, KRIs. 
- The Risk Details tab is relabeled to Details. 
- The Add Risk subpage and Risk Review subpage no longer include the Risk Mitigations tab.
 Note : The functionality that enables you to add and manage risk mitigations now exists in the Details tab > Mitigations and Actions section. 
 On the Add Risk and Risk Review subpage, the Details tab layout is redesigned to include three modular sections:

## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Risk managers
## Workflow Process
 The Add Risk subpage and Risk Review subpage no longer include the Risk Mitigations tab. The functionality that enables you to add and manage risk mitigations now exists in the Details tab > Mitigations and Actions section. 
## Other Applications
 When adding and managing mitigations, you can associate mitigations with functional plans. When writing integrated data quality plans in the RBQM > Plan Documents capability, if you add a risk assessment table to a plan document, the risks table includes the Functional Plans Affected column, which autopopulates based on the entries made in the Mitigations and Plans > Functional Plans Affected field for each mitigation added to risks in the Add Risk and Risk Review subpages.
## How Do I Benefit?
 The Add Risk subpage and Risk Review subpage enhancements:
- Improve usability by intuitively mirroring RBQM's risk documentation interface design with the real world risk examination workflow. 
- Improve and modernize the general look and feel of the user interface. 
- Align the design style with the most current Medidata platform style. 
- Facilitate easier identification of functional plans impacted by risks, providing clearer links between risks and mitigation efforts for more effective responses.
