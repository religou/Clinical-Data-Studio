# Filter Review Reports By Countries, Sites, And Patients

## Filter Review Reports By Countries, Sites, And Patients
## Description
 You can now filter review reports by countries, and further by sites and patients.
## Affected Roles
 All.
## Advantages
 Filtering review reports enables data managers and site managers to view reports of interest specifically by countries, sites, or patients while running studies.
## User Impact
 On the Medidata Detect > Utilities > Review Reports page: for a review report if you click Column Filter and then select one or more countries from the list, the sites and patients lists update as per the countries and sites you select, respectively.
