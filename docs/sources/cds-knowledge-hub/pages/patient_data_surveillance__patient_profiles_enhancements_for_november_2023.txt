# Patient Data Surveillance: Patient Profiles Enhancements for November 2023

## Patient Data Surveillance: Patient Profiles Enhancements for November 2023
## What Is New?
 Patient Profiles includes the following enhancements in the November 2023 release:
- The page header section and body section on all Patient Profiles pages include stylistic updates to match the page design of all other Medidata Detect capability pages. 
- The Design page includes the Last Updated field, which shows the time and date in which the study data currently presented in Patient Profiles was last refreshed and rendered in the user interface. 
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 All Patient Profiles users.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
- The updated page header and body design:
- Promotes cross-capability consistency by aligning the Patient Profiles page design with the page design in all other Medidata Detect capabilities. 
- Modernizes the Patient Profiles page design with a sleeker look and feel. 
- The Last Updated timestamp:
- Clearly communicates when the most recent study data refresh in Patient Profiles occurred 
- Helps you determine whether or not study data refreshes are occurring at the expected frequency. 
## Where Do I See the Change?

## What Else Should I Know?
 Study data automatically refreshes in Patient Profiles daily at approximately 8:00 am (EST), and the data presented within the user interface is current as of 2:00pm (EST) the previous day.
