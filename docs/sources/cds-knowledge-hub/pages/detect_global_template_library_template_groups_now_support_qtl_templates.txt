# Detect Global Template Library Template Groups Now Support QTL Templates

## Detect Global Template Library Template Groups Now Support QTL Templates
## What Is New?
 You can save and store commonly used QTL configurations as templates in your organization's global template library. You can then import your QTL configurations to reuse when creating QTLs within a study. When editing templates groups, Template Group window now includes the QTL tab.
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Members of your organization who build studies can save and store QTL configurations as templates in your organization's template library.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process. 
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications. 
## How Do I Benefit?
 Adding and using specific QTLs as Detect templates:
- Enables you and your team to quickly and easily reapply common QTLs as needed across study configurations. 
- Promotes faster study startup time. 
- Ensures consistency and accuracy in QTLs details across studies. 
## Where Do I See the Change?
 In Configuration and Reporting, o n your organization's Template Library page, you can: 
- Click a Group Name from the list of templates to edit the Group Name or Description, and then click the QTL tab to view details of the QTL templates in the group. 
## Where Do I Learn More?
 Use the following links to find new and updated content in support of this enhancement:
- Detect Templates 
- View, Add, and Manage Template Groups 
- Add Listings, Custom KRIs, and QTLs as Templates to Template Groups 
## What Else Should I Know?
- The name of the template group and template name must be unique. 
- Avoid naming groups or wording descriptions in such a way that can unblind or compromise other studies or study users.
