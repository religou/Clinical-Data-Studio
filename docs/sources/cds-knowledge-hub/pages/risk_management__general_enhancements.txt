# Risk Assessment: General Enhancements

## Risk Assessment: General Enhancements
## What Is New?
- The Risk Review > Owner dropdown includes a list of users who have Risk Management specific access. 
- We introduce a search bar in the dropdown menu for selecting Critical Data or Critical Process. 
- We have made updates to the Risk Assessment report, removing redundant columns for Indicator Type and adding header for Mitigation title and description. 
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Risk Assessment users.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 These enhancements provide general quality of life improvements for users.
## Where Do I See the Change?
- The Owner dropdown updates can be found on the Risk Review page. 
- The Add Critical Data window and Add Critical Processes window now includes search functionality in the Critical Data field and Critical Process field, respectively.
