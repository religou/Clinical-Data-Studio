# Plan Documents: Plan Documents Updates Ensure Better User Experience

## Plan Documents: Plan Documents Updates Ensure Better User Experience
## What Is New?
 The RBQM > Plan Documents page includes updates that provide a cleaner user interface and enhance performance. The Updated By column on the page shows the name of the user who last updated the plan document.
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Data managers with either Manage Data Quality Plans or View Data Quality Plans building blocks.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 Data managers can identify plan documents of interest and work with greater efficiency to ensure compliance across teams.
## Where Do I See the Change?
 On the RBQM tab > Plan documents page.
## Where Do I Learn More?
 Use the following links to find new and updated content in support of the enhancement:

## What Else Should I Know?
 You need the View Data Quality Plans and Manage Data Quality Plans building block added to your Cloud Administration role to view and publish plan documents, respectively.
