# Track and Untrack Approved Listings for Clean Patient Data Analysis

## Track and Untrack Approved Listings for Clean Patient Data Analysis
## What Is New?
 You can activate up to ten data listings to track for analysis in Clean Patient Tracker . Clean Patient Tracker then consumes for each activated listing t he number of unreviewed individual data listing records  to visualize, list, and prioritize: 
- Outstanding study-level data cleaning task metrics 
- The sites in your study with the highest unclean patient counts 
- The specific data cleaning tasks with the highest count of unclean or unreviewed records 
- The most unclean patients in your study. 
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Data managers.
## Workflow Process
 You can use Clean Patient Tracker to track unvetted and unreviewed records associated with the data listings , and further drill down directly to clean those listings' records. 
## Other Applications
 Clean Patient Tracker .
## How Do I Benefit?
 Tracking listings in Clean Patient Tracker:
- Visualizes, lists, and prioritizes outstanding data cleaning tasks so you can focus on the most unclean data or areas of most importance in your study. 
- Facilitates faster cleaning of data. 
- Refreshes for an up-to-date view of data cleanliness. 
- Increases study team-level coordination and visibility of data cleanliness and outstanding data cleaning tasks. 
- Promotes cleaner, more fully vetted study data. 
## Where Do I See the Change?
 On the Data Listings page > Approved Listings table, the far right side of the table now includes the Track column. You click the deactivated Activation button, , for the corresponding listing you want Clean Patient Tracker to track. Similarly, t o deactivate listings from being tracked in Clean Patient Tracker, you can click the activated Activation button, .
