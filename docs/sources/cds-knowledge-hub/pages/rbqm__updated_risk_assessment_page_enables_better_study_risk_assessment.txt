# RBQM: Updated Risk Assessment Page Enables Better Study Risk Assessment

## RBQM: Updated Risk Assessment Page Enables Better Study Risk Assessment
## What Is New?
 You can now access the Assessment Details page from the Risk Assessment tab in the left hand pane in Medidata Clinical Data Studio > RBQM.
 The Risk Assessment page includes improvements that enable risk managers to better ascertain and assign risk factors specific to the study.
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Risk managers.
## Workflow Process
 No change to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 The improvements enable risk managers to better ascertain and assign risk factors specific to the study.
 Risk managers can view the protocol version associated with the risk assessment and plan the course of action to adopt to mitigate or minimize the risk.
## Where Do I See the Change?
 The RBQM > Risk Assessment > Details > Assessment Details page includes the following updates:
- The page title is now updated to Assessment Details.  
- The  page includes separate sections for assessment details and participants. 
- Save, Cancel, Add Risk Assessment, and Download Report buttons appear at the top right corner of the page. 
- You can now add the protocol version to the page which appears on a downloaded risk assessment report.
