# Manage Designs

## Manage Designs
 Medidata Patient Profiles includes the following capabilities relating to design management:
- Patient Profiles: Copy Patient Profile Designs Across Client Divisions 
- Data Surveillance: Patient Profile Refresh Building Block Allows Users to Refresh Published Designs 
- Data Surveillance: Identify and Review New and Updated Patient Profile Data in Published Designs 
- Data Surveillance: Patient Profiles Highlights Variables Containing Critical Data Points When Designing Profiles 
- Patient Profiles Does Not Permit Duplicate Design Names 
- Profile Designs Marked As Master Become Read-only 
- Design Management Patient Profiles 
- Enhanced Ability to Identify Data Sets and Variables in Your Design in Patient Profiles 
- Duplicate Designs to Other Study Environments Within the Same Study in Patient Profiles 
- Designate Designs as Blinded or Unblinded 
- Patient Profiles Cloud Indicates Whether a Design is Unblinded or Blinded 
- Study Environments Require Study Data to Create or Edit Designs 
- Copy Designs Between Studies 
- Patient Profile Designs No Longer Marked As Blinded 
- Publish Profile Designs as View Only or Master for Detect 
- Data Surveillance: Add Patient-level Internal Review Comments Within Patient Profile Designs
