# Resize Patient Selector Columns to Better View Variables

## Resize Patient Selector Columns to Better View Variables
## What Is New?
 You can click-and-drag the Drag Handle button, , in any Patient Selector column header to resize the column width.
 Tip : Click the video below to enlarge.
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 All Patient Profile users.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 Resizing Patient Selector column headers enables you to custom fit the table spacing to better view and interact with more variables on screen.
## Where Do I See the Change?
 Patient Profiles > Design page > Profile tabs > Patient Selector.
