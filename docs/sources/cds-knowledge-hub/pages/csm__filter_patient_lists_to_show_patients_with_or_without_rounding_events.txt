# CSM: Filter Patient Lists to Show Patients With or Without Rounding Events

## CSM: Filter Patient Lists to Show Patients With or Without Rounding Events
## What Is New?
 In Centralized Statistical Monitoring (CSM)'s Rounding & Propagation capability, when viewing the list of patients at a site with recorded events, you can filter the list to show:
- All patients at the site with recorded events. 
- Only patients at the site with rounded events. 
- Only patients at the site that do not have rounded events. 
- Only patients at the site with propagated events. 
- Only patients at the site that do not have propagated events. 
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Central monitors
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 Filtering patient lists to show patients with or without rounded or propagated records increases data transparency and improves your ability to understand and analyze your data.
## Where Do I See the Change?
 The Centralized Statistical Monitoring (CSM) > Data Veracity > Rounding & Propagation page includes the following updates:
- On the Rounding Events tab:
- When you click a Sites with Rounding heatmap cell, the Patients with Recorded Events dialog now includes a View menu, which enables you to filter the dialog to show:
- All Patient Records (default view) 
- Records Included in Count 
- Records Not Included in Count 
- When you click a link in the Rounding Events Summary table > # Affected Patients column, the Patients with Recorded Events dialog now includes a View menu, which enables you to filter the dialog to show:
- All Patients 
- Patient with Rounding Events (default view) 
- Patients without Rounding Events 
- On the Propagation Events tab:
- When you click a Sites with Propagation heatmap cell, the Patients with Record Event dialog now includes a View menu, which enables you to filter the dialog to show:
- All Patient Records (default view) 
- Records Included in Count 
- Records Not Included in Count 
- When you click a link in the Propagation Events Summary table > # Affected Patients column, the Patients with Recorded Events dialog now includes a View menu, which enables you to filter the dialog to show:
- All Patients 
- Patient with Propagation Events (default view) 
- Patients without Propagation Events
