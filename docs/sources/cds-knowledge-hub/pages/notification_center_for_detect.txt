# Notification Center for Detect

## Notification Center for Detect
## Description
 The Medidata Notification Center is now integrated with Detect to help deliver notifications to users. This release includes notifications for both scheduled and manual refreshes for Detect QTL, KRI, and CSM. Manual refreshes become available once the notification is sent, and scheduled refreshes appear after 7:00 a.m. EST on the day the refresh is requested. 
## Affected Roles
 All.
## Advantages
 You can now receive refresh notifications for all your studies in Detect, based on predefined templates.
## User Impact
 While there is no direct UI impact, the refresh notifications are sent through email, and also made available in the Medidata Notification Center.
