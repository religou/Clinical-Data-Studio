# Find the Precise, Comprehensive Risk Management User Assistance You Need

## Find the Precise, Comprehensive Risk Management User Assistance You Need
## What Is New?
 The Medidata Detect Knowledge Hub includes fully reorganized, rewritten, and improved Risk Management user assistance  including: 
- High-level, comprehensive information about Risk Management's purpose, benefits, data workflow, user workflow, and targeted user base. 
- The full scope of tasks and actions you can perform in Risk Management including: 
- Conceptual information explaining each task, when and why you would perform each task, and special considerations to help you optimally perform each task. 
- More detailed and accurate step-by-step instructions. 
- A redesigned table of contents with several new articles with titles that clearly and accurately depict the articles' contents. 
 Numerous locations within the Risk Management user interface also now include progressive user assistance (PUA) indicated by a clickable icon. 
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 All Medidata Detect users.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 The updated Risk Management user assistance allows  you to quickly, easily, and self-sufficiently: 
- Find the precise Risk Management user assistance you need. 
- Understand the purpose and benefits of Risk Management, the features it provides, and the full scope of actions you can perform. 
- Use Risk Management to its optimal potential. 
## Where Do I See the Change?
 You can find the improved Risk Management user assistance in the Medidata Detect Knowledge Hub > Risk Management section and in the Risk Management user interface.
