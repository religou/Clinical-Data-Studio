# Warning Messages to Help You Avoid Unintentional Data Loss

## Warning Messages to Help You Avoid Unintentional Data Loss
## Description
 Risk Management 2022.2.0 introduces warning messages when you add, edit, or delete a risk or a Critical Data, Critical Process, or Risk Categorization template. This helps prevent unintentional data loss if you change your mind or select the X , Cancel , or Delete options by mistake. 
## Affected Roles
 All.
## Advantages
 If you click the X , Cancel , or Delete buttons in error, this could lead to unintentional data loss. The addition of a warning message helps you avoid the accidental click of X , Cancel , or Delete. 
## User Impact
 Warning messages appear when there is a risk of unintentional data loss. The messages alert you to cancel or confirm an action. The warning messages appear when you: 
- Create, edit, or delete a risk or a risk category associated with a study. 
- Navigate between the Add New Risk category and Select Risk category tabs when adding a risk category. 
- Delete or modify an existing risk associated with Critical Data, Critical Processes, Risk Categorizations, or Risk Categorization templates. 
- Click Cancel while creating or editing Critical Data, Critical Processes, or Risk Categorization templates. Clicking Cancel closes the page, and data entered in the template is lost. 
- Update the Add Review frequency when adding a new risk to a Critical Data, Critical process, or Risk Categorization template. 
- Update the Target Review Date and Review Status when reviewing or editing a risk associated to a Critical Data, Critical process, or Risk Categorization template. 
- Update the Last Reviewed by and Mark Reviewed fields when reviewing a risk associated to a Critical Data, Critical process, or Risk Categorization template. 
- Add Review Status, Frequency, and Review Dates in the Risk Inventory. 
- Update the Risk Status to Closed. You can no longer edit other fields on the Risk Review form. 
- Close or transfer a risk.
