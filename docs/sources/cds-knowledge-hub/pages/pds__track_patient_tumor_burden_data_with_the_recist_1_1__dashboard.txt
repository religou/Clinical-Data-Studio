# PDS: Track Patient Tumor Burden Data with The RECIST 1.1 Dashboard

## PDS: Track Patient Tumor Burden Data with The RECIST 1.1 Dashboard
## What Is New?
 Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST) v1.1 is the standard criteria used to evaluate treatment outcomes of solid tumors in oncology studies. For more details on RECIST 1.1, see  RECIST 1.1 . Patient Data Surveillance (PDS) includes an interactive   RECIST 1.1 dashboard that visualizes, classifies, and lists the tumor burden change for patients in Oncology studies. You can use the RECIST 1.1 dashboard to: 
- Track tumor burden change for all patients for a specific scan visit.
- Track tumor burden for individual patients in a study. 
- Compare tumor response measurements with site entered response details. 
- View visualizations of tumor burden, tumor burden response, and scan visit information. 
- Identify discrepancies in tumor measurements.  
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Study Managers, Medical Monitors, and Data Managers.
 All Detect users with View Oncology Analytics and Detect Oncology Reviewer access.
## Workflow Process
 RECIST 1.1 consumes study data from Rave EDC, visualizes and lists: 
- Tumor burden percentage change at specific visits, and over time. 
- A summary count of total patients, and patients in Complete Response (CR), Stable Disease (SD), Partial Response (PR), Progressive Disease (PD), and Not Evaluable /Not Applicable (NE/NA|) stages in a study. 
- Tumor burden response by patient per scan visit type. 
## Other Applications
 The RECIST 1.1 dashboard works only if you configure data sets and variables in Medidata Detect > Configuration and Reporting > Detect Mapping > Oncology Mapping > RECIST. Source and target variables you configure in Oncology Mapping populate the RECIST 1.1 dashboard and the tumor burden response by patient table. For more information, see  Configuration and Reporting: Map Data Set and Variables for the RECIST 1.1 Dashboard .
## How Do I Benefit?
 RECIST 1.1:
- Quantifies tumor burden and outcome calculations for patients. 
- Summarizes the total count of patients, and those in various stages of treatment response at the selected scan visit type and site. 
- Tracks tumor burden over a period of time and identifies discrepant records. 
- Assists data managers in assessing the accuracy of RECIST 1.1 calculations and safety monitoring.

 Tumor Burden Response By Patient  
 The tumor burden response table includes study data that RECIST 1.1 ingests from Rave EDC and tumor burden responses calculated by the built-in algorithm. Each record represents a patient at a scan visit. The tumor burden response by patient table updates data as per the scan visit type and site selected. 
 The tumor burden response table includes the following fields. 
- Patient ID  
- Site  
- Country  
- Status  
- Visit Name  
- Visit Date 
- Overall Response  
- Smallest Sum of Diameter   
- Sum of Diameters (SLD) 
- Tumor % Change  
- TL Response  
- NTL Response   
- NL Response  
- Calculated Overall Response   
- Calculated TL Response  
- Calculated NTL Response  
- Calculated NL Response  
 Records with potential discrepancies between site entered and algorithm calculated overall tumor response, show , a warning icon in the Status column. You can mark a record as reviewed or unreviewed. Once you mark a record as reviewed, the Status column shows a icon. The algorithm calculated tumor response values appear in millimeters (mm). Along with Patient ID, Site, and Country, you can also filter records by Status.
