# Patient Profiles: Quality of Life Enhancements

## Patient Profiles: Quality of Life Enhancements
## What Is New?
 Patient Profiles includes the following quality of life enhancements:
- When working with published designs, the Refresh button becomes inactive if the current data shown in the design is from the most recent data refresh or if a refresh is in progress. 
- The Patient Profile Designs page > Published Designs table now includes a Last Refresh Date column, which shows the timestamp of when the last refresh was performed for each published design. 
- When downloading patient profiles, you can now export the patient profile comment history. 
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Patient profile designers.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 The quality of life enhancements provide the following benefits:
- Disabling the Refresh button when the design contains the most up to date data or when a refresh is in progress prevents you from needlessly initiating a new data refresh. 
- Seeing a profile design's last refresh date on the Patient Profiles Designs page > Published Designs table clearly indicates the freshness of the data shown in each profile design. 
- Patient profile exports enable you to include comment history details in reports. 
## Where Do I See the Change?
 You can find:
- The disabled Refresh button when working within published design on the upper-right corner of the page. 
- The Last Refresh Date column on the Patient Profiles Designs page > Published Designs table. 
- When working within a published design, when you click the Download button or the Download Profile button, the Download Profiles window > Download Option menu now includes the Comments option. When you select the Comments option, the download report includes the comment history.

## What Else Should I Know?
- The last refresh date is only available for published designs and not draft designs since you cannot refresh draft designs. 
- Comment timestamps in the Comments pane show in your local timezone; however, the comment timestamp in downloaded patient profile PDF files show in GMT/UTC.
