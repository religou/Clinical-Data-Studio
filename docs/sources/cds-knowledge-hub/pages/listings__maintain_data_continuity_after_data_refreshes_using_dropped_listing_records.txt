 Change Type

 Functionality or Module 
 | Listings
 Summary of Changes 
 | 
 As highlighted in the Release Newsletter : 
 Listings now includes a dropped status for records which get dropped after a data refresh as they no longer meet the criteria configured in the listing's conditional expression. 
 when a listing is refreshed, records that no longer meet the filter criteria (due to data updates) are automatically removed from the view. This results in the loss of visibility for records that may have already been reviewed or contain comments. To ensure data continuity by retaining "dropped" records in the listing if they possess historical review data or user comments, regardless of whether they still satisfy the primary filter expression. 
 Benefit to Users 
 | Data managers can track records that were dropped after refreshing listings, and have insight into historical reviews or user comments associated with those records. This enables data managers to have a complete view into study data. 
 Summary of User Impact 
 | 
 Impact to Users and Roles 
 Data managers.
 Impact to Workflow Processes 
 No impact to the workflow process.
 Impact to Applications 
 No impact to applications.
 Impact on Interface 
 On the Data Surveillance > Listings pages, records marked as reviewed, or with comments, that do not meet the listing criteria after a data refresh, appear disabled and with a Dropped status.
 Impact to Existing Data or Settings
