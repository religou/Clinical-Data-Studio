# Identify and Track Study Risks Using the Key Risk Indicators Table

## Identify and Track Study Risks Using the Key Risk Indicators Table
## Description
 You can view the key risk indicators (KRIs) being tracked in your study and their respective KRI values at each site or country from the Key Risk Indicators table. 
 The Key Risk Indicators table allows you to: 
- See all KRIs being tracked in your study and their respective KRI values at each site and country. 
- Determine which site's or country's KRI values are outliers and non-outliers. 
- Determine which   site's or country's   KRIs have surpassed the risk threshold. 
 Based on your observations made from the Key Risk Indicator table, you can determine: 
- The issues or risks that require further investigate or mitigation.  
- The best approach or mitigating actions to take on the issues or risks. 
- The effect the mitigating actions have made on the issues or risks over time. 
## Affected Roles
 Anyone who is assigned to track risks can use the Key Risk Indicators table. For example: 
- Data Quality  (Site, Country, or Data Manager)  – Might  look at abnormal trends in data, adherence to data entry instructions, or proactive discrepancy management.   For example, the manager might examine the number of queries and query response time. 
- Safety  (Medical Monitor)  –   Might be interested in the processing of safety information, non-serious adverse events (AEs), and serious adverse events (SAEs). 
- Subject  (Study, Country Managers)  – Might  refer to outliers in screen failure rate or trends in ratio of subjects. 
## Advantages
 The Key Risk Indicators table: 
- Provides a clear and consistent method of tracking and calculating known and anticipated risks to study data quality. 
- Allows you to track, compare, and determine how risks are evolving at the  study, site, and country levels. 
- Allows for rapid detection of possible issues or risks that may require mitigating actions. 
- Helps you determine any necessary mitigating actions to take on risks. 
- Allows you to see if areas of risk have improved over time after taking mitigating actions. 
- Helps ensure the study conduct operates as planned. 
- Improves decision making, data quality, and good clinical practice (GCP) compliance. 
## User Impact
 You can access the Key Risk Indicators table on the KRIs page. To learn more, see  KRIs .
## Special Considerations
 Medidata Detect does not perform site-level or country-level KRI analysis for studies with fewer than three sites or three countries, respectively.
