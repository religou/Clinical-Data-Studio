# Detect Global Template Library and Template Groups

## Detect Global Template Library and Template Groups
## What Is New?
 You can save and store commonly used listing , custom numeric KRI, or custom percentage KRI configurations as templates in your organization's global template library. You can then import your listing and KRI templates to reuse when creating new listings or KRI configurations within a study. 
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Members of your organization who build studies can save and store listing and custom KRI configurations as templates in your organization's template library.
 Members of your organization who review data; for example, study managers or data managers, can import and reuse listing and KRI configuration templates in studies.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 Adding and using specific listings and custom KRIs as Detect templates:
- Enables you and your team to quickly and easily reapply common listings and custom KRIs as needed across study configurations. 
- Promotes faster study startup time. 
- Ensures consistency and accuracy in listing and custom KRI details across studies. 
## Where Do I See the Change?
 Tip : Click the images below to enlarge.
 The Detect Landing Page > Configuration and Reporting card now includes a Detect Templates link. 
 Your organization has its own client division (CD) -level global Template Library page in Detect's Configuration and Reporting module. The template library stores and lists the collection of your organization's template groups and associated templates.
 On your organization's Template Library page, you can:
- Click a Group Name from the list of templates to edit the Group Name or Description or view details of the Listing or KRI templates in the group. 
- Click New Template Group and use the Template Group window to create template groups in the template library.

## What Else Should I Know?
- The name of the template group and template name must be unique. 
- Avoid naming groups or wording descriptions in such a way that can unblind or compromise other studies or study users.
