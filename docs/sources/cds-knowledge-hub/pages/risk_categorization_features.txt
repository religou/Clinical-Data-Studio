# Risk Categorization Features

## Risk Categorization Features
 Risk Management includes the following risk categorization-related capabilities:
- Risk Review Detailed Page Improvements 
- Flexible Risk Categories and Risk Category Templates 
- Multiple Key Risk Indicators for a Risk 
- Multiple Quality Tolerance Limits for a Risk 
- New Required QTL Fields 
- New Risk Category Added for Diversity 
- Risk Categorization and Risk Scoring 
- Warning Messages to Help You Avoid Unintentional Data Loss 
- Add Custom Risk Categories to Templates and Associated Studies
