 Summary of Changes 
 | 
 As highlighted in the Release Newsletter : 
 While conducting studies, Lead Clinical Data Managers (LCDMs) and Clinical Data Team Leads (CDTLs) may need to navigate through multiple systems to gather critical operational data needed to manage their studies effectively. This leads to: 
- Lack of complete visibility – It is time-consuming to get a complete view of Clinical Data Studio (CDS) operational activities, and user work compliance without manually consolidating data from various sources. 
- Increased risk and inefficiency –The necessity to switch between capabilities to check on statuses and risk, introduces unnecessary friction, increasing the time spent on oversight, and potentially delays identifying critical issues. 
- Delayed decision-making – Without centralized metrics, lead data managers struggle to quickly answer fundamental questions about work progress, delaying timely interventions and increasing operational risk for the sponsor. 
 To overcome this, Clinical Data Studio now includes a Data Oversight dashboard.
 The Data Oversight dashboard serves as a singular place to understand key operational activities like set up, data import, refresh, and data review as documented in Data Connect and Data Surveillance.  
 Benefit to Users 
 | Data Oversight provides:
- Complete operational transparency – Lead Clinical Data Managers (LCDMs) and Clinical Data Team Leads (CDTLs) can access critical operational metrics and user work compliance from a single page. 
- Flexibility to select key operational metrics – You can select metrics of interest to display on the Data Oversight dashboard, such as:
- Metrics covering reviewed patient profiles, and last refresh date. 
- Visibility into listings created, last refresh date, and records reviewed/not reviewed per listing. 
- Tracking unreviewed AI Reconciliation data per report. 
- Tracking reviewed vendor queries per query set. 
- Reduced friction and risk – By centralizing information, Data Oversight avoids the need to switch between multiple applications, bringing critical information directly into one page.

 Data Oversight reads data from the following modules based on their latest refresh data snapshot.
- Patient Profiles. 
- AI Reconciliation. 
- Listings. 
- Vendor Queries, and 
- Data Connect. 
 Updates made to records, such as marking listings as reviewed or unreviewed, or similar actions in any of the  modules, reflects in the metrics shown on the Data Oversight dashboard instantly.
