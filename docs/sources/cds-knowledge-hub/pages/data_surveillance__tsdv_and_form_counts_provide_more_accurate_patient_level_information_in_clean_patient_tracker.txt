# Data Surveillance: TSDV and Form Counts Show Information at Patient Level In Clean Patient Tracker

## Data Surveillance: TSDV and Form Counts Show Information at Patient Level In Clean Patient Tracker
## What Is New?
 Clean Patient Tracker now includes TSDV/SDV and form counts information at the patient level at a site.
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Data Managers.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 You can ensure the data integrity and accuracy of Rave data ingested into Clean Patient Tracker.   Data managers can obtain the complete patient level information at a site and streamline the data tracking process for the patient. 
## Where Do I See the Change?
 Data Surveillance > Clean Patient Tracker, includes the following information for patients at a site:
- Incomplete   –   Sum of form statuses where form is incomplete. 
- Non conformant   – Sum of form statuses where form is non conformant. 
- Requires freeze   – Sum of form statuses where form requires freeze. 
- Requires lock   – Sum of form statuses where form requires lock. 
- Requires review   – Sum of form statuses where form require review. 
- Requires signature  – Sum of form statues where form requires signature. 
- Requires TSDV/ SDV   –   Sum of all TSDV/SDV required for patients at a particular site.
