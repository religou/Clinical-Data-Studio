# RBQM: Identify Potential Study Data Issues Using Signals

## RBQM: Identify Potential Study Data Issues Using Signals
## What Is New?
 In the centralized monitoring process, a signal is an  alert or indication of a potential problem, risk, or deviation in a clinical study.   Signals get  detected remotely  by analyzing study data centrally, and allow proactively identifying potential risks in the study data.  Signals help sponsors  focus resources  on sites or issues needing immediate attention, improving data integrity and patient safety.
In short,  
- A  signal is a potential issue identified through data analysis. 
- An issue is a confirmed finding, the result of a signal validated through central monitor review, site discussion, or site visit.  
 RBQM includes a new Signals feature that you can use to build automated and manual signals. Each signal is an observation that meets a set threshold, and derives data from a module you configure for signal generation. 
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Central Monitors and Central Statistical Monitors, Clinical Research Associates, and users with View Signals and Manage Signals building blocks.
## Workflow Process
 Study data automatically refreshes daily at approximately 6:00 AM (EST). When you click Refresh on the Signals page, signals generate for configured modules, based on the source module's last successful data update, and the set threshold for each module. On the Signals page, each configured module shows the last snapshot date and time from its source module. This means you can determine the exact version of the signals data in each module based on its snapshot date. 
 While configuring signals for individual modules, you can set up thresholds based on which automated signals get created. You must also choose the modules you want the signals to report on. For example, if you do not enable KRIs in the Signals Configuration, and add custom configurations, no signals get created for any KRI in your study when the signals data refreshes. Once you configure a signal, observations appear as signals based on custom configurations and the latest data snapshot for individual modules when you next refresh data. Custom configuration changes made after 6:00 AM EST do not show in the automated signals report until the next day.  
## Other Applications
 Signals read data from the following capabilities:
- Patient Profiles. 
- KRIs. 
- QTLs. 
- Rounding. 
- Propagation. 
- Potential Duplicate Patients. 
- Under/over Reporting. 
## How Do I Benefit?
 With automated signals, Central Monitors can aggregate and track signals, create and assign actions for signals, and investigate signals of interest. You can monitor values that appear outside the set threshold and investigate observations.  Consolidating signals from various analyses into a single, centralized report: 
- Streamlines the identification, tracking, and management of data quality issues across study data. 
- Allows for continuous monitoring and ensures that potential risks get mitigated before they escalate as issues. 
- Enhances efficiency, reduces manual effort, and improves decision-making for users involved in central monitoring and central statistical monitoring. 
- Provides clear, actionable insights, ensuring that critical data quality issues are identified and addressed promptly.

## What Else Should I Know?
- You can perform the following signals report actions only if you have the View Signals building block added to your role in Cloud administration:
- View the Signals Report. 
- Add, update, assign actions.  
- Resolve Signals. 
- View Signals Audit Report. 
- With the Manage Signals building block, in addition to performing all the above actions, you can create, assign, dismiss, watch, and close signals. 
- You can refresh data only once for the automated signals. If an  observation falls below the configured threshold, it does not update when you refresh data. The last value which met the threshold was met is shown as the signal observation. 
- You cannot generate or configure signals for Rounding and Propagation, if pivot is turned on under RBQM > Configuration > Data Mapping > Rounding and Propagation for the data set. 
- Custom configuration changes made after 6:00 AM EST do not show in the automated signals report until the next day.  
- For Statistical Monitoring modules such as Rounding, Propagation, Duplicate Patients, and Under/over Reporting, if the Signals workflow encounters an error with any one module, the automated signals report for that module shows observations misaligned with those shown on the module's dashboard. On the signals report, the Module Snapshot still reflects the latest snapshot data from that module. For example, if Rounding has an error, then the signals for Rounding may not align with the data shown on the RBQM > Statistical Monitoring > Rounding and Propagation > Rounding tab.
