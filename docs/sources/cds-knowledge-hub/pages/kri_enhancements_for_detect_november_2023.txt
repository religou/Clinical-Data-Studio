# KRI Enhancements for Detect November 2023

## KRI Enhancements for Detect November 2023
## What Is New?
 Key Risk Indicators includes the following enhancements:
- On the Key Risk Indicators > KRI Dashboard:
- Country names that extend beyond the Country column width are abbreviated with ellipses. 
- When you select the Site option or Country option from the View By menu, and then you click the Side by Side View button, then in the Side by Side View panel, site and country names that extend beyond the column width are abbreviated with ellipses. 
- On the Key Risk Indicators > KRI Scheduler, t he browser tab name now shows KRI Scheduler instead of KRI Dashboard for users with View KRI Dashboard permissions. 
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Users with permissions to view the KRI Dashboard.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 The abbreviated site and country names gives the KRI Dashboard a cleaner, more usable look and feel. The updated KRI Scheduler browser tab name helps you better navigate and orient yourself while working within Detect.
