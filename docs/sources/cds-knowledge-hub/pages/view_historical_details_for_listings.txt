# View Historical Details For Listings

## View Historical Details For Listings
## Description
 When you create a listing, Data Reviewer takes a snapshot of the data in the listing. You can view the history and refresh the listing manually to get the most recent search results.
## Affected Roles
 All.
## Advantages
 You can view listing results at a specific snapshot date and time.
## User Impact
 On the Data Reviewer > Data Reviewer page > Listings tab, you can:
- Click a saved listing and then click   Refresh   to refresh the listing with the most recent search results. 
- Click the   Show History   link to view a side panel showing:
- The list of historical refreshes made to listing. 
- The Last Updated Date details. 
- The Last Updated By details. 
 Within the Show History side panel, you can click a listing refresh date to view the listing snapshot as recorded on that refresh date.
