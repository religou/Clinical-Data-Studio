# Last Action Taken Date Excludes Future Dates

## Last Action Taken Date Excludes Future Dates
## What Is New?
 The Last Action Taken Date field calendar for any risk no longer shows future dates. Instead you can select either a past date or today's date. Future dates appear disabled in the calender.
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Study users with the Customize Study Components building block access.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 Study team members can ensure future dates are not selected for the last action taken field, thus keeping the study data devoid of any error and enhancing accuracy.
## Where Do I See the Change?
 In RBQM > Risks > on the Risk Inventory page, if you click a risk ID, and then click Action Taken under the Mitigation and Actions section, a Add Action slide out panel appears. If you click the Action Taken Date, a calendar appears which shows future dates as disabled. You can select any date up to today's date.
