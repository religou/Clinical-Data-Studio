# Chinese Characters Display In Patient Profile Reports and PDFs

## Chinese Characters Display In Patient Profile Reports and PDFs
## Description
 Chinese characters appear in profiles reports and PDFs you generate for profile designs with elements or variables created using Chinese fonts.
## Affected Roles
 All.
## Advantages
 Chinese characters that you enter in Patient Profiles display throughout the user interface and in the profile exports. 
## User Impact
 On the Medidata Detect > Patient Data Surveillance (PDS) > Patient Profiles > Patient Profile Designs > Elements tab, if you use Chinese characters while working with elements or variables, Chinese characters appear in the profile reports and the PDF exports you generate on the Profiles tab.
