# CSM: Potential Duplicate Patients Export Updates

## CSM: Potential Duplicate Patients Export Updates
## What Is New?
 Potential Duplicate Patients includes the following export-related updates:
- The Export button is relabeled to Download. 
- The download of sites with potential duplicate patients includes sites without addresses. 
- Embedded text beneath the Download button states: "Download a list of Potential Duplicates, including those not mapped." 
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Central monitors
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 Relabeling the Export button to Download unifies the Potential Duplicate Patients page with the terminology used elsewhere through Medidata Detect.
## Where Do I See the Change?
 Centralized Statistical Monitoring > Data Veracity > Potential Duplicate Patients > Download button.
