## QTL Patient and Time Trend Graphs More Clearly Deliniate Excursions
## Description
 On the QTL Dashboard > Patient tab and the Time tab, when the trend graph's observed value is an excursion but it happens to overlap with the expected value, the observed value displays in red.
## Affected Roles
- Study managers 
- Medical monitors 
## Advantages
 Ensuring that excursions persistently appear in red improves the trend graph's usability and readability.
## User Impact
 You do not need to zoom in on the graph to more clearly determine when a value is an excursion.
