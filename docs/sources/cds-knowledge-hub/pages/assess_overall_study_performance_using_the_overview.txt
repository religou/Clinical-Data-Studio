# Assess Overall Study Performance Using the Overview

## Assess Overall Study Performance Using the Overview
## Description
 Medidata Detect now includes the Overview page. The Overview is the initial landing page when you launch Medidata Detect . The Overview provides Medidata Detect's summarized analysis of your study data by reporting: 
- An overall study performance assessment based on an  A-F grading scale . 
- The percentage of discrepant data in your study data at the overall study level. 
- The average discrepancy score for all data points in the study. 
- The most discrepant, high-risk individual study factors at the following perspectives: 
- Risk Categories 
- Domains 
- Countries 
- Sites 
 The Overview serves as a clear starting point in your study data review process by:
- Reporting the overall health and level of discrepant data in the study. 
- Presenting the worst to best performing aspects within each data perspective based on percentage of discrepant data. 
- Providing guided navigation, which allows you to easily drill down and investigate the aspects and specific data most requiring your attention. 
## Affected Roles
 All Medidata Detect users use the Overview. Based on your role in a study, you may want to focus on specific data perspectives or aspects within a perspective. 
## Advantages
 The Overview: 
- Provides a clear and natural starting point in your study data review process. 
- Navigates you on the optimal path to find and investigate the data that's most important and relevant to you. 
- Provides quick and easy access to the error plots. 
- Ensures you are always clearly oriented within Medidata Detect . 
- Expedites the data review process. 
- Leads to higher-quality study data. 
## User Impact
 The Overview is the initial landing page when you launch Medidata Detect .   You can  navigate to the Overview page from any other Medidata Detect page. To learn more, see   Overview . 
## Special Considerations
 None.
