## Change Details
 Product 
 | Clinical Data Studio
 Version 
 | 2026.1.0
 Change Type 
 | Enhancement (Updated Functionality)
 Functionality or Module 
 | Statistical Monitoring
 Summary of Changes 
 | 
 AI Reconciliation and Anomalies pages now include AI assistant Dot label and marks AI-generated data with the label Dot Generated.
 Benefit to Users 
 | Data managers can differentiate between system-calculated and AI-generated data metrics in the study and act to maintain data integrity. 
 Summary of User Impact 
 | 
 Impact to Users and Roles 
 Data managers.
 Impact to Workflow Processes 
 No impact to workflow processes.
 Impact to Applications 
 No impact to other applications.
 Impact on Interface 
 On the RBQM > Statistical Monitoring > Anomalies page, a blue dot indicates the Medidata AI Assistant Dot. The bottom of the page also shows the Dot generated label for AI generated data.
 On the Data Surveillance > AI Reconciliation Reports page, the Dot label also appears for individual reports:
 Impact to Existing Data or Settings 
 No existing data changes; no configuration or permission updates required.

 | High
