## Insights: Downloaded Open Query Records Report Includes Data Snapshot Timestamp
## What Is New?
 The Open Queries download file now includes a timestamp showing the date and time of the data snapshot represented in the downloaded list of records. The data snapshot timestamp is formatted as YYYYMMDDTHHMMSSZ5, where:
- YYYY – Year 
- MM – Month 
- DD – DD 
- T – Represents the word time. 
- HH – Hour 
- MM – Minute 
- SS – Second 
- Z5 – Eastern Time Zone 
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Data managers. 
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process. 
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications. 
## How Do I Benefit?
 The data snapshot timestamp:
- Tells you how recent the data is for the records listed in the downloaded file. 
- Enables you to compare records from other Open Queries downloads with different data snapshot timestamps. 
## Where Do I See the Change?
 You can find the timestamp when you go to Insights > Open Queries page > Open Query Records table, and then click the Download button and open the Open Queries .CSV file. The timestamp shows in Column B, Row 1.
