# Configuration and Reporting: Preview the Number of Patients That Exceed a QTL or Percentage KRI Risk Threshold

## Configuration and Reporting: Preview the Number of Patients That Exceed a QTL or Percentage KRI Risk Threshold
## What Is New?
 When you hover your cursor on an active or inactive QTL or percentage KRI, you can preview: 
- The number of patients that meet the criteria for all active and inactive QTLs or percentage KRIs over the number of patients the QTL or KRI is calculated against. 
- The QTL or KRI description.  
 Note : For numeric KRIs, you can only see the KRI description.  
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Detect Configuration Manager Administrators. 
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 You can validate whether or not KRI and QTL configurations are set up correctly before activating. 
## Where Do I See the Change?
 On the:
- Configuration and Reporting > QTL Configuration > Quality Tolerance Limits page > Parameter column when you hover your cursor on a Parameter name. 
- Configuration and Reporting > KRI Configuration > Key Risk Indicators page > KRI Configurations tab > Name column when you hover your cursor on a percentage KRI name.
