# View Patient-level Data for KRIs

## View Patient-level Data for KRIs
## What Is New?
 You can now preview KRI data and the number of patients that meet the KRI criteria by viewing patient-level data for all the configured KRIs.
- The Key Risk Indicators listing page now includes a radio button next to each KRI and a new KRI Patient Matches button, which allows to retrieve patient-level data that matches the KRI configuration.
- When you click the KRI Patient Matches button, the KRI Patient Matches page shows, and lists the following: 
- Cumulative data: 
- Total Patients 
- Patient Matching Numerator 
- Patient Matching Denominator 
- Patient-level data: 
- Patient ID 
- Site 
- Country 
- Numerator query 
- Denominator query. 
- The KRI Patient Matches page is paginated, and shows by default up to 25 patient details per page. You can select up to 100 records per page. 
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Users who can view or manage KRI Configurations.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
- You can now perform enhanced Quality Assurance for KRI patient data. 
- Execute KRI logic Quality Assurance after the KRI is activated. 
 Where Do I See the Change? 
 Detect Landing Page > Configuration and Reporting > KRI Configuration > Key Risk Indicators page > click KRI Patient Matches > KRI Patient Matches page
