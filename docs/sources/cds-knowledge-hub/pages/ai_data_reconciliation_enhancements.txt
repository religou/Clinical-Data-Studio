# AI Data Reconciliation Enhancements

## AI Data Reconciliation Enhancements
## What Is New?
 Increased AI Data Reconciliation usability of existing features: 
- All reports within AI Data Reconciliation appear default sorted with confidence from high to low. 
- Thumbs down and thumbs up buttons added next to the confidence column in the data grid for AI Data Reconciliation reports 
- A clock icon to link to the Audit Viewer to provide an audit of actions, for example, who is linking the records, thumbs up and thumbs down, and so on.  
- Ability to export an AI Data Reconciliation Report, so that it can be reviewed it with the site monitor, and the site monitor can review it with the site 
- An Action By column on the Rave Insights that displays the user name, so that you know who performed the Rave . 
- Removed linking from the AI Data Reconciliation slide out pages to Overlapping AE/CM reports. 
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Data managers.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 A streamlined Data Reconciliation process.
## Where Do I See the Change?
- Under the Confidence column on the AI Data Reconciliation page, see the Confidence ranking and the Thumbs Up and Thumbs Down button. 
- Next to the title of the AI Data Reconciliation page, see the clock icon for accessing the Audit Viewer. 
- The Download button on the Data Reconciliation page. 
- The Action By column on the Query History page (AI Data Reconciliation page > click Rave Query under the Status column for a record). 
- Removed linking from the AI Data Reconciliation slide out pages to Overlapping AE/CM reports.
