# Signals:Track Signal Action Progress Using the Actions Tab

## Signals:Track Signal Action Progress Using the Actions Tab
## What Is New?
 Actions are steps central monitors take in investigating signals in the study. You can now receive a notification when you are assigned actions and signals. The Signals page includes an Actions tab where you can track the progress for all actions assigned to team members for signals in your study.
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
- Central Monitors and Central Statistical Monitors, Clinical Research Associates. 
- Users with View Signals and Manage Signals building blocks. 
## Workflow Process
 No impact to workflow process.
## Other Applications
 Signals read data from the following capabilities:
- Patient Profiles. 
- KRIs. 
- QTLs. 
- Rounding. 
- Propagation. 
- Potential Duplicate Patients. 
- Under/over Reporting. 
## How Do I Benefit?
 Clinical research associates no longer need to individually review each signal to find assigned actions. The Actions tab groups all the actions into a single list, providing a complete set of actions assigned in a study. This enables faster accessibility, reducing time taken to complete oversight, making reconciliation and progress tracking smother, provides a clear view of the workload across study team members. It also enables better management at high-incidence sites and prevents duplication of effort if actions already exist for signals.
