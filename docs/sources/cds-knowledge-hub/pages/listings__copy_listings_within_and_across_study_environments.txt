# Listings: Copy Listings Within And Across Study Environments

## Listings: Copy Listings Within And Across Study Environments
## What Is New?
 Data managers with the appropriate access can now copy listings within a study or across multiple studies.
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
- Data managers 
- All users with the Manage Data Reviewer building block. 
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 Data managers can use existing listings for anomaly detection, by either reusing or duplicating listings. They can save time and effort without the need to build listings from scratch, maintain consistency, and streamline their anomaly detection processes and enhance efficiency.
## Where Do I See the Change?
 On the Data Surveillance tab > Listings page > Approved or Draft Listings tab, you can click  Manage Listings . If you then select listings, and click Copy from above the listings table, you can then copy the listings across clients, study groups, studies, and study environments.
 You cannot approve a copied draft listing, until you preview and save the listing expression. You can further edit and validate the expression for a copied listing. A warning symbol appears next to copied listings indicating that you must preview and save the listing first.
