# Configure How Detect Determines and Signifies a Site's Level of Risk

## Configure How Detect Determines and Signifies a Site's Level of Risk
## Description
 The KRI Dashboard now visually signifies sites that Detect assesses as being medium or high risk . Detect Configuration Manager's KRI Configuration enables you to configure:
- The level of weight (low, medium, or high) that a particular KRI risk category carries when Detect calculates the KRI's value and a site's overall level of risk. 
- Which specific KRIs you want Detect to factor in when calculating each site's risk level. 
## Affected Roles
 Detect Configuration Manager Admin
## Advantages
 You can fully customize the level of weight that you want Detect to apply to a KRI's risk category when calculating the KRI's value. This helps you more precisely determine at-risk sites based on your study goals and protocol. You can also quickly and easily customize which KRIs you want Detect to factor in when calculating each site's risk level.
## User Impact
 KRI Configuration now contains two tabs: KRI Configurations and Risk Category Weight .
 On the KRI Configurations tab, the Key Risk Indicators list contains a new Add to SRI column. The column contains a toggle button that allows you to enable or disable Detect from factoring that KRI into Detect's Site Risk Indicator analysis.
 On the Risk Category Weight tab, you can customize and configure the level of risk (low, medium, or high) that is associated to specific risk categories .

## Special Considerations
 On the KRI Configurations tab, all system KRIs assigned the Data Collection risk category have Add to SRI enabled by default. This cannot be disabled.
 On the Risk Category Weight tab, the Data Collection risk category will have a weight of Medium selected by default. All other Risk categories will have a null value selected by default.
 Detect assigns all system KRIs an unmodifiable, medium weight . 
 You cannot enable Add to SRI for:
- An inactivated KRI. You must first ensure a KRI is activated before you can enable the KRI's Add to SRI button. 
- A KRI if the KRI's risk category is not assigned a weight on the Risk Category Weight tab.
