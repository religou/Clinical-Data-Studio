# Data Surveillance: View Refresh Status For Individual AI Data Reconciliation Reports

## Data Surveillance: View Refresh Status For Individual AI Data Reconciliation Reports
## What Is New?
You can now view individual refresh status for each of the eight AI Data Reconciliation reports.
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Data managers.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to the other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 You can determine further action based on the latest data in individual AI data reconciliation reports. For example, if a report refresh failed, you may need to contact the administrator to reconfigure the report.
## Where Do I See the Change?
 In the Data Surveillance > AI Data Reconciliation reports section, when you click either the Conditions or Treatments tab, each report appears. with an icon that indicates refresh status. For example, the Condition tab in a study A, appears:
- - indicates the report failed to refresh. If you open the report, a Report Refresh Failed label shows next to the report name.  
- No icon - indicates the report refreshed successfully. If you open the report, it shows the timestamp when the data was last refreshed along with a Success label next to the report name. 
-  - indicates the report was skipped during the data refresh process. You must contact your system administrator to update report configurations.
