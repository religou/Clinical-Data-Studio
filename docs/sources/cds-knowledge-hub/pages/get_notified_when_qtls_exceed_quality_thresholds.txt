# Get Notified When QTLs Exceed Quality Thresholds

## Get Notified When QTLs Exceed Quality Thresholds
## What Is New?
 If a QTL in your study breaches the preidentified data quality threshold or is considered an excursion, then you receive a notification in the Notification Center that at least one QTL has a status of Out of Range or Excursion. 
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 QTL Dashboards users that opt in to receive notifications through the Notification Center. 
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 While there is no direct UI impact, the notifications are sent through email, and also made available in the Medidata Notification Center.
## How Do I Benefit?
 You can now receive notifications for all Detect Notification-enabled studies in which Detect determines that a QTL has become out of range of the data quality threshold or an excursion.
## Where Do I See the Change?
 You see the refresh notifications through email and also in the Medidata Notification Center.
## Where Do I Learn More?
 None.
## What Else Should I Know?
 To view Medidata testing documentation for this release, refer to the Medidata Validation Portal .
