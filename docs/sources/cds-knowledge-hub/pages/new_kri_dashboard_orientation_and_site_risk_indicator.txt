# New KRI Dashboard Orientation and Site Risk Indicator

## New KRI Dashboard Orientation and Site Risk Indicator
## Description
- The KRI Dashboard orientation has been changed to vertical to enable you to select a site or a country to view KRIs in detail. 
- You can select a detailed view for sites and countries with the aid of a new Side by Side View button. 
- You can select up to four sites by checking the check boxes next to sites and click View. 
- You can compare up to four sites or countries. 
- The KRI Dashboard now shows the Site Risk Indicator (SRI), based on selected KRIs. 
## Affected Roles
- Site Monitor 
- Study Manager 
## Advantages
- Concise view of the KRI outliers for a country and site 
- Easy to view side-by-side comparison of the KRI outliers 
- Grading site performance based on selected KRIs. 
## User Impact
- A new Side by Side View button added on the KRI Dashboard page that allows you to get a detailed view for up to four selected sites and countries. 
- 
- The Site view has a site option and the Countries view has a country option.
- The View, Close, and Clear buttons are locked while in view mode. 
- The View By dropdown on the KRI Dashboard includes two new options: 
- Sites - High Risk
- Sites - Medium Risk.
