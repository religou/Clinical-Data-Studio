## Change Details
 Product 
 | Clinical Data Studio
 Version 
 | 2026.1.0
 Change Type 
 | Enhancement (Updated  Functionality)
 Functionality or Module 
 | RBQM Library
 Summary of Changes 
 | 
 You can now import custom templates for C ritical data, Critical process and Risk categorization across study groups. 
 Benefit to Users 
 | Data managers can import custom templates across study groups and easily re-use templates.
 Summary of User Impact 
 | 
 Impact to Users and Roles 
 Data managers.
 Impact to Workflow Processes 
 No impact to workflow processes.
 Impact to Applications 
 No impact to other applications.
 Impact on Interface 
 On the RBQM > Risk Assessment left-hand menu, if you click
- Critical Data page, and then click  Add Critical Date , you can import any active custom critical data template from another study. 
- Critical Processes page, and then click Add Critical Process , you can import any active custom critical processes template from another study. 
- Categorization page, and then click Add Risk Category , you can import any active custom risk categorization template from another study. 
 Impact to Existing Data or Settings 
 No existing data changes; no configuration or permission updates required.
