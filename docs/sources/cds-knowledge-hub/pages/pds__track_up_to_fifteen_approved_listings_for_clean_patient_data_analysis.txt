# PDS: Track Up to Fifteen Approved Listings for Clean Patient Data Analysis

## PDS: Track Up to Fifteen Approved Listings for Clean Patient Data Analysis
## What Is New?
 You can now activate up to 15 approved data listings for analysis in Clean Patient Tracker .
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 Data managers
## Workflow Process
 You can use Clean Patient Tracker to track unvetted and unreviewed records associated with up to 15 data listings . You can then further drill down directly to clean those listings' records in Data Reviewer. 
## Other Applications
 Clean Patient Tracker .
## How Do I Benefit?
 You can now track an additional five data listings in Clean Patient Tracker. Tracking listings in Clean Patient Tracker:
- Visualizes, lists, and prioritizes outstanding data cleaning tasks so you can focus on the most unclean data or areas of most importance in your study. 
- Facilitates faster cleaning of data. 
- Refreshes for an up-to-date view of data cleanliness. 
- Increases study team-level coordination and visibility of data cleanliness and outstanding data cleaning tasks. 
- Promotes cleaner, more fully vetted study data. 
## Where Do I See the Change?
 On the Data Listings page > Approved Listings table, the far right side of the table includes the Track column. You can click the deactivated Activation button, , for the corresponding listings you want Clean Patient Tracker to track. Similarly, t o deactivate listings from being tracked in Clean Patient Tracker, you can click the activated Activation button, .
