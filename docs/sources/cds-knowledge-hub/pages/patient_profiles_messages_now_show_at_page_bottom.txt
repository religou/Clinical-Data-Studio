# Patient Profiles Messages Now Show at Page Bottom

## Patient Profiles Messages Now Show at Page Bottom
## What Is New?
 Patient Profiles 2024.4.3 now show messages at the bottom of pages.
## What Is the Impact?
## Users/Roles
 All.
## Workflow Process
 No impact to the workflow process.
## Other Applications
 No impact to other applications.
## How Do I Benefit?
 Patient Profiles users have a better user experience on the application with messages appearing uniformly.
## Where Do I See the Change?
 In Data Surveillance > Patient Profiles, messages appear at the bottom of the page.
