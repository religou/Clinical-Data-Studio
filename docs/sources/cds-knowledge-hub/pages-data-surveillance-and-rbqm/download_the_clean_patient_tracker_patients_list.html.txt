# Download the Clean Patient Tracker Patients List

## Download the Clean Patient Tracker Patients List
 You can download the Clean Patient Tracker Patients list to a CSV file. Downloading the Clean Patient Tracker Patients list enables you to: 
- Easily exchange the list externally with stakeholders to facilitate communication. 
- Locally save and archive the list. 
- Access Data Surveillance , and then click Clean Patient Tracker in the left-hand menu.
The Clean Patient Tracker page shows. 
- In the Patients section, click Download .
The Clean Patient Tracker Patient Data list downloads to your browser as a CSV file. You can save the file locally to your machine.
