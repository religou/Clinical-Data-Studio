# Review AI Reconciliation Reports Findings

## Review AI Reconciliation Reports Findings
 AI Reconciliation runs proprietary AI-based algorithms   on study data and identifies and lists discrepant records that require reconciliation in the following eight reports: 
- Conditions Without Treatments 
- Worsening Conditions 
- Overlapping Conditions 
- Overlapping non-Worsening Conditions and Ongoing Medical History 
- Invalid CTCAE Grade 
- Missing Lab for CTCAE Grade 
- Overlapping Treatments 
- Treatments Without Conditions or Ongoing Medical History 
 Within the reports, you can:
- View the list of report findings and associated finding details. 
- Investigate the patient profile of the patient associated with a record. 
- View and link other treatments, conditions, or ongoing medical history as being related to a finding.
 Note : You can only do this for the Conditions without Treatments, Worsening Conditions, Treatments without Conditions or ongoing MH reports.
- View and submit Medidata Rave queries on report findings. 
- Manually agree or disagree with findings. 
- Manually mark findings as reviewed or unreviewed. 
## View Report Findings
 Each AI Data Reconciliation report lists records that Data Surveillance considers as being findings. Findings may have additional, related, or unrelated findings associated with them. Data Managers can query finding details, review records, mark records as reviewed, or unreviewed and link findings during the data reconciliation process.

 Column Descriptions 
- Patient ID – The ID of the patient associated with the report record. 
- Site – The site the patient belongs to. 
- Country – The three-letter abbreviation for country in which the site resides. 
- Status – Indicates the record has a Rave query; indicates the record is marked as reviewed. 
- Data Set – For Conditions record data originates from the AE data set; for Treatments record data originates from the CP or CM data set. 
- Reported Term – The condition or treatment the patient reported. 
- Preferred Term – The coded term for the reported term in the record. 
- Start Date – The date when the patient first experienced the condition or treatment. 
- End Date – The last date when the patient experienced the condition or treatment. 
- Confidence – The AI Data Reconciliation algorithm's level of confidence that the record is a finding or a discrepancy. Values are High, Medium, Low.
- High confidence indicates that the algorithm considers with high confidence that the finding details are accurate. 
- Medium confidence indicates that the algorithm has neither high or low confidence that the finding details are accurate. 
- Low confidence indicates that the algorithm has low confidence that the finding is accurate, often due to missing or ambiguous information.

## Link Treatments, Conditions, or Ongoing Medical History (MH) to Findings
 You can link additional treatments, conditions, or medical history for the findings in the following reports:
- Conditions without Treatments 
- Worsening Conditions 
- Treatments without Conditions or Ongoing Medical History (MH) 
- Missing Lab for CTCAE  
 Linking records indicates you identified missing data corresponding to the reported term in the finding, or you disagree with the report findings and the algorithm's high confidence level, and that the algorithm missed a possible connection between events. Linking reports outside the event time frame does not impact the confidence level as the algorithm is already aware that the finding and the outside time frame record are related.  
 Note : Linking records does not automatically train the algorithm that two records are in the same time frame. 
## Linking in Conditions without Treatments and Treatments without Conditions or Ongoing MH Reports
 By linking records, you can train the AI Reconciliation algorithm to identify similar findings in future, and build a relation based on the WHODRUG and MEDRA databases.  When you link one or more conditions, treatments, medical history (MH), or lab records, the confidence level changes to low and Data Surveillance stores the information for future occurrences of similar conditions and treatments. For more information see, Condition Without Treatments Algorithm Methodology and  Treatments Without Conditions and Ongoing MH Algorithm Methodology .

## Missing Dates 
 If you link treatments, conditions or ongoing medical history that occur within the same time frame as the event, then the confidence level lowers. If any part of the date that connects the findings is missing either due to incorrect information or human error, the AI Reconciliation algorithm sets this to the first day of the month or the first month of the year, depending on the piece of information missing from the date. For example, if the date is UN MAR 2019 , the algorithm assumes it as 1 MAR 2019. Similarly, if the date is 5 UNK 2019, the algorithm takes it as 5 Jan 2019. 
 Note : This rule does not apply to non-conformant dates. 
 If no date is given, then the AE, or CM, or CP start date is taken as the start of the clinical study, and there is no end date. That is, the record is assumed ongoing.

## Agree or Disagree with Findings 
 AI Reconciliation assigns to each report finding the algorithm's level of confidence that a record is indeed a finding. A finding is a data anomaly. The confidence levels are: High, Medium, and Low. High confidence means that the algorithm has a high confidence that the finding is accurate. Low confidence means that the algorithm has a low level of confidence that the finding is accurate, often due to missing or ambiguous information. Note though, that depending on the report you are working in, the criteria for High, Medium, and Low confidence levels vary. To learn more, see Confidence Level for Individual Reports .
 As a data manager, you can provide feedback on the finding by agreeing or disagreeing with the finding. The green thumbs up and the red thumbs down icons in  the Feedback column in the row corresponding to a finding indicate if you agree or disagree, respectively. A gray thumbs up and thumbs down icon together indicate that no feedback exists yet for the finding.
## Automatically Disagree 
 You disagree automatically with a finding when you link conditions, treatments, or medical history to findings . The record's confidence level also lowers, and t he thumbs down icon replaces the thumbs up or the gray thumbs up icon in the corresponding row. The in the Feedback column in a reviewed record indicates the built-in algorithm missed a possible relation between the finding and another event, and acts as feedback for marking similar records in future.  
## Automatically Agree 
 When you submit a query , AI Reconciliation adds a thumbs up icon in the Feedback column in the row corresponding to the finding. This indicates you agree that the finding is indeed a data anomaly. The confidence level for the finding remains high. 
## Manually Disagree

 Explanation by Specific Report 
 | High

 | Medium

 | Low 
 | A finding has low confidence if the start date of one record matches the end date of another or vice versa.

 Confidence Level 
 Explanation by Specific Report 
 | High  
 | A finding has high confidence when low confidence criteria do not apply.  
 | Medium  
 | The Conditions without Treatments report do not assign a medium confidence level to any finding. 
 | Low 
 | A finding has low confidence when:
- It includes conditions that cannot be treated. 
- The patient with a condition undertakes a treatment for which another patient within the study has linked as treating the condition.

 Confidence Level 
 Explanation by Specific Report 
 | High  
 | All findings are assigned a high confidence level.
 | Medium  
 | The Worsening Conditions report does not assign a medium confidence level to any finding. 
 | Low 
 | The Worsening Conditions report does not assign a low confidence level to any finding. 
 Treatments Without Conditions Report 
 Confidence Level 
 Explanation by Specific Report 
 | High  
 | A finding has high confidence when low or medium confidence criterion does not apply.  
 | Medium  
 | The Treatments without Conditions report do not assign a medium confidence level to any finding. 
 | Low 
 | A finding is low confidence if either: 
- The treatment is not known to treat any condition/history. 
- The patient has a condition or history for which some user within the study has linked as being treated by the treatment. 
 Missing Lab for CTCAE Grade Report 
 Confidence Level 
 Explanation by Specific Report 
 | High  
 | The confidence level is high if no lab record exists within the same time frame as the adverse event for the record.
 | Medium  
 | The confidence level is medium if the lab record exists within the same time frame as the adverse event, but neither the analyte name nor the sample type match the lab record. 
 | Low 
 | The confidence level is low if the lab record exists within the same time frame as the adverse event and matches either the sample type or analyte name, but not both.

 Confidence Level

- if the patient adverse event record has an assigned numeric CTCAE grade but the adverse event is not present in the CTCAE version 5.0 criteria.

- If the patient adverse event record has an assigned numeric CTCAE grade and that adverse event is present in the CTCAE version 5.0 criteria, but the grade is not a permissible one.  
 | Medium  
 | Not applicable 
 | Low 
 | Not applicable
