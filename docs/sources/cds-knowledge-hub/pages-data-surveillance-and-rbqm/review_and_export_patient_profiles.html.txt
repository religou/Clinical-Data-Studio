# Review and Export Patient Profiles

## Review and Export Patient Profiles
 The workflow progress bar below depicts where viewing a patient profile occurs within the recommended patient profile design workflow. 
 You can view and mark as reviewed any study subject's patient profile at any time during the profile design process. You can also export patient profiles to PDF format. 
 Tip : Play the video below to learn how to review and publish a patient profile.

## View a Patient Profile
 During the profile design process, patient profile designers and users with view-only privileges can view a patient profile with actual study data to help you evaluate how the profile design is taking shape in real time. As a designer, this provides the convenience of iteratively editing the design and then viewing the profile again with the modified design against the subject data. Therefore, you always have a clear and accurate picture of whether the design is correct and showing the required results. If not, you can quickly recognize what you need to edit in your design to achieve your design goals. 
- 
 Access the design. 
- Access Data Surveillance , and then click Patient Profiles in the left-hand menu.
 The Patient Profile Designs page shows the Published Designs and Draft Designs   tabs, which contain the list of designs in your study environment. 
- On the Patient Profiles Designs page, do one of the following: 
- Click the Published Designs tab.
 Published Designs Tab Column Descriptions 
- Name  – The patient profile design's name. 
- Type – Indicates whether the patient profile is one of the following types: 
- Detect – The patient profile design is integrated with an accessible directly from other Data Surveillance and RBQM capabilities. 
- View Only – A read-only patient profile design. 
- Last Refresh Date – The time stamp when the last data refresh was performed for the profile design. 
- Published Date  – The time stamp when the profile design was marked as master. This field displays for Published Designs. 
- Updated By  – The user who most recently updated the patient profile design. 
- Click the Draft Designs tab.
 Draft Designs Tab Column Descriptions 
- Name  – The patient profile design's name. 
- Last Updated – The time stamp of when the most recent update was made to the draft patient profile design. 
- Updated By  – The user who most recently updated the patient profile design. 
- Optional . Click any column header to sort the designs list. Clicking once sorts the list in descending order (A to Z, 0 to 9) by the selected column header; twice sorts in ascending order (Z to A, 9 to 0).
 Note : Published Designs and Draft Designs lists appear in the reverse chronological order based on the Published Date and the Last Updated dates, respectively. 
- Click anywhere within the row of the design you want to access. 
 The Design page appears and shows the Profiles tab by default. 
 The Profiles tab > Patient Selector section shows data loaded in ascending alphanumeric order by Patient ID. By default, the patient profile for the first patient listed appears below. For large data sets, a progress bar appears above the Patient Selector section to indicate the progress of data uploaded from data sets. 
- Optional . Sort or filter the Patient Selector to find the patient profiles you need.
 Tip : You can click-and-drag in any Patient Selector column header to resize the column width to custom fit the table spacing to view more variables on screen.
- Click within the corresponding row of a Patient ID to view that patient's patient profile.

## Filter the Patient Selector
- Click  to apply filters to all columns in the Patient Selector section. Sort or scroll through the list as necessary, and then click within the corresponding row of a Patient ID to view that patient's patient profile below. 
 Tips for Viewing Patient Profile Data
- Type or select from   in the Patient ID column to view the corresponding patient profile. For example,  if you enter 1005, and press Enter or click outside of the Filter field, the Patient Selector section loads profiles for patient IDs containing "1005", like 100501, 100523, 100528 in an ascending order.  
- The filter response differs slightly depending on the selected parameter. You can apply column filters to all columns in the Patient Selector section. If you filter by one column and then by another, the first record that matches the filter parameters shows at the top of the Patient Selector section. The first query that matches the filter parameter in the Patient Selector section, shows at the top of the Patient Query table. 
- When you select a Patient ID in the Patient Selector section, the Patient ID displays in the browser tab. When you navigate to another patient ID, the browser tab shows the new patient ID. For example, you select patient ID 10011004 from the Patient Selector section, the browser tab reads Patient Profiles - 10011004 . The browser tab reads Patient Profiles if you navigate to any other page in Patient Profiles. 
- The Patient Profile tab below the Patient Selector section and all element tables in the patient profile appear as expanded by default. 
- You can collapse all element tables simultaneously by clicking the Collapse All button below the section header and above the first element. Click Expand All to expand everything again. 
- You can click  in the right-hand corner of the Patient Selector or any element table to collapse the section. Click  to expand again. 
- You can also see Best Practices for Finding Profile Data for Specific Elements . 
## Best Practices for Finding Profile Data for Specific Elements
 On the Profiles tab, the Patient Profile tab below the Patient Selector section and all element tables in the patient profile appear as expanded by default. However, for patient profiles with many elements, having all elements expanded can require a lot of effort and page scrolling to find a particular element in the profile. Therefore, you can collapse all elements to help you scan the profile data and find the elements you need. And then you can expand individual element tables as needed. 
- Click  Collapse All  below the section header and above the first element. 
- Scan the profile data, and click  on each specific element you want to expand and view. 
 Note : When you export a patient profile to PDF, all elements always display as expanded. 
## Identify and Review Patient Profile Data Updates
 When viewing an individual patient profile within a published patient profile design, you can quickly view an automated list of specific data within the selected patient profile that is new or updated as of the latest data refresh. 
 The automatic listing and reporting of specific data changes in a patient profile:
- Gives you clearer visibility and understanding of the data that w as previously reviewed but now requires rereview due to post-review data changes. 
- Reduces the time and effort required to analyze patient profile data to determine which specific data requires review. 
 Therefore, conducting patient profile design reviews is now clearer, simpler, and more efficient. 
 Note : Patient Profiles only identifies and reports new and updated data for patient profiles that have been marked as reviewed prior to the most recent data refresh. It does not report new and updated data for patient profiles that have never been marked as reviewed.

- Provide a convenient, centralized way to summarize or notate interesting review findings. 
- Facilitate a highly visible and continuous flow of notes and findings to reference across review cycles. 
 Notes:
- All team members in your study can view all patient profile comments.
