# Refresh Designs to Reflect the Latest Available Study Data

## Refresh Designs to Reflect the Latest Available Study Data
 You can set scheduled refreshes or manually refresh individual published patient profile designs to show and reflect data based on the latest study data ingested by Data Surveillance. 
 Performing data refreshes on individual patient profile designs enables you to: 
- Control the data that you want to show for your patient profile designs. 
- Control the pace of your patient profile review cycles. 
- Gain clearer visibility and understanding of the data that: 
- Requires initial review. 
- Is reviewed and up to date. 
- Was previously reviewed but now requires rereview due to post-review data changes. 
 Therefore, conducting patient profile design reviews is now clearer, simpler, and more efficient. 
 Important : We highly recommend r eviewing patients marked with the Requiring Review status before refreshing profile designs. You can filter the Patient Selector by the Review Status column > Requiring Review filter to find patients that require review. Otherwise, you risk losing track of and not reviewing new and updated data from previous data snapshots. 
## Refresh Patient Profiles Data On-Demand
 Patient Profiles supports on-demand refresh directly within the application to instantly trigger manual data updates independently of predefined automated schedules .  Items already scheduled or queued for data refresh today disable automatically, and are unavailable for selection for on-demand refreshes. 
 Total daily refreshes combine both scheduled and on-demand refreshes : You can perform up to two refreshes per day per design. 
 Tip : We recommend refreshing data for patient profile designs only when you need to see new data. For example, you might want to refresh a patient profile design to review data after a specific study milestone date. 
- 
 Access a published design. 
- Access Data Surveillance , and then click Patient Profiles in the left-hand menu.
 The Patient Profile Designs page shows the Published Designs and Draft Designs   tabs, which contain the list of designs in your study environment.
- On the Patient Profiles Designs page, c lick the Published Designs tab.
 Published Designs Tab Column Descriptions 
- Name  – The patient profile design's name. 
- Type – Indicates whether the patient profile is one of the following types: 
- Detect – The patient profile design is integrated with an accessible directly from other Data Surveillance and RBQM capabilities. 
- View Only – A read-only patient profile design. 
- Published Date – The time stamp when the profile design was marked as master. This field displays for Published Designs. 
- Updated By  – The user who most recently updated the patient profile design. 
- Optional . Click any column header to sort the designs list. Clicking once sorts the list in descending order (A to Z, 0 to 9) by the selected column header; twice sorts in ascending order (Z to A, 9 to 0).
 Note : Published Designs lists appear in the reverse chronological order based on the Published Date and the Last Updated dates, respectively. 
- Click anywhere within the row of the design you want to access. 
 The Design page appears and shows the Profiles tab by default. 
- On the Profiles tab, click  Refresh .
 Notes:
- The Refresh button is inactive if the current data shown in the design is from the most recent data refresh or if a refresh is in progress. 
- Patient profile designs   may take several minutes or more to refresh depending on the design complexity and patient volume. 
- The name of the last user who refreshed the profile shows in the design page header below the design name. 
## Refresh Data On A Fixed Schedule
 You can set published profile designs in a study to refresh data at a specified cadence by configuring a refresh schedule on the  Unified Scheduler page . You can also schedule refreshes on the Patient Profiles page.
## View Refresh Schedule
 You can view the details of the scheduled refresh configured for published patient profile designs.
- Access Data Surveillance . 
- Click Patient Profiles   in the left-hand menu. 
- On the Published Designs tab, click   View Refresh Schedule .
The Refresh schedule page shows all the scheduled refreshes. For each schedule, the following appears:
 Schedule Refresh Details 
- Name – Title of the refresh. 
- Frequency – Frequency at which refresh occurs. 
- Last Edited B y – The user who last edited the refresh schedule. 
- Optional.  Click Export Settings to export the scheduled refresh configurations.

## Delete Schedule

- Access Data Surveillance , and then click   Patient Profiles   in the left-hand menu. 
- On the Published Designs tab, click   View Refresh Schedule .
 The Refresh schedule page shows all the scheduled refreshes 
- For any schedule, click   , the   More   icon from the top right corner of the schedule.

- A Delete Schedule message appears.
 Note : You cannot undo deletion. We recommend deleting schedules only if needed.
- Click Delete.
 The refresh schedule is deleted. 
## View Refresh History
 Review data refresh history within to monitor data update details for individual modules . The Refresh history page shows: 
- When refreshes occurred . 
- The type of refresh performed  
- The associated snapshot date . 
- The user who scheduled or started the refresh . 
- For listings, it shows the record count associated with the refresh. 
- Access Data Surveillance , 
- Click   Custom Graphs   in the left-hand menu. 
- Select any custom graph. 
- Click   , the   More icon , above the custom graph. 
- Click   Refresh History . The Refresh History page shows all refreshes for the custom graph.
 Column Descriptions 
- Refresh Date – Date and time when refresh started. 
- Refresh Type – Scheduled or On-demand. For scheduled refreshes, the refresh cadence also appears. 
- Snapshot Date – Date and time for the data snapshot.  
- Refreshed By – Person who started the refresh. 
- Optional. Filter or Sort the refresh history.
