# Access Data Surveillance

## Access Data Surveillance
 Access to Data Surveillance requires that you are invited to a study that is integrated with and linked to the Data Surveillance app in Medidata. Once you receive an invitation and you activate your Medidata account, you can log in to Medidata and access your studies in Data Surveillance. To learn more, see 
 Login, Navigate Medidata, and Access Studies .
 Note : You require permissions to access and perform specific actions in each Data Surveillance capability. To learn more, see Data Surveillance User Roles and Capabilities .
 To access Data Surveillance: 
- Do one of the following: 
- 
- If you are not yet logged into Medidata:
- Log in to iMedidata.com 
 Your   Medidata homepage  appears. This  includes any assigned studies, study groups, app access, and eLearnings. 
- Click the Data Surveillance tab in the tab bar below the Medidata Home page welcome message. 
- In the Clients table, click the client name containing the study you want to access. 
- In the Study Groups table, click the study group name containing the study you want to access. 
- In the Studies table, click the study you want to access.
 The Data Surveillance > Clean Patient Tracker page shows by default. 
- If you are currently working in another Medidata app:
- Click the Data Surveillance tab in the tab bar below the study banner.
The Data Surveillance > Clean Patient Tracker page shows by default.
