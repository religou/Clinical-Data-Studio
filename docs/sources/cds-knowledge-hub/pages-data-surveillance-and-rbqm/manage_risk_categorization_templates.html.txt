# Manage Risk Categorization Templates

## Manage Risk Categorization Templates
 RBQM administrators can create study-level risk categorization templates for risk categories that the study team plans to include in the risk assessment for multiple studies.
 RBQM administrators can also update template details and activate and deactivate risk categorization templates for use across all studies in the study group.
 Using risk categorization templates:
- Reduces time and effort when creating risk assessments. 
- Ensures accurate, consistent risk category details across multiple studies.

- Move your cursor over the Risk Category Objective field to show the pencil icon. Add an objective or edit any existing objective text.
 Note : Some objectives appear blank. Add text for each objective because this text appears when you hover your cursor over the risk name in any of the Risk Assessment or Risk Inventory pages.  Note : Before using the template, you must activate it on the template listing page by moving the gray toggle to the right so it turns blue .  
## Activate or Deactivate a Risk Categorization Template
 You must activate the template before using it in a study.
- Access the study-level list of Risk Categorization Templates .
- Do either of the following:
- To activate a template, find the template you want to activate and move the toggle in the Activate column from gray  to blue .
- To deactivate a template, find the template you want to deactivate and move the toggle in the Activate column from blue to gray .
 Tip : Click All to activate or deactivate all templates in the list.
## Edit a Risk Categorization Template
 You must deactivate the template before you can edit it.
 Note : For templates that are activated, the OK button appears at the bottom instead of Save . Activated templates are read-only at the study group level.
- Access the study-level list of Risk Categorization Templates . 
- Ensure the Activate toggle is gray  before you select the template to be edited. 
- Change the field values in the template. Hover your cursor over a field to access the pencil icon and adjust the field value. 
- Click Save . 
## Delete a Risk Categorization Template
 The Delete button only shows for a template that is deactivated and has never been activated in the past.
- Access the study-level list of Risk Categorization Templates . 
- Select the template to be deleted. 
- Click the Delete button.
The message, "Risk Categorization Template deleted successfully," confirms deletion of the template.
