# Generate and Acknowledge Active Risk Assessment Reports

## Generate and Acknowledge Active Risk Assessment Reports
 You can generate a PDF report of the active risk assessment to enable risk assessment participants to : 
- View or share the report in PDF format. 
- Evaluate the risk plan's current status. 
- Ensure the risk plan includes the correct information as specified in the risk assessment. 
 In addition, risk assessment participants who are assigned to acknowledge risk plan reports can sign and date their acknowledgement on the active risk assessment's risk plan report PDF.
- Access RBQM , and then click Risk Assessment > Details in the left-hand menu.
- Click Download . 
- Select one or more of the following the risk assessment components to include in the report: 
- Risk Inventory – Include all identified risks for a specific study. 
- Critical Data – Include all identified critical data points for the specific study. 
- Critical Processes – Include all identified critical processes for the specific study. 
- Risk Categorization – Include all identified Risk Categories for the specific study. These attributes appear for each Risk Category:
- Risk Category Number 
- Category of the Risk Category 
- Risk Category Template Name 
- Risk Category Objective 
- Risk Category Factors (Impact, Probability, Detectability) 
- Risk Category Weight 
- Risk Category Score 
- Questions for Discussion – Include all risk category questions for the specific study. 
- Click .pdf or. xlsx .
- Click  Generate Report .
The report downloads locally to your web browser as a PDF or an Excel file.
- Open a report, and acknowledge the report by printing it and manually signing and dating the Signature and Date Acknowledged , respectively, at the top of a printed copy or using a signature feature if included in your PDF viewer software.
 Notes :
- You must enter data in any field not marked optional before generating the report. 
- You do not need to add a participant before generating a report. 
- After you click Generate Report, the report for the DRAFT risk assessment shows on the screen. You can then print, save, or download the report. 
- The protocol version you add on the Risk Assessment Details page appears on the downloaded risk assessment report.
