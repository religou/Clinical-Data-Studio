# KRI Dashboard

## KRI Dashboard
 Key Risk Indicators (KRIs) are standard and protocol specific study metrics that enable you to consistently:  
- Identify and/or track known or anticipated risks to data quality. 
- Evaluate country and site performance in a study. 
- Monitor site compliance with data collection. 
 KRIs are predefined metrics that you determine at the outset of a study, KRIs are calculated at site and country level with overall study metric.  
 The KRI Dashboard lists the KRIs that your study monitors and their respective KRI values at each site or country.  
 RBQM includes the following system KRIs are available on most Medidata Rave studies : 
- AE Entry Delay 
- Manual Query Rate 
- Non-System Query Delay 
- Number of AEs per Patient-Month 
- Number of SAEs per Patient-Month 
- Percent Out of Window Visit 
- Percent Missing Visit 
- System Query Rate 
- System Query Delay 
- Visit Entry Delay 
- Modification rate 
 You can also define and track custom KRIs  to help monitor protocol specific risks, more often than not related to non-compliance with collection of critical data. We recommend you balance t he number of custom KRIs against what is critical, the availability of other analyses (such as over/under-reporting), and the resulting operational burden.
 For clinical data trending, and reporting over time including site level reporting consider creating companion visualizations. For more information, see  Custom Graphs . 
 For more information on each KRI, see KRI Descriptions and Calculation Methodologies . 
 Important : You do not create or manage custom KRIs on the KRI Dashboard. Buyt you can do so using KRI Configuration .  
 Important : KRIs do not support datetime operations.
## What Is the KRI Dashboard?
 The KRI Dashboard shows the KRI values and trends for all selected KRIs and summarizes their impact on the  Site Risk Score (SRI) . 
## What Is the Purpose of the KRI Dashboard?
 To ensure quality conduct, studies can implement RBQM by identifying, assessing, monitoring, and mitigating the risks that could affect the quality or safety of a study. 
 The KRI Dashboard allows you to: 
- View all KRIs and respective values - system and protocol specific. 
- Determine which KRI values are outliers  based on statistical or predefined thresholds . 
- View historical trends in site- and country-level KRI data. 
- View sites’ performance respective to other sites within each KRI. 
- Rank sites based on the Site Risk Score (SRI) to allow better site prioritization based on the aggregated score across relevant KRIs weighted based on the risk category 
 Based on your observations made from the KRI Dashboard, you can: 
- Automatically set up signal detection that requires further investigation or mitigation. See the Signals page . 
- Determine the best approach or mitigating actions to take on the issues or risks. 
- Assess the effect the mitigating actions have made on the issues or risks over time. 
## Who Uses the KRI Dashboard?
 Anyone who tracks risk can use the KRI Dashboard. For example: 
- Remote or site monitors - Can view comprehensive overview of site compliance with data collection, reporting as per protocol and study determined expectations. 
- Data Managers – Can use a series of system KRIs that provide a good overview of data currency metrics including query rates, query management timeliness, and data reporting delays. 
- Medical Monitors – Might be interested in viewing safety information, for example, non-serious adverse events (AEs), and serious adverse events (SAEs) reporting patterns.
