# View Audit Details for Deactivated KRIs

## View Audit Details for Deactivated KRIs
 You can view audit details for deactivated key risk indicators (KRIs) that describe when the KRI was deactivated, who deactivated the KRI, and why the KRI was deactivated for analysis.
- Access RBQM , and then click Configuration > KRIs in the left-hand menu.
- On the Key Risk Indicators page > KRI Configurations list > Status column, click   in the corresponding row of the deactivated KRI whose audit details you want to view. 
- In the Deactivation Note, read the following details:
- Deactivated Date  – The date and timestamp when the KRI was deactivated. 
- Deactivated By  – The user in your organization who deactivated the KRI. 
- Note – The reason or justification for deactivating the KRI. Deactivation notes are read-only. 
- Click   to close the window and return to the KRIs page. 
 Note : Formerly deactivated KRIs that have since been reactivated no longer show the   in the Status column or have an associated deactivation note.
