# View Audit Details for Deactivated QTLs

## View Audit Details for Deactivated QTLs
 You can view audit details for deactivated quality tolerance limits (QTLs) that describe when the QTL was deactivated, who deactivated the QTL, and why the QTL was deactivated for analysis.
- Access RBQM , and then click Configuration > QTLs in the left-hand menu.
The Quality Tolerance Limit page shows. 
- In the Quality Tolerance Limits list > Status column, click   in the corresponding row of the deactivated QTL whose audit details you want to view. 
- In the Deactivation Note, read the following details:
- Deactivated Date  – The date and timestamp when the QTL was deactivated. 
- Deactivated By  – The user in your organization who deactivated the QTL. 
- Note  – The reason or justification that the QTL was deactivated. Deactivation notes are read-only. 
- Click   to close the window and return to the Quality Tolerance Limits page. 
 Note : Formerly deactivated QTLs that have since been reactivated no longer appear the   in the Status column or have an associated deactivation note.
