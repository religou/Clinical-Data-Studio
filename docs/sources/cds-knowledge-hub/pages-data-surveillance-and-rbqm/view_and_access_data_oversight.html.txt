# Access Task Oversight

## Access Task Oversight
 Access to Task Oversight requires that you are invited to a study that is integrated with and linked to the Data Surveillance app in Medidata. Once you receive an invitation and you activate your Medidata account, you can log in to Medidata and access your studies in Data Surveillance. To learn more, see Login, Navigate Medidata, and Access Studies .
 Note : You require permissions to access and perform specific actions in each Data Surveillance capability. To learn more, see   Data Surveillance User Roles and Capabilities .
 To access Task Oversight: 
- 
- Do one of the following:
- If you are not yet logged into Medidata:
- Log in to   iMedidata.com 
 Your   Medidata homepage  appears. This  includes any assigned studies, study groups, app access, and eLearnings.
 Note : You require access to Data Surveillance capability to access Task Oversight. To learn more, see   Data Surveillance User Roles and Capabilities .
- Click Task Oversight tab in the tab bar below the Medidata Home page welcome message. 
- In the   Clients   table, click the client name containing the study you want to access. 
- In the   Study Groups   table, click the study group name containing the study you want to access. 
- In the   Studies   table, click the study you want to access.
- If you are currently working in another Medidata app:
- Click the   Task Oversight   tab in the tab bar below the study banner.
The Task Oversight page shows by default.
