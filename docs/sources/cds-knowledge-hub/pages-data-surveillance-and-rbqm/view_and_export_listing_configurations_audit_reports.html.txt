# View and Export Listing Configurations Audit Reports

## View and Export Listing Configurations Audit Reports
 You can view an audit report of historical actions and updates made on all data listing configurations in your study. For example, you can see:
- User actions made on data listing configurations. 
- Users who create or updated data listing configurations. 
- Users who deactivated or activated specific data listings. 
 Viewing the audit trail of actions made on data listing configurations ensures traceability using a reportable single source of truth. 
 You can also export the audit trail for:
- Additional data manipulation in Excel or .csv format. 
- Offline stakeholder communication. 
- Local record retention. 
 Note : Actions made in Data Surveillance can take up to five minutes to show in audit reports.
- 
 If necessary, access the Manage Listing page. 
- Access Data Surveillance , and then click Listings in the left-hand menu.
The Data Listings page shows. 
- Click Manage Listing on the upper-right side of the Data Listings page.
 The Manage Listing page > Listings table shows all draft and approved listings in your study and each listing's associated details. 
- Click next to the Manage Listing page header. 
- The Data Reviewer Listings Audit Report appears.
 Tip : Click Show Filters above the upper-right corner of the table to filter the report by Start Date and End Date .
- Optional : To export the audit report, click Export in the upper-right corner of the page, and then select one of the following:
- To Excel 
- To CSV 
- To PDF
