# AI Reconciliation

## AI Reconciliation
 While conducting a study, study managers must ensure the accuracy of data that sites enter. Bad or missed data reconciliation could mean a delay in submission or even impact study approval. Manually reconciling data is: 
- Time-consuming and tedious due to manual data review. 
- Prone to errors as individuals without clinical experience usually do the reconciliation.   
 AI Reconciliation is a Data Surveillance capability that runs AI-based algorithms on study data to automatically reconcile data and report missing and duplicate information in place of manual data reconciliation. 
## What Is AI Reconciliation?
 AI Reconciliation is a Data Surveillance  capability that runs proprietary AI-based algorithms on study data to: 
- Identify patient records with anomalies, duplicate, or missing information. 
- Find relations between conditions, treatments, or ongoing medical history using medical databases. 
- Output the identified records in a readable and shareable report format. 
 AI Reconciliation lists the identified records in the following reports:
- Conditions Without Treatments 
- Worsening Conditions 
- Overlapping Conditions 
- Overlapping non-Worsening Conditions and Ongoing Medical History 
- Invalid CTCAE Grade 
- Missing Lab for CTCAE Grade 
- Overlapping Treatments 
- Treatments Without Conditions or Ongoing Medical History

## What Is the Purpose of AI Reconciliation?
 The purpose of AI Reconciliation is to apply AI-based algorithms to your study data to automatically reconcile data and report missing and duplicate information instead of manual reconciliation. 
## What are the Benefits of AI Reconciliation?
 AI Reconciliation:
- Ensures accurate data reconciliation. 
- Saves time compared to manual reconciliation. 
- Produces easily readable and shareable reports and report findings. 
- Helps study teams overcome resource deficiencies. 
## Who Uses AI Reconciliation?
 The most common AI Reconciliation users are:
- Data Managers 
- Clinical Operations users 
## How Does AI Reconciliation Work?
 AI Reconciliation aims to reconcile AEs, reactogenicity, concomitant medication, concomitant procedure, and medical history data coming from Medidata Rave. Once your study is in Data Surveillance, you can map the data sets and variables that Data Surveillance consumes for AI Reconciliation to run its analysis on to generate reports and identify discrepant data findings . 
 Then, AI Reconciliation runs its proprietary AI-based algorithms on the mapped study data and identifies and displays records that require reconciliation in the following reports: 
- Conditions Without Treatments 
- Worsening Conditions 
- Overlapping Conditions 
- Overlapping non-Worsening Conditions and Ongoing Medical History 
- Invalid CTCAE Grade 
- Missing Lab for CTCAE Grade 
- Overlapping Treatments 
- Treatments Without Conditions or Ongoing Medical History 
 To learn more, see  About AI Reconciliation Reports .
 In AI Reconciliation, you can:
- View the list of report findings and associated finding details. 
- Investigate the patient profile of the patient associated with the record. 
- View or link other treatments, conditions, or ongoing medical history as being related to a finding. 
 Note : You can link treatments, conditions, or ongoing medical history only for the following reports:
- Conditions without Treatments 
- Worsening Conditions 
- Treatments without Conditions or ongoing MH 
- View and submit Rave queries on report findings. 
- Manually agree or disagree with findings . 
- Manually mark findings as reviewed or unreviewed 
 Note : You can refresh data in AI Reconciliation up to two times a day based on Rave refresh schedules. For more information, see   Clinical Data Studio Data Pipeline .
## How Does AI Reconciliation's Algorithm Recognize Data That Requires Reconciliation?
The AI Reconciliation algorithm uses business rules to surface potential discrepancies in patient data. In the Conditions without Treatments and Treatments without Conditions reports, the algorithm leverages a Knowledge Graph (KG) that contains information such as condition- treatment relationships, which enables Data Managers and Clinical Operations users to identify and decide if data discrepancy exists in reconciled data. In the Knowledge Graph, relationships are built based on biomedical drug databases and enriched with WHODrug/MedDRA to enable integration with clinical data.
 Note : AI Reconciliation Reports rely on preferred terms to identify discrepancies. Preferred terms come from Medidata Coder which uses coding dictionaries to code verbatim terms, or an external coder which uses MEDRA or WHODrug data dictionaries. For more information on selecting study data source, see  Map Event Analysis Data . 
## How does the AI Reconciliation's Algorithm Assign Confidence Scores to Findings?
 Confidence refers to the AI Reconciliation algorithm calculated degree of certainty that a record is indeed a finding. In other words, that a data anomaly such as the absence of a necessary treatment for a given condition, exists in the listing. 
 For all listings, confidence level is based on predefined rules detailed in Review AI Reconciliation Reports Findings .
