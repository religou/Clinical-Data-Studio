# Add and Manage Risk Categories

## Add and Manage Risk Categories
 Risk categories are broader categories of risk as opposed to a specific piece of critical data or a critical process. You can identify and include as part of your risk assessment the risk categories that are critical to your study outcome; for example, technology or complexity.
 Adding critical data to the risk assessment helps you focus and monitor the most important data to ensure:
- Protocol compliance, patient safety, data quality, and data integrity. 
- Product or treatment efficacy. 
 You can:
- Add risk categories to the risk assessment. 
- Add, view, edit, and delete questions associated with a risk category. 
- Score a risk category's level of risk to the study. 
- Assign a risk score weight to a risk category. 
- Add and manage the risks associated with risk categories. 
- View the total sum of all risk category risk scores to get a general sense of the study's level of risk. 
- View the risk categories included in the risk assessment. 
- View the risks associated with risk categories. 
- Delete risk categories. 
- View and export Categorization Audit Reports. 
## Add Risk Categories to the Risk Assessment
 Risk categories are broader categories of risk as opposed to a specific piece of critical data or a critical process. You can identify and include as part of your risk assessment the risk categories that are critical to your study outcome; for example, technology or complexity.
 You can add risk categories by: 
- Creating new risk categories at the study level. 
- Using a preexisting study-level critical data template. 
 Note:  Risk categories are permanently inherited from the parent critical data or critical processes. Updating the risk category on the parent critical data or critical processes does not update existing associated risks. To change the risk category for a critical data or critical process, create a new critical data or critical process with the updated risk category and link your risks accordingly. 
 When adding risk categories, you can add questions for the study team to consider when evaluating the risk category's risks.
 Adding critical data to the risk assessment helps you focus and monitor the most important data to ensure:

- Associate QTLs , KRIs , and risk mitigations to the risk.

## Score a Risk Category's Level of Risk to the Study
 You can score a risk category's level of risk to the study. RBQM calculates the risk score for each risk category based on the following risk factors: Impact, Detectability , Probability. To learn more, see Risk Scores . 
 RBQM tallies the collective sum of each risk category's risk score to determine the Total Risk Categorization Score. To learn more, see View the Total Risk Categorization Score . 
- Access RBQM , and then click Risk Assessment > Categorization  in the left-hand menu.
 The Categorization page appears. 
- Optional . To filter the Categorization table to show only risk categories assigned to a specific category, click the View by menu, and then select a category. 
- In the row of the risk category you want to update, click a number in one or more of the following columns:
- Probability – Select a value on a 3-point risk score scale: 
- On a 3-point risk score scale:  High probability = 3, Medium probability = 2, Low probability = 1. 
- On a 5-point risk score scale:  High probability = 5, Medium probability = 3, Low probability = 1. 
- Impact – Select a value on a 3-point risk score scale:  
- On a 3-point risk score scale:  High impact = 3, Medium impact = 2, Low impact = 1. 
- On a 5-point risk score scale:  High impact = 5, Medium impact = 3, Low impact = 1. 
- Detectability – Select a value on a 3-point risk score scale: 
- On a 3-point risk score scale:  Difficult to Detect = 3, Medium to Detect = 2, Easy to Detect = 1.
- On a 5-point risk score scale:  Difficult to Detect = 5, Medium to Detect = 3, Easy to Detect = 1.
- If necessary, assign a risk score weight to a risk category . 
 The Risk Category Score column updates to reflect the risk category's risk score. RBQM calculates the Risk Category Score by multiplying the Category Probability, Category Impact, Category Detectability, and Risk Category Weight. 
 Risk Category Score = (Probability x Impact x Detectability) x Risk Category Weight 
 The maximum risk category score is 27. Color coding indicates the severity of the risk where values 0.1-3.0 appear in green (minimal risk), 3.1-9.0 appear in yellow (moderate risk), and 9.1-27.0 appear in red (high risk). To learn more, see Risk Scores . 
## Assign a Risk Score Weight to a Risk Category
 You can assign a risk weight to risk categories to rank the importance of the risk category. 1.0 is the default weight. Values range from 0.1 (lowest) to 1.0 (highest). Do not enter 0. 
- Access RBQM , and then click Risk Assessment > Categorization  in the left-hand menu.
 The Categorization page appears. 
- Optional . To filter the Categorization table to show only risk categories assigned to a specific category, click the View by menu, and then select a category. 
- In the Risk Category Weight column, click the number in the row corresponding to the risk category you want to update. 
- Click the Up or Down arrow to adjust the risk category weight.
 Note : 1.0 is the default weight. Values range from 0.1 (lowest) to 1.0 (highest). Do not enter 0. 
- Click anywhere outside of the Risk Category Weight cell.
The risk category's Risk Category Weight value and Risk Category Score update.

## View the Total Risk Categorization Score
 The Total Risk Categorization Score shows at the top of the Risk Assessment > Risk Categorization page.  
 Total Risk Categorization Score = The combined total risk category score of all risk categories included in the Risk Assessment 
 For example: 
- 10 risk categories with the following risk category scores: 10 + 12 + 8 + 10 + 6 + 4 + 15 + 15 +10 + 10. 
- The sum of the risk scores for all categories equals 100. 
 The Total Risk Categorization Score uses a color-coded meter to indicate the study's level of risk based on the risk categories. Green indicates minimal risk; yellow indicates moderate risk; red indicates high risk. The color associated with the score is calculated by dividing the Total Risk Categorization Score by the number of risk categories x 27. RBQM then converts the result to a percentage. In the example above, the percentage that determines color calculates as 100/(10 x 27) = 0.37 or 37%.  
 RBQM shows the color of the Total Risk Categorization Score as Yellow based on the percentage: 
- 0-32% (Green)  
- 33%-66% (Yellow) 
- 67%-100% (Red) 
 For example: 
 The Total Risk Categorization Score can give you an overall sense of the study's level of risk. 
- Access RBQM , and then click Risk Assessment > Categorization  in the left-hand menu.
 The Categorization page appears. The Total Risk Categorization score shows at the top right-hand corner of the page.

## View the Risks Associated with Risk Categories
 You can view the list of risks associated with risk categories in your risk assessment. For example, you can view the risks:
- For oversight to ensure the study team is following the risk plan, or 
- As a reference for another study's risk assessment. 
- Access RBQM , and then click Risk Assessment > Categorization  in the left-hand menu.
 The Categorization page appears. 
- Optional . To filter the Categorization table to show only risk categories assigned to a specific category, click the View by menu, and then select a category. 
- On the Risk Categorization table, click the Down Arrow , , to the left of the risk category name whose risks you want to view.
A table shows the list of risks associated with the risk category and the following risk details:
 Column Descriptions 
- Risk ID – The risk's system-assigned unique ID. You can click a risk ID to view and edit the risk review details . 
- Risk Name – The name of the risk. 
- Review Status – A color-coded pill icon shows the review status for each risk associated with the critical data:
- Red – Overdue. The Current Date > Target Review Date, indicating the Risk Review is overdue. 
- Yellow – Due Soon. The Current Date is between Last Review Date and Target Review Date, indicating the Risk Review is due soon. 
- Green – Reviewed. The Last Review Date = Current Date, indicating the Risk is reviewed. 
- Gray – Closed. The Risk Status is closed. 
- Risk Score – A calculated number (score) that reflects the risk's severity. When adding a risk or updating a risk's review details, you can score each of the following risk factors, Probability, Impact, and Detectability, on a scale of 1 to 3. The risk score is calculated by multiplying each risk factor's score together (Probability x Impact x Detectability). The maximum risk score is 27. 
- A risk score of 1-3 appears with green color-coded block and indicates a minimal risk. 
- A risk score of 4-9 appears with a yellow color-coded block and indicates. 
- A risk score of 10-27 appears with a red color-coded black and indicates a high risk. 
## View and Export Categorization Audit Reports
 Audit Reports allow you to  track actions made on the risk categorization of your study . This provides a single, traceable record of updates for audit readiness. For example, you can 
- Identify specific changes that were made (like adding risks to a risk category, deleting risk categories from the risk assessment). 
- Track the study personnel who made updates. 
- Identify when the updates were made. 
 Note :   You require permissions to view the audit trail. To learn more, see   RBQM Building Blocks and Permissions . 
- Access RBQM , and then click   Risk Assessment > Categorization   in the left-hand menu. The Categorization page shows. 
- Click the Clock     icon next to the page header. A new browser tab opens to the Audit Viewer > Categorization Audit Report page. 
- Optional: You can click Show Filters  above the upper-right corner of the table to filter the reports by  Start Date  and  End Date . 
 Tip :   In Audit Viewer, you can click   Export   and then  Excel ,   CSV , or   PDF   to download the report locally and view offline or sort data by column headings.   Report exports include the entire history, regardless of the filters applied in the Audit Viewer window. 
 Important:  Categorization Audit Report records actions made on the risk categorization of your study after 30-July-2026. Any actions  made on the risk categorization prior  to this date is excluded from the audit report.
 Note:   Any changes to risks on Categorization page will be recorded in Risk Inventory Audit Report. To learn more, see Generate Risk Inventory Audit Report .
