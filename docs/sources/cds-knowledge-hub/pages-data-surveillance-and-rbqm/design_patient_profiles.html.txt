# Design Patient Profiles

## Design Patient Profiles
 The workflow progress bar below illustrates the recommended patient profile design workflow from creation to completion.
 The goal of Patient Profiles is to build patient profile designs. You build the patient profile design infrastructure using containers of information known as elements. Elements consist of headers , flags ,  tables , and timelines . And then you populate your elements with data in the form of variables, which derive from multiple preloaded study environment-specific data sets.
 At this point, you can create your design  and begin adding elements .
 When adding elements, and based on the element type you are working with, you can:
- define element details; 
- adjust the placement of variables, rows, and columns within elements; 
- configure variable data points to show in the color of your choosing; 
- and much more. 
 After adding elements to your profile design, you can:
- arrange where the elements appear in the design layout ; and 
- apply color-coding to entire element sections. 
 Meanwhile, throughout the course of the patient profile design process, you can:
- view patient profiles   with study data to help you evaluate how the profile design is taking shape in real time. 
 When satisfied with your design and layout, you can:
- review and generate reports based on the profile design. 
 You can also perform various design management tasks such as:
- Reassign Master Designs 
- Duplicate Designs Within Studies 
- Delete Designs 
- Rename Designs 
 Note : In Data Surveillance, s tudy data automatically refreshes twice daily at approximately 9:00am (EST) and 9:00pm (EST), and the data presented within the user interface is current as of 11:00 pm (EST) the previous day, and 11:00 am (EST) the same day, respectively. You can refresh Patient Profiles twice daily based on these refresh schedules. For more information, see Clinical Data Studio Data Pipeline.
