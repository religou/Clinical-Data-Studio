# Listings

## Listings
 Listings are conditional expressions that parse your study data to identify and report patient data records within a specified variable that may indicate a study risk. 
## What Are Listings?
 Listings are manually configured conditional expressions that parse your study data to capture patient records within a specified variable that match the expression's defined conditions . For example, you can configure an expression to capture patient data records for female patients below age 50 who have had a serious adverse event . 
## What Is the Purpose of Listings?
 At study startup, study teams perform a risk assessment and define the high-risk, critical, or complex study data to monitor for data quality and integrity. To aid in data monitoring, you can create data listings to   identify and report patient data records that may indicate a study risk. The data records captured by the data listing's expression, in general, are the high-risk data points that your study team would want to review, investigate, and potentially mitigate. 
## Who Uses Listings?
 Data managers view, create, edit Listings, and review and validate listings results.
 Other study team members—for example, clinical study managers, clinical research associates, site monitors, central monitors, safety monitors, medical monitors, data managers, biostaticians, programmers, clinical reviewers—use listings to review and validate specific study data that pertains to their role and responsibilities.
## What Are the Benefits of Using Listings?
 By creating and using listings, you can: 
- Focus your data investigation on the most meaningful, high-risk study data. 
- Increase the amount of data you can review while saving time and effort. 
- Ensure protocol compliance, subject safety, data quality, and data integrity. 
- Direct more effective study-mitigating actions. 
## What Are Draft Listings and Approved Listings
- Draft Listings – Listings that have not passed preliminary validation and received approval for team-wide study use. You can only:
- Preview up to 100 patient data records in a draft listing's results. 
- View and edit draft listing configurations 
- Approve draft listings . 
- Approved Listings – Listings that have passed preliminary validation and received approval for team-wide study use. You can:
- View and edit approved listing configurations 
- Review and validate an approved listing's results 
- Track Listings for unreviewed records in Clean Patient Tracker . 
- Refresh an approved listing to view the listing's latest results 
- Export an approved listing's results 
- View and export an approved listing's historical data snapshots 
## How Do Listings Work?
 First, you can optionally  view your study's data sets to:
- Holistically explore and investigate your entire set of study data, and 
- Determine if you should create any Listings to monitor data that may be of high risk to the study. 
 Then you can create draft listings by configuring conditional expressions that run against your study data to capture patient records within a specified variable that   match the expression's defined conditions . For example, you can configure an expression to capture patient data records for   female patients below age 50 who have had a serious adverse event . 
 Your study team can then preview draft listings to ensure the listing passes preliminary validation. In which case, you can approve the listing for team-wide use in your study. Once approved, you and your study team can review and validate the data listing's full set of results . As part of the review process, you and your study team can: 
- Add internal review comments directly on individual data records that appear in a data listing's results. 
- View and submit Medidata Rave queries on Rave-enabled Listings' records directly in Data Surveillance. 
- Mark individual data records as reviewed or in progress. 
 You can also track Listings for unreviewed records in Clean Patient Tracker . 
 Note: In Data Surveillance and RBQM, s tudy data automatically refreshes four times daily at approximately 9:00am (EST), 3:00pm (EST), 9:00pm (EST) and 3:00am (EST), and the data presented within the user interface is current as of 11:00 pm (EST) the previous day, 5:00 am (EST) the same day, 11:00 am (EST) the same day, 5:00 pm (EST) the same day, respectively. Each study data refresh produces a data snapshot. Updated data from Rave, and activated datasets from Data Connect  are available in 6 to 12 hours within Data Surveillance and RBQM. 
 Important : Listings support datetime operations.
