# Configure KRI Risk Category Weight for Site Risk Calculation

## Configure KRI Risk Category Weight for Site Risk Calculation
 The KRI Dashboard visually signifies sites that RBQM assesses as being medium or high risk to your study's performance. KRI Configuration enables you to configure t he weightage (low, medium, or high) that a particular KRI risk category carries when RBQM calculates a site's overall level of risk. C ustomizing the risk category  weight that RBQM applies when assessing site risk more precisely determines at-risk sites based on your study goals and protocol.   
- Access RBQM , and then click Configuration > KRIs in the left-hand menu.
- On the Key Risk Indicators page, click the Risk Category Weight tab. 
- For each risk category, select one of the following weights:
- Low – KRIs belonging to a risk category with low weightage have a reduced impact on RBQM's assessment of a site's performance risk level. Select Low for KRI risk categories that you feel should have less influence on determining a site's performance. 
- Medium – KRIs belonging to a risk category with medium weightage have a standard impact on RBQM's assessment of a site's performance risk level. Select Medium for KRI risk categories that you feel should have a moderate influence on determining a site's performance. 
- High – KRIs belonging to a risk category with high weightage have an increased impact on RBQM's assessment of a site's performance risk level. Select High for KRI risk categories that you feel should have a heavy influence on determining a site's performance.
 Note: You cannot enable Add for SRI for a KRI on the Key Risk Indicator page if the KRI's risk category has no assigned weight. 
- Click Save .
