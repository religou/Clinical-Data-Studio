# View Archived Risk Assessment Reports

## View Archived Risk Assessment Reports
 You can view archived risk assessment reports for auditing purposes or for viewing snapshots in time to see how the risk assessment has progressed over the course of the study.
- Access RBQM , and then click Risk Assessment > Details in the left-hand menu.
- In the Version History page > Version History table, click the Report icon, , in the row associated with the archived risk assessment report you want to view. 
The Risk Assessment Report opens in a separate browser tab. You can download or print the report, but you cannot edit archived reports.
