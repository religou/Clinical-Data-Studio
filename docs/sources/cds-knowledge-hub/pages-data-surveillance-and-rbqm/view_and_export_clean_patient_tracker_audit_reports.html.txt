# View and Export Clean Patient Tracker Audit Reports

## View and Export Clean Patient Tracker Audit Reports
 You can view audit reports of the historical details of actions taken on Clean Patient Tracker in your study environment, such as: 
- Adding missing page values. 
- Marking patients as clean. 
 Audit reports:
- Enable sponsors to quickly and easily show compliance with clear, comprehensive audit details of actions performed in Clean Patient Tracker. 
- Provide sponsors with greater oversight to ensure study data is being monitored correctly. 
- Allows for a quick view of the review status of patient data in bulk in tabular format. 
- Access Data Surveillance , and then click Clean Patient Tracker in the left-hand menu.
The Clean Patient Tracker page shows. 
- Click  from the top right corner of the page.
A new browser tab opens to the Clean Patient Tracker Audit Report page.
 Tip : In Audit Viewer, you can click Export and then  Excel , CSV , or PDF to view the report offline or sort data by column headings.
