# Custom Graphs

## Custom Graphs
 Custom Graphs is a self-serve data visualization and reporting tool that enables you to chart the relationship between any combination of datasets on a single visual using real-time study data on demand. 
 You can plot any study data you want to visualize based on your specific needs or area of interest to: 
- Gain a clearer understanding or perspective of the data. 
- Identify study trends and patterns. 
- Track study performance. 
- Build and share reports. 
## What Is Custom Graphs?
 Custom Graphs is a self-serve study data visualization and reporting tool. 
## What is the Purpose of Custom Graphs?
 You can use Custom Graphs to chart the relationship between any combination of datasets on a single visual using real-time study data on demand. 
## Who Uses Custom Graphs?
 All study team members can use Custom Graphs to create or view data visualizations.
## What Are the Benefits of Using Custom Graphs?
 Custom Graphs enables you to flexibly plot any study data you want to visualize, catering to your specific needs or area of interest. For example: 
- Medical monitors can view the number of adverse events per site.
- Site monitors can track patient enrollment rate.   
- Data managers can view missed visits or data entry delays. 
- CRAs can view, track, and compare patients records for pulse against blood pressure.  
- Study managers can see the number of nonrandomized patients in the study or track the number of study dropouts per month. 
 You can also view the data in the optimal graphical rendering—whether in a bar chart, stacked bar chart, line chart, multiline chart, scatter chart, donut chart, or segmented donut chart. 
 Graphically visualizing study data relationships enables you to more effectively: 
- Gain a clearer understanding or perspective of the study data. 
- Identify study trends and patterns. 
- Track study performance. 
- Build and share reports or present on study data metrics using clear, visually appealing graphical renderings. 
 And you can create Custom Graphs on your own anytime you want. 
## How Does Custom Graphs Work?
 You can plot study data in a bar chart, stacked bar chart, line chart, multiline chart, scatter chart, donut chart, segmented donut chart, or tabular chart. 
 You can create, view, edit, and delete study data visualizations. 
 Note : In Data Surveillance and RBQM, s tudy data automatically refreshes four times daily at approximately 9:00am (EST), 3:00pm (EST), 9:00pm (EST) and 3:00am (EST), and the data presented within the user interface is current as of 11:00 pm (EST) the previous day, 5:00 am (EST) the same day, 11:00 am (EST) the same day, 5:00 pm (EST) the same day, respectively. Each study data refresh produces a data snapshot. Updated data from Medidata Rave, and   activated datasets from Data Connect   are available in 6 to 12 hours within Data Surveillance and RBQM.
