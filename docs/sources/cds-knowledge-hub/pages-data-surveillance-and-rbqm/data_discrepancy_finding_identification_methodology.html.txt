# AI Reconciliation Report Data Discrepancy Identification Methodology

## AI Reconciliation Report Data Discrepancy Identification Methodology
 In AI Reconciliation, a condition is an adverse event that occurs after patients enroll in a study. A treatment is the medication or procedure patients take in response to a condition in the same study. This article focuses on how the AI Reconciliation algorithm works for the Treatment Without Conditions and the Conditions without Treatments and Ongoing MH reports. 
 Patients take treatments in response to a condition when a condition  expects a treatment. The AI Reconciliation algorithm identifies conditions that expect treatments, and do not have corresponding treatments. A condition and treatment are a pair if the treatment treats the condition, and the start date of the treatment lies within the timeframe of the condition starting and ending. A treatment treats a condition if the indication for the treatment matches the condition, or if the algorithm can deduce that the treatment treats the condition using the medication-indication table.
