# Approve Draft Listings

## Approve Draft Listings
 After your study team 
 previews a draft listing and the listing passes preliminary validation, you can approve the listing for team-wide use in your study. Once approved, you and your study team can 
 review and validate the listing's full set of results .
 Note : Upon the Listing's initial approval, Data Surveillance creates a snapshot of the listing's results at the time of approval. See View and Export a Listing's Historical Data Snapshots .
- Access Data Surveillance , and then click Listings in the left-hand menu.
The Listings page shows. 
- On the Listings page > Draft Listings table, select a draft listing.
 Tip : You can  preview a draft listing's results as well as review and edit a draft listing's configuration before approving. 
- Click Approve Listing . 
The listing appears in the Listings page > Approved Listings tab.
 Note : The Edit and Approve Listing buttons become disabled once you click Approve Listing , until the listing is approved.
 Note : When you edit an approved listing and save your changes, the listing returns to draft status, loses all existing reviews, and requires re-approval.
