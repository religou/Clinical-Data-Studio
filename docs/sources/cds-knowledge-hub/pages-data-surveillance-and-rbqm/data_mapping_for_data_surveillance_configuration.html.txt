# Data Mapping

## Data Mapping
 You can configure within Data Mapping the data sets and variables that Data Surveillance and RBQM uses to consume and produce the reports, statistical analysis, and visualizations for: 
- Data anomaly detection and trends . 
- Event incidence and under-over reporting events . 
- Potential duplication patients . 
- Rounding and propagating vital sign records . 
- AI Data Reconciliation reports . 
- RECIST 1.1 Dashboard . 
- Clean Patient Tracker missing page count reports. 
- Third-party, fully ingested studies .
