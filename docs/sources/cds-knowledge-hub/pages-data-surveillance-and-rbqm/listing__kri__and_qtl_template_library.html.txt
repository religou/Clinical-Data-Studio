# Listing, KRI, and QTL Template Library

## Listing, KRI, and QTL Template Library
 You can use templates in Data Surveillance and RBQM to save and store commonly used data listing , custom graph,  QTL , custom numeric KRI, or custom percentage KRI configurations for reuse within a study.
## What Are Templates?
 Templates are listing, custom graph, QTL,  custom numeric KRI , or custom percentage KRI  configurations that you can save and store in your organization's global template library. 
## What Is the Purpose of Templates?
 You can store and reuse common listing, custom graph , QTL, and KRI configurations that your organization is likely to include in multiple study configurations.
## Who Uses Templates?
 Members of your organization who build studies can save and store listing, custom graph , QTL, and custom KRI configurations as templates in your organization's template library.
 Members of your organization who review data; for example, study managers or data managers, can import and reuse listing and KRI configuration templates in studies.
## What Are the Benefits of Templates?
 Adding and using specific listings,  custom graphs, QTLs, and custom KRIs as templates:
- Enables you and your team to quickly and easily reapply common data listings, custom graph s,  QTLs, and custom KRIs as needed across study configurations. 
- Promotes faster study start time. 
- Ensures consistency and accuracy in listing, custom graph , QTL, and custom KRI details across studies. 
## How Do Templates Work?
 In Listings , Custom Graphs ,  QTL Configuration , and KRI Configuration , you can save listing, custom graph , QTL, or custom KRI configurations, respectively, as templates. When you save templates, you export and store the templates in a template group.
 Template groups generally comprise a collection of templates that you might logically bundle together and use when building studies. For example, you could have a template group that consists of one or more listings, or a combination of listings, QTLs, and KRIs. Templates in the template group may pertain to studies based on therapeutic area, phase, or therapy . 
 Template groups live at the study  level . You can access and reuse templates for any study across studies . Your organization has its own client division-level global template library in Data Surveillance and RBQM . You can create template groups in the template library. The template library also stores and lists the collection of your organization's templates groups and associated templates.
