# Download the Risk Inventory

## Download the Risk Inventory
 You can download a Risk Inventory Report PDF. The report contains pertinent information about the risks' Review Status, Origin, Action Required, and so on.
 Downloading the report PDF allows you to view on demand the data associated with each risk. The PDF presents the report in a clear, easy-to-read, and exchangeable format for:
- Offline stakeholder communication 
- Record retention 
- Review, approval, and signature. 
- Access RBQM , and then click Risks   in the left-hand menu.
The Risk Inventory page shows.
- Click Export .
The Risks Inventory report downloads to your machine. 
- Open the PDF. The report name is formatted as Risk Inventory <week day>, <day number> <month abbreviation> <year> <mm_dd_yy>, for example, Risk Inventory Mon, 21 Aug 2023 10_20_24.
