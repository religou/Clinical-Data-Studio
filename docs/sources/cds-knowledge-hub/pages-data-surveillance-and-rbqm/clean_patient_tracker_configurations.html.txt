# Clean Patient Tracker Configuration

## Clean Patient Tracker Configuration
 The Clean Patient Tracker Configuration page lets you configure missing page counts from Medidata Rave or externally coded studies, and Rave form statuses that appear in Clean Patient Tracker.
 To track missing page counts in the Clean Patient Tracker > Patients table > Missing Pages column, you can configure in Data Surveillance whether you want to:
- Manually enter missing page counts, or 
- Autopopulate missing page counts directly from:
- Rave, or 
- Externally ingested and mapped missing page count report datasets. 
 To track the Rave form statuses for each patient in the Clean Patient Tracker > Patients table, you can also configure whether you want to show forms that are:
- Incomplete, or 
- Require review, or 
- Require lock, or 
- Require freeze, or 
Require signature, or 
- Are non conformant. 
 To track specific AI Reconciliation reports in Clean Patient Tracker > Patients table, you can configure only the reports of interest from the following: 
- Conditions Without Treatments 
- Worsening Conditions 
- Overlapping Conditions 
- Overlapping non-Worsening Conditions and Ongoing Medical History 
- Invalid CTCAE Grade 
- Missing Lab for CTCAE Grade 
- Overlapping Treatments 
- Treatments Without Conditions or Ongoing Medical History 
 For more information, see  About AI Reconciliation Reports .
 Configuring Data Surveillance to autopopulate missing page counts and Rave form status in Clean Patient Tracker: 
- Enables your organization to seamlessly incorporate your missing page report data into Data Surveillance for tracking and analysis. 
- Ensures Clean Patient Tracker analyses the most recent available missing page counts to produce the most accurate data analysis. 
- Reduces the time and effort to track missing page counts. 
- Ensures data analysis quality by reducing data entry error.
