# Treatments Without Conditions and Ongoing MH Algorithm Methodology

## Treatments Without Conditions and Ongoing MH Algorithm Methodology
 Patients undergo treatments in response to a condition in a study. The AI Reconciliation algorithm identifies treatments that do not treat a condition, and for which the treatment start date lies outside the time period of the condition. A treatment is considered to treat a condition if the treatment matches the condition in the Knowledge Graph .

 The algorithm does not consider treatments if any of the following hold true: 
- The treatment is missing a coded term. 
- The treatment ended before the patient onboarded the study. 
- The treatment's indication suggests it is used for purposes where a condition is not expected, such as diagnostic procedures, prophylates, birth control, or recreational use.
