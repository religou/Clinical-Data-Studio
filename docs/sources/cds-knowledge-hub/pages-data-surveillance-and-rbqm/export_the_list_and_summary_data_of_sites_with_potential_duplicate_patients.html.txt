# Export the List and Summary Data of Sites with Potential Duplicate Patients

## Export the List and Summary Data of Sites with Potential Duplicate Patients
 You can export the list of site locations in your study with potential duplicate patients. The export includes summary data for each site listed. Exporting enables you to:
- Sort the data for site locations with potential duplicate patients. 
- Easily exchange the list and summary data with sites and other stakeholders to facilitate communication. 
- Locally save and archive the list and summary data. 
- Access RBQM , and then click Statistical Monitoring > Duplicate Patients in the left-hand menu.
- In the Potential Duplicate Patients page > Potential Duplicate Patients Summary section, click Download .
 The detect duplicate patients listing downloads locally to your machine as a .csv file. 
- Snapshot Date – The user's generated system time. 
- Group ID – The grouping of the duplicate patients. 
- Patient – The patient ID. 
- Confidence – Indicates Statistical Monitoring's level of confidence that the patient is a duplicate. Values are: Low, Medium, High. 
- Country – The country in which the duplicate patient record exists. 
- Site – The site where the duplicate patient record exists. 
- Address – The address of the site. 
- Birth Date – The patient's birth date as entered in the EDC. 
- Ethnicity – The patient's ethnicity. 
- Race – The patient's race. 
- Gender – The patient's gender. 
- Height – The patient's height. 
- Weight – The patient's weight.
