# View the Historical Risk Assessment List

## View the Historical Risk Assessment List
 You can view the historical list of risk assessment iterations including the currently active risk assessment draft and all archived drafts recorded throughout the duration of the study. 
- Access RBQM , and then click Risk Assessment > Version History in the left-hand menu.
The Version History page shows the following details for the draft risk assessment and all archived risk assessments in the study history:
 Column Descriptions 
- NAME – The risk assessment title. 
- VERSION – The risk assessment version. 
- STATUS – Indicates whether the risk assessment is the currently active draft risk assessment or an archived risk assessment. 
- DATE PERFORMED – The date in which the assessment was performed. 
- TEMPLATES APPLIED – A system-supplied value showing the number of risk categorization templates used in the risk assessment. Click to view the names of the applied templates. 
 Tip : If necessary, use the pagination at the bottom of the Version History table to find the historical risk assessment you need.
