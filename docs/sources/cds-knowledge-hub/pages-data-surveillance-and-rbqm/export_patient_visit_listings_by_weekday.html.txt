# Export Patient Visit Listings by Weekday

## Export Patient Visit Listings by Weekday
 You can export the list of scheduled patient visits that occurred on a specific day of the week. Exporting the patient visit listing for a specific weekday enables you to:
- Sort the patient visit listing data. 
- Easily exchange the patient visit listing data with sites and other stakeholders to facilitate communication. 
- Locally save and archive the patient visit listing data. 
- Access RBQM , and then click Statistical Monitoring > Weekend and Holiday in the left-hand menu.
The Weekend and Holiday Visits page shows. 
- In Countries , select to view the visit data by all countries or a specific country. 
- Click a weekday card.
The patient visit listing for the selected weekday appears. 
- If necessary, filter the patient visit listing. For tips on filtering, see  Investigate Site Visits On Non-Business Days .
 Note : The export only includes filtered patient visit data. F or example, if you filtered the visit listing for a specific site, then the export includes only the patient visit data for that site. 
- Click Export on the top-right side of the patient visit listing.
 The patient visit listing downloads locally to your machine as a .csv file.
 Note : The export only includes the first 500 records in the patient visit listing.
