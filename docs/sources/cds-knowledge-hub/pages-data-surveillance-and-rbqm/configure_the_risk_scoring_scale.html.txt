# Configure the Risk Scoring Scale

## Configure the Risk Scoring Scale
 You can configure Risk Assessment to g enerate a risk's overall risk score by scoring the Probability, Impact, and Detectability risk factors on a 3-point or 5-point scale. 
- Access RBQM , and then click Configuration > Risks in the left-hand menu.
The Risk Assessment Configuration pages shows. 
- In the Risk Study Scale section, select one of the following:
- 3 Point Scale 
- 5 Point Scale 
 Tip : Scoring risks and risk factors on a 5-point scale create a wider risk score disparity between low and high risks, which improves your ability to identify high risk items.
- Click Save Changes .
