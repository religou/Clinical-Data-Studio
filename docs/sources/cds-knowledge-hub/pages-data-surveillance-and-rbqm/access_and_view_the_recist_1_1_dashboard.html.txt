# Access and View the RECIST 1.1 Dashboard

## Access and View the RECIST 1.1 Dashboard
 On the RECIST 1.1 dashboard, you can view summary count cards, an interactive waterfall plot, and a patient records t able showing tumor response-related information for the patients in your study. The tumor response by patient table also includes tumor measurements that the built-in algorithm calculates, which you can use to identify and query discrepancies. This helps improve data accuracy and patient safety. 
- Access Data Surveillance , and then click Oncology in the left-hand menu.

The RECIST 1.1 dashboard shows the following:
- 
 The number of patients at each tumor response stage, based on the scan visit type and site. 
- Total Patients – Total number of patients in the study. 
- Patients in PD – Number of patients whose tumor response is progressive disease (PD). 
- Patients in SD – Number of patients whose tumor response is stable disease (SD). 
- Patients in PR – Number of patients whose tumor response is partial response (PR. 
- Patients in CR – Number of patients whose tumor response is complete response (CR). 
- Patients in OR – Number of patients for whom the overall response is missing for a selected visit. (OR). 
- Patients in NE/NA – Number of patients whose tumor response is not evaluable (NE) or not applicable (NA). 
- 
 A visual representation of the tumor burden change percentage for the study patients. 
- A waterfall chart that shows the percentage change relative to the baseline tumor burden for a patient at a specific time, for example, at Last Scan Visit or Cycle 3 Day 1. 
- A line chart that shows a patient's progress throughout the oncology study. 
- 
 A tumor burden response by patient table. 
 The tumor burden response by patient table includes study data that RECIST 1.1 ingests from Medidata Rave, and tumor burden responses calculated by the built-in algorithm . Each record represents a patient at a scan visit. The tumor burden response by patient table updates data as per the scan visit type and site selected.  
- Patient ID – The ID of the patient who participates in the oncology study. You can click the patient ID to access the patient profile design associated with the patient in Patient Profiles. 
- Site – The site that the patient belongs to. 
- Country – The country in which the site resides.

- Sum of Diameters (SLD ) – The sum of longest diameters of target lesion, nontarget lesions, and new lesions.  
- Tumor % Change – Site entered tumor burden percent change.

- NTL Response – Tumor response stages (CR, PR, PD, SD, NE, NA) for nontarget lesions. 
- NL Response – Yes indicates new lesions. No indicates no new lesions at scan visit. 
- Calculated Overall Response – Overall tumor response as calculated by the built-in algorithm.
- Calculated TL Response – Tumor response stages (CR, PR, PD, SD, NE, NA) for target lesions as calculated by the built-in algorithm. 
- Calculated NTL Response – Tumor response stages (CR, PR, PD, SD, NE, NA) for nontarget lesions as calculated by the built-in algorithm. 
- Calculated NL Response – Indicates if the algorithm calculated the occurrence of new lesions for the scan visit.
