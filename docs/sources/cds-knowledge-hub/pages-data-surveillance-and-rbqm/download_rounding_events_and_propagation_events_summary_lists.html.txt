# Download Rounding Events and Propagation Events Summary Lists

## Download Rounding Events and Propagation Events Summary Lists
 You can download to a CSV file the: 
- Rounding Events Summary list, 
- Rounding Events Summary > Patients with Record Events list, 
- Propagation Events Summary list, and 
- Propagation Events Summary > Patients with Record Events list 
 Downloading the   Rounding Events Summary lists and the Propagation Events Summary lists   enables you to:
- Easily exchange the lists externally with stakeholders to facilitate communication. 
- Locally save and archive the lists. 
## Download the Rounding Events Summary List
- Access RBQM , and then click Statistical Monitoring > Rounding & Propagation in the left-hand menu. 
- On the Rounding & Propagation page, click Rounding Events to view the analysis and summary data for sites with roundings events. 
- Scroll down the page to the Rounding Events Summary (Records rounded/Total records) table. 
- Click Download .
The Sites with Rounding Events list downloads to your machine as CSV file. 
## Download the Rounding Events Summary > Patients with Record Events List
- Access RBQM , and then click Statistical Monitoring > Rounding & Propagation in the left-hand menu. 
- On the Rounding & Propagation page, click Rounding Events to view the analysis and summary data for sites with roundings events. 
- Scroll down the page to the Rounding Events Summary (Records rounded/Total records) table. 
- In the # Affected Patients column, click the link associated with the site whose patients with record events you want view.
The Patients with Record Events dialog appears. 
- Click Download .
The Patients with Rounding Events list downloads to your machine as CSV file.
