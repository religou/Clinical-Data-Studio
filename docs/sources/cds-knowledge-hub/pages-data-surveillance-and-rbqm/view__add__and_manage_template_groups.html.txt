# View, Add, and Manage Template Groups

## View, Add, and Manage Template Groups
 In Templates, you can save listing, custom graph, QTL, or custom KRI configurations, respectively, as templates. When you save templates, you export and store the templates in a template group. Template groups generally comprise a collection of templates that you might logically bundle together and use when building studies. For example, you could have a template group that consists of one or more listings, or a combination of listings, QTLs, and KRIs. Templates in the template group may pertain to studies based on therapeutic area, phase, or therapy. Template groups live at the client division (CD)  level . You can access and reuse templates for any study across multiple client divisions.  Your organization has its own client division-level global template library in Data Surveillance. You can create template groups in the Data Surveillance Library. The template library also stores and lists the collection of your organization's templates groups and associated templates.
 In Templates, you can access your organization's template groups to import and reuse listing, custom graph, QTL, and KRI templates when creating listing, custom graph, QTL, or KRI configurations across studies.
## View the Template Groups and Individual Templates in Your Organization's Template Global Library
 You can view the list and details of the templates groups and their associated templates contained within your organization's template library.
- Access Data Surveillance or RBQM . 
- Click the  Platform Library tab. 
- Click Template Groups   in the left-hand menu.
The page containing your organization's global template library appears. 
- If necessary, to view the templates stored in a template group, click the template group name in the  Name column. 
- In the template group page, click the  Listings tab, Custom Graphs  tab, KRIs tab, or QTL tab to view the list of templates for listings templates, custom graphs, custom KRI or QTLs, respectively.
 Tips
- Click any column header to sort the list by column in alphanumeric order. An up or down arrow next to the column name shows the column that the list is currently sorted by. 
- Click and horizontally drag the border of any column header to resize columns. 
## Add Template Groups to Your Organization's Template Global Library
 When you save listing, custom graph, QTL, and custom KRI configurations as templates, you must export and store the templates in a template group. Template groups generally comprise a collection of templates that you might logically bundle together and use when building studies. For example, you could have a template group that consists of one or more listings, or a combination of listings, custom graphs, QTLs, and KRIs. The templates in the template group may pertain to studies based on therapeutic area, phase, or therapy. Template groups live at the client division (CD)  level . You can access and reuse templates for any study across multiple   client divisions.   
- Access Data Surveillance or RBQM . 
- Click the  Platform Library tab. 
- Click Template Groups   in the left-hand menu.
The page containing your organization's global template library appears. 
- Click Create Group . 
- Enter a Group Name .
- Enter the Description .
 Tip : Avoid naming groups or wording descriptions in such a way that can unblind or compromise other studies or study users.
- Click Save .
Your organization's global template library page now lists the new template group. You can now add and store templates  in this template group. 
## Manage Template Groups
 You can edit template group names and descriptions. For example, you can fix a typo or misspelling in a name or description or make an update that more clearly identifies or describes the group.
- Access Data Surveillance or RBQM . 
- Click the  Platform Library tab. 
- Click Template Groups   in the left-hand menu.
The page containing your organization's global template library appears. 
- For the template group you want to manage, click edit icon in that row. 
- If necessary, edit the  Name . 
- If necessary, edit the Description .
 Tip : Avoid naming groups or wording descriptions in such a way that might unblind or compromise other studies or study users.
- Click Save .
