# KRI Configuration

## KRI Configuration
 You can use RBQM Configuration's KRI Configuration capability to: 
- View the list of system KRIs and custom KRIs available in your study environment .
 Tip : To learn more about KRIs, see What Are Key Risk Indicators (KRIs) ? 
- Define and add custom KRIs that target specific facets of your study data that you want to monitor for risk. 
- Configure the level of weight in which a KRI impacts a site's level of analysis. 
- View patients that meet or do not meet a KRI configuration's query conditions . 
- Activate KRIs to get consumed, calculated, analyzed, and rendered in the KRI Dashboard , Risk Inventory , as well as the Add Risk modal and Risk Review modal in Risk Assessment and in Risk Inventory. 
- Configure the specific KRIs you want RBQM to factor in when calculating each site's performance risk level . 
- View patients that meet KRI configuration query conditions . 
 The KRI Dashboard allows you to: 
- Track the KRIs in your study and their respective KRI Values at each site. 
- Determine which KRI values are outliers and non-outliers at specific sites or countries. 
- Determine which KRIs have surpassed the risk threshold at specific sites or countries.
 Based on your observations made from the KRI Dashboard, you can determine: 
- The issues or risks that require further investigate or mitigation.  
- The best approach or mitigating actions to take on the issues or risks. For example, writing an issue against the custom KRI and capturing the custom KRI on a risk inventory.
- The effect the mitigating actions have made on the issues or risks over time. 
 KRI  data also gets filtered, calculated, and rendered in Risk Assessment and Risk Inventory. Data sets and variables you use to build KRI configurations appear with a Medidata Rave, external or derived label next to the name.  Similarly, data sets and variables with critical data points to the study appear with a blue flag 
 You can also use RBQM's KRI Configuration capability to: 
- Deactivate KRIs from getting consumed, analyzed, calculated, and shown in RBQM when that KRI no longer requires analysis. 
- Remove KRIs from RBQM's Site Risk Calculation . 
- Add and Store Custom KRI Configurations as Reusable Templates . 
- View Audit Details for Deactivated KRIs . 
- Download KRI Configuration Reports . 
- Generate KRI Configuration Audit Reports . 
 Important : KRIs do not support datetime operations.
